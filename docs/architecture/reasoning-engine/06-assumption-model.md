# 06 — Assumption Model

## Purpose

The Assumption Model makes every material non-evidentiary condition explicit so reasoning cannot hide missing premises or present assumptions as facts.

## Assumption Classes

| Class | Meaning | Required Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Scope Assumption | provisional interpretation of an unresolved boundary | constrain Result and request review when material |
| Applicability Assumption | proposition that a rule or evidence item applies | cite basis and test counterconditions |
| Completeness Assumption | declared belief about evidence coverage | never convert absent evidence into present evidence |
| Continuity Assumption | prior valid condition is presumed unchanged | attach time and change sensitivity |
| Identity Assumption | tentative identity or correlation | remain possible, never confirmed fact |
| Interpretation Assumption | one bounded reading of ambiguous language | preserve alternatives and ambiguity |
| Causal Assumption | proposed dependency between conditions | remain hypothesis-like unless supported |
| Analytical Assumption | simplifying condition required for a bounded inference | state excluded complexity and impact |

## Assumption Contract

Each Assumption records identity, class, proposition, necessity, origin, Scope, supporting and opposing evidence, affected Units and Results, materiality, sensitivity, challenge conditions, expiration, validation status, and owner for external clarification when applicable.

## Impact and Challenge

A material Assumption must be challenged by checking counterevidence and alternative interpretations. If changing it alters a Result materially, the Result carries an assumption dependency and cannot be represented as unconditional.

## Rules

- **ASSUMPTION-001:** Every material Assumption must be explicit, typed, scoped, and traceable.
- **ASSUMPTION-002:** Assumptions must remain distinguishable from evidence, canonical statements, hypotheses, and Results.
- **ASSUMPTION-003:** Reasoning must not invent an Assumption merely to force a conclusive Result.
- **ASSUMPTION-004:** Material Assumptions require sensitivity and counterevidence assessment.
- **ASSUMPTION-005:** Assumption limitations must propagate to every dependent Unit and Result.
- **ASSUMPTION-006:** Expired, contradicted, or invalidated Assumptions require dependent Results to be re-evaluated.
- **ASSUMPTION-007:** An unresolved authority or policy question must be marked Review Required, not converted into an Assumption.

## Enterprise Example

An impact analysis lacks evidence for whether one retired architecture version remains applicable. Reasoning may declare a bounded continuity Assumption solely to show conditional effects, but must expose how results change if the assumption is false.

## Boundaries

Assumptions do not authorize evidence collection, package correction, policy interpretation, decision-making, or action.
