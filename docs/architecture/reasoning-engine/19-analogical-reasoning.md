# 19 — Analogical Reasoning

## Purpose

Analogical Reasoning compares a target architecture situation with a bounded source case to identify transferable structural similarities and explicit differences.

## Contract

An analogical Unit records source and target identities, comparison Scope, mapped concepts and relationships, relevant similarities, material differences, transfer conditions, counteranalogies, uncertainty, and resulting limited finding.

Surface naming, popularity, organizational proximity, or shared technology does not establish structural analogy. Authority and conclusions do not transfer from the source case.

## Rules

- **ANALOGY-001:** Every analogy must declare source, target, mapping, and comparison purpose.
- **ANALOGY-002:** Material differences and unmapped elements must remain visible.
- **ANALOGY-003:** Source-case authority, decision, or outcome must not transfer to the target.
- **ANALOGY-004:** Analogy may generate or qualify a Hypothesis but cannot establish fact.
- **ANALOGY-005:** Competing and counteranalogies must be preserved when material.
- **ANALOGY-006:** Analogical findings must not become recommendations or execution instructions.

## Enterprise Example

A capability boundary resembles a previously analyzed boundary in relationship structure, but differs in authority and lifecycle. Reasoning may use the structural match to form a testable hypothesis while keeping the authority difference blocking.

## Boundaries

No similarity algorithm, case library, nearest-neighbor search, embedding, retrieval method, or recommendation behavior is selected.
