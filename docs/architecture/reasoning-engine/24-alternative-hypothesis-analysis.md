# 24 — Alternative Hypothesis Analysis

## Purpose

Alternative Hypothesis Analysis compares material explanations or findings under one evidence and Scope contract without choosing an action or suppressing viable alternatives.

## Comparison Dimensions

- evidence coverage and independence;
- counterevidence and contradiction;
- assumption burden and sensitivity;
- canonical compatibility;
- explanatory scope and exclusions;
- falsifiability and discriminating evidence;
- provenance and authority applicability;
- uncertainty and confidence rationale.

## Refinement

A Hypothesis may be narrowed, split, qualified, superseded, or rejected when package evidence supports the change. Refinement preserves prior versions, rationale, and lineage. It cannot add new evidence or rewrite the original proposition silently.

## Rules

- **ALTHYP-001:** Every material alternative must receive the same comparison contract.
- **ALTHYP-002:** Comparative support must remain distinct from recommendation or decision.
- **ALTHYP-003:** Refinement must preserve prior hypothesis identity, version, and rationale.
- **ALTHYP-004:** Discriminating evidence must already exist in the Processing Output Package.
- **ALTHYP-005:** Ties, incomparability, and indeterminacy must remain explicit.
- **ALTHYP-006:** A discarded alternative must retain its rejection evidence and Trace.

## Enterprise Example

Three explanations fit a conflict. One has broader evidence but also a critical assumption; another has narrower direct support. Analysis reports these differences without naming a preferred remediation.

## Boundaries

No experiment design, evidence acquisition, ranking engine, recommendation system, or decision rule is created.
