# 17 — Inductive Reasoning

## Purpose

Inductive Reasoning derives a bounded pattern or generalization from multiple eligible observations while preserving sampling, coverage, uncertainty, and counterexamples.

## Contract

An inductive Unit records observation set, independence and representativeness, Scope, inclusion and exclusion criteria, detected pattern, exceptions, alternative explanations, confidence rationale, and generalization limit.

## Integrity

Repeated artifacts sharing one lineage are not independent observations. Absence of contradictory evidence is not confirmation. A pattern in Runtime-derived evidence remains observational and cannot redefine canonical architecture.

## Rules

- **INDUCT-001:** Induction must declare population, sample, Scope, coverage, and selection limitations.
- **INDUCT-002:** Duplicated or correlated observations must not be counted as independent.
- **INDUCT-003:** Counterexamples and missing coverage must remain visible.
- **INDUCT-004:** A bounded pattern must not become a universal or canonical rule.
- **INDUCT-005:** Confidence must reflect representativeness, independence, conflict, and uncertainty.
- **INDUCT-006:** Inductive findings must not produce recommendations, decisions, or execution.

## Enterprise Example

Several independently sourced architecture reviews show the same boundary ambiguity. Induction may report a recurring pattern within reviewed domains, but cannot claim all domains are affected or recommend a policy change.

## Boundaries

No sampling algorithm, statistical model, anomaly detector, learning system, or telemetry ingestion is defined.
