# Phase 7A — Director Reasoning Philosophy — Final Closure

*Official historical closure document. Summary only — it redesigns nothing, adds no new philosophy, and modifies no existing document.*

## Executive Summary

Phase 7A established the **philosophical foundation of Director Reasoning** — the cognitive layer that turns organizational knowledge into organizational judgment. It defined *why* Director Reasoning exists and the principles every future reasoning component must obey: reasoning consumes the Organizational Model, the Relationship Graph, and Organizational Memory, and produces judgments, recommendations, decisions, and plans — never direct action.

This phase defined **philosophy only**. No algorithms, no implementation, no prompts, no agents, no runtime. It builds on the certified Phase 5–6 architecture baseline without modifying it, and defers every implementation concern behind the Director gate.

## Architectural Achievements

Phase 7A established (no new philosophy is introduced in this closure):

- **Reasoning philosophy** — the transform from organizational knowledge to organizational judgment as the reason the cognitive layer exists.
- **First principles** — truth over convenience, evidence before conclusion, context before action, organization before optimization, long-term over short-term, explicit uncertainty, explainable recommendations.
- **Reasoning boundaries** — reasoning is advisory only; it never executes, publishes, spends, deploys, or modifies memory.
- **Decision principles** — the modes of sound judgment reasoning must be capable of, described as principles, not algorithms.
- **Director authority** — final authority always rests with the Director; recommendations require explicit approval before any irreversible action.
- **Future evolution** — how reasoning will inform planning, orchestration, and autonomy while its authority stays at zero.
