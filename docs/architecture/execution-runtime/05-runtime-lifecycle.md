# 05 — Runtime Lifecycle

## Purpose

Define constitutional lifecycle meanings for one admitted Runtime responsibility without creating a state machine.

## Lifecycle Meanings

- **Presented:** a candidate admission package is available for constitutional validation.
- **Admissible:** required authority, Scope, inputs, and constraints are complete and compatible.
- **Accepted:** Runtime has explicitly assumed the bounded responsibility.
- **Realizing:** the accepted responsibility is operationally active.
- **Constrained:** realization remains bounded by a declared limitation or condition.
- **Suspended:** continuation is temporarily prohibited pending the authorized boundary.
- **Terminated:** responsibility ended before its expected outcome.
- **Completed:** Runtime has concluded its accepted responsibility and produced an outcome package.
- **Closed:** required evidence and accountability obligations are complete.

These are constitutional lifecycle meanings, not states, transitions, events, jobs, commands, or implementation logic.

## Lifecycle Authority

Runtime may declare operational condition within its accepted responsibility. It cannot create or renew authorization, redefine completion, approve outcomes, or choose recovery beyond established authority.

## Rules

- **P21-LIFECYCLE-001:** No lifecycle meaning may begin without its constitutional prerequisites.
- **P21-LIFECYCLE-002:** Acceptance must identify responsibility, authority, Scope, and contract version.
- **P21-LIFECYCLE-003:** Suspension and termination must preserve evidence and accountable reason.
- **P21-LIFECYCLE-004:** Completion must not imply business acceptance, Governance approval, or Director approval.
- **P21-LIFECYCLE-005:** Lifecycle history must remain attributable and auditable.
- **P21-LIFECYCLE-006:** This lifecycle must not be interpreted as a state machine or workflow.

## Enterprise Example

An admitted responsibility may become suspended when a Governance constraint expires. Runtime preserves evidence and waits for the proper authority; it does not renew permission.
