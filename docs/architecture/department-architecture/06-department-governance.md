# 06 — Department Governance

## Purpose

Department Governance defines **how a department is governed and kept traceable**. It is the departmental application of the enterprise-wide governance regime: one regime, reaching into every department, holding each accountable for its domain and making its ownership, authority, and accountability reconstructable.

## Architectural role

Governance sits over the department model ([02](02-department-model.md)), responsibilities ([03](03-department-responsibilities.md)), boundaries ([04](04-department-boundaries.md)), and coordination ([05](05-department-coordination.md)). Where those define what a department is, owns, refuses, and collaborates as, governance defines the standards a department must always satisfy and how its compliance is proven. It is the enterprise's governance ([9A](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)) applied at the department level.

## The model

### A department falls under enterprise governance — no exceptions
There is one governance regime for the enterprise, and every department is inside it ([enterprise governance](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)). A department does not invent its own governance and is never exempt. Governance reaches uniformly into each department.

### Governance aligns with Director Authority
Departmental governance expresses the Director's standards within the department; it never sits above the Director ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). Where a department's action would exceed governance or its delegated authority, governance blocks it and escalates — ultimately to the Director.

### A department's ownership and authority are traceable
Which domain a department owns, the bounded authority delegated to it, the line by which it is accountable upward, and its collaborations with other departments can all be reconstructed and audited. Traceability is structural — a property of the department, not of any run of work ([department responsibilities](03-department-responsibilities.md)).

### Governance preserves the department's boundaries
Governance enforces that a department stays within its domain and delegated authority — that it does not reason, execute, plan, decide, or cross into another domain ([department boundaries](04-department-boundaries.md)). Governance is how the boundaries are held in practice, not merely declared.

### Governance preserves, it does not perform
Departmental governance checks, records, and escalates. It reasons about no work and executes no task. It is the department's accountability layer — not an actor in reasoning or execution.

## What department governance preserves

- **Director Authority** — enforced within the department, never overridden.
- **Bounded, revocable delegation** — the department never exceeds its grant.
- **Exclusive domain ownership** — one department, one domain, one accountable owner.
- **Traceability** — the department's structure and collaborations are auditable.

## Inputs

- The **department model, responsibilities, boundaries, and coordination** ([02](02-department-model.md)–[05](05-department-coordination.md)) — what governance governs.
- The **enterprise governance regime** ([9A](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)) — the standards applied at the department level.

## Outputs

- A **department governance and traceability frame** — uniform standards and a reconstructable record — that holds each department accountable within the enterprise.

## Boundaries

- Governs **departments as seats and their structure, not concrete departments** — it names none.
- Performs **no reasoning and no work** — it checks, records, and escalates.
- Describes **no runtime or mechanism** — only the governance concern at the department level.

## Future direction

As concrete departments and their agents are defined, each falls under this governance the moment it exists — same regime, same traceability, no per-department exemptions. Departmental governance may deepen its checks over time ([future evolution](07-future-evolution.md)), but it stays enterprise-anchored, Director-topped, and traceable. Departments multiply; accountability stays whole.
