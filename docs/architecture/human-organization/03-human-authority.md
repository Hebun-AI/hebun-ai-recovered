# 03 — Human Authority

## Purpose

Human Authority defines **the authority a human holds by occupying a seat** — and establishes that it is exactly the seat's authority, no more and no less. A human occupant is authorized the same way any occupant is: by delegation, bounded, revocable, under the Director. This document says so precisely, so human participation never becomes a back door around the authority model.

## Architectural role

Where the role model ([02](02-human-role-model.md)) defines that a human occupies a seat, human authority defines *what that human may do* — the seat's delegated authority, inherited whole. Accountability ([05](05-accountability-model.md)) pairs with it; governance ([06](06-human-governance.md)) enforces it. Human authority is the seat's authority ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)) viewed through a human occupant.

## The model

### A human occupant's authority is the seat's authority
A human holds exactly the authority delegated to the seat it occupies — a human manager holds the manager seat's grant ([manager authority](../manager-architecture/03-manager-authority.md)); a human specialist holds the specialist seat's grant ([specialist authority boundaries](../specialist-architecture/04-specialist-authority-boundaries.md)). Being human adds no authority and removes none.

### It is delegated, never originated
A human occupant's authority descends through the hierarchy from the Director ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). A human creates no authority by being human. Authority is always traceable up to the Director, whoever occupies the seat.

### It is bounded by the seat
A human occupant may act only within the seat's authority — bounded by the seat's grant and by the seat's place in the hierarchy (a department, a capability). A human occupant never exceeds the seat's bound on the strength of being human ([human participation principles](01-human-participation-principles.md)).

### It is revocable
The delegation to a seat is revocable regardless of occupant ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). A human occupant holds no authority the Director cannot reclaim. Human occupancy does not make a grant permanent.

### Committing actions stay behind Director approval
A human occupant's authority lets it be *accountable* for outcomes that include committing or irreversible actions, but the commitment itself rests on the Director's approval ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). A human occupant never manufactures a committing authority the Director did not grant. This is identical to how every seat treats committing actions.

### Human authority never sits above the Director
No human occupant — at any seat — holds authority at or above the Director. The Director is the apex for humans exactly as for any occupant ([human participation principles](01-human-participation-principles.md)). Humans participate under the Director, never over the structure.

## Preserving Director Authority

- A human occupant sits **beneath** the Director, in a seat, like any occupant.
- Its authority is **the seat's** — delegated, bounded, revocable, traceable up.
- **Committing actions** rest on Director approval — human occupancy grants no exception.

This is how Human Organization "preserves Director Authority": by giving humans the seat's authority and nothing more.

## Inputs

- The **human role model** ([02](02-human-role-model.md)) — the seat a human occupies.
- The **enterprise and seat authority models** ([9A](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md), [9C](../manager-architecture/03-manager-authority.md), [9D](../specialist-architecture/04-specialist-authority-boundaries.md)) — the authority inherited.

## Outputs

- A **defined authority for human occupants** — the seat's authority, delegated, bounded, revocable, Director-topped — that accountability and governance rely on.

## Boundaries

- Grants authority to **the human as seat occupant, not to a concrete role** — it names none.
- Defines **no permission model, authentication, or access control** — those are runtime, out of scope.
- Manufactures **no authority** — human occupancy stays within the seat's grant.

## Future direction

As humans occupy seats throughout hybrid organizations, each holds exactly its seat's delegated, bounded, revocable authority under the Director. The authority model does not change to admit human occupants — it already defines how any occupant is authorized. Occupants vary; the authority envelope is the seat's, always.
