# 04 — Human–AI Collaboration

## Purpose

Human–AI Collaboration defines **how human and AI occupants collaborate as peers** within the one organization. Its point is that collaboration between a human and an AI is not a special case: it is ordinary cross-organization collaboration, following the same rules, because both are occupants of seats in the same structure.

## Architectural role

This document applies the cross-organization collaboration rules ([9E](../cross-organization-collaboration/README.md)) to the human–AI case. Where the role model ([02](02-human-role-model.md)) established that seats are occupant-agnostic, this document establishes that collaboration is occupant-agnostic too: the seat's collaboration rules govern, whoever fills the seats.

## The model

### Collaboration is between seats, not natures
Two occupants collaborate as the *seats* collaborate — under the interaction shapes, ownership rules, coordination paths, and escalation of Phase 9E ([collaboration principles](../cross-organization-collaboration/01-collaboration-principles.md)). Whether the occupants are human, AI, or one of each changes nothing about which rules apply.

### Human–AI collaboration is ordinary
A human specialist collaborating with an AI specialist, a human manager coordinating with an AI manager, a human specialist reporting to an AI manager or the reverse — all are ordinary uses of the existing collaboration structure ([organizational interaction model](../cross-organization-collaboration/02-organizational-interaction-model.md)). None is special-cased.

### Ownership and accountability are preserved regardless of occupant
Each occupant keeps its seat's ownership and accountability through collaboration ([collaboration principles](../cross-organization-collaboration/01-collaboration-principles.md)): a human specialist keeps its capability ownership, an AI manager keeps its department accountability. Ownership never dissolves because occupants of different natures collaborate.

### Collaboration flows through authority, not around it
Human–AI collaboration flows through the hierarchy and the seats' authority exactly as any collaboration does ([organizational interaction model](../cross-organization-collaboration/02-organizational-interaction-model.md)). A human occupant opens no side-channel around an AI manager's authority, and vice versa. What exceeds the occupants' authority escalates ([escalation model](../cross-organization-collaboration/05-escalation-model.md)).

### Occupant nature is not an authority
Being human confers no authority over an AI occupant, and being AI confers none over a human. Authority is the seat's ([human authority](03-human-authority.md)); the relationship between two occupants is the relationship between their seats. Nature is never a lever.

### The Director governs human–AI collaboration
All human–AI collaboration sits under the Director, and committing or beyond-authority matters escalate to the Director ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). No human–AI arrangement resolves a Director-level matter on its own.

## Inputs

- The **cross-organization collaboration architecture** ([9E](../cross-organization-collaboration/README.md)) — the rules human–AI collaboration follows.
- The **human role model and authority** ([02](02-human-role-model.md), [03](03-human-authority.md)) — the seats and grants the human side brings.

## Outputs

- A **defined human–AI collaboration model** — ordinary collaboration between seats, ownership and authority preserved, nature never a lever — that keeps a hybrid enterprise coherent.

## Boundaries

- Collaborates between **seats occupied by humans or AI, not concrete roles** — it names none.
- Performs **no reasoning, work, or orchestration** — it arranges structural collaboration, nothing more.
- Defines **no runtime communication, UI, or protocol** — how occupants exchange information is out of scope.
- Bypasses **no authority and no ownership**.

## Future direction

As hybrid organizations grow, human–AI collaboration multiplies within these same rules: between seats, ownership preserved, through authority, nature never a lever, Director-topped. More mixed collaboration, same structure. The enterprise becomes hybrid; collaboration stays one model.
