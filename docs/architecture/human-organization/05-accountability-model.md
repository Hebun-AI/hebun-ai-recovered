# 05 — Accountability Model

## Purpose

The Accountability Model defines **how a human occupant stays accountable** — and establishes that a human answers for its seat exactly as any occupant does. Accountability is the property that keeps the one organization whole; this document ensures human participation strengthens it rather than fracturing it.

## Architectural role

Where human authority ([03](03-human-authority.md)) defines what a human occupant may do, this document defines what it *answers for*. It pairs authority with accountability at the seat and preserves the accountability that departments ([9B](../department-architecture/03-department-responsibilities.md)), managers ([9C](../manager-architecture/01-manager-principles.md)), and specialists ([9D](../specialist-architecture/03-specialist-responsibilities.md)) already hold — regardless of occupant.

## The model

### A human occupant answers for its seat
A human is accountable for exactly what its seat is accountable for — a human manager answers for its department, a human specialist for its capability ([human role model](02-human-role-model.md)). Accountability is the seat's; the human inherits it whole by occupying the seat.

### Accountability runs upward, to the Director
A human occupant answers to the level above it and ultimately to the Director ([enterprise organizational model](../enterprise-organization/02-organizational-model.md)), exactly as any occupant does. Being human neither shortens nor redirects the accountability line. Humans are accountable *within* the hierarchy, not outside it.

### Accountability is paired with authority
A human occupant is accountable to the extent it is authorized and authorized to the extent it is accountable ([human authority](03-human-authority.md)). The pairing that holds for every seat holds for human occupants: no accountability without matching authority, no authority without matching accountability.

### Ownership carries accountability, regardless of occupant
When a human occupant owns a domain or capability, it answers for that ownership's outcomes ([department responsibilities](../department-architecture/03-department-responsibilities.md), [specialist responsibilities](../specialist-architecture/03-specialist-responsibilities.md)). If ownership transfers to or from a human occupant, accountability moves with it under the transfer rules ([ownership transfer](../cross-organization-collaboration/03-ownership-transfer.md)). Human occupancy never separates ownership from accountability.

### One accountable occupant per seat
Because a seat has a single occupant of record ([human role model](02-human-role-model.md)), accountability stays unambiguous — there is always exactly one occupant answering for the seat, human or AI. Mixed departments do not blur this: each seat has its own single accountable occupant.

### Accountability is traceable
A human occupant's accountability — what it answers for, to whom, and how ownership and authority pair at its seat — can be reconstructed and audited ([human governance](06-human-governance.md)). Traceability of accountability does not weaken because the occupant is human.

## Inputs

- The **human authority model** ([03](03-human-authority.md)) — the authority accountability pairs with.
- The **seat accountability structures** ([9B](../department-architecture/03-department-responsibilities.md), [9C](../manager-architecture/01-manager-principles.md), [9D](../specialist-architecture/03-specialist-responsibilities.md)) — what a human occupant answers for.

## Outputs

- A **defined accountability for human occupants** — the seat's accountability, upward to the Director, paired with authority, carrying ownership — that governance relies on.

## Boundaries

- Assigns accountability to **the human as seat occupant, not a concrete role** — it names none.
- Defines **no runtime, review process, or enforcement mechanism** — those are out of scope.
- Performs **no reasoning or work** — it is a model of accountability, not an actor.

## Future direction

As humans occupy seats across hybrid organizations, each answers for its seat, upward, to the Director — accountability paired with authority, carrying ownership, single per seat, traceable. The accountability model does not change to admit human occupants — it already defines how any occupant answers. Occupants vary; accountability stays whole and unambiguous.
