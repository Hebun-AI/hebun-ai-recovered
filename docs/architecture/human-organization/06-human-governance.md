# 06 — Human Governance

## Purpose

Human Governance defines **how human occupants fall under one governance regime** — and establishes that they are governed exactly as any occupant, with no exemption and no separate human regime. Its purpose is to close the door on a two-regime organization: humans are governed by the same enterprise-wide standards, and their participation is as traceable as any other.

## Architectural role

Governance sits over the whole human-organization architecture — the role model ([02](02-human-role-model.md)), authority ([03](03-human-authority.md)), collaboration ([04](04-human-ai-collaboration.md)), and accountability ([05](05-accountability-model.md)). It is the enterprise governance ([9A](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)) applied to human occupants, consistent with the governance every level already carries ([department](../department-architecture/06-department-governance.md), [manager](../manager-architecture/06-escalation-governance.md), [specialist](../specialist-architecture/06-reporting-and-governance.md)).

## The model

### Human occupants fall under enterprise governance — no exceptions
There is one governance regime, and every human occupant is inside it ([enterprise governance](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)). Human participation invents no governance of its own and grants no exemption. Governance reaches human occupants exactly as it reaches AI occupants.

### Human Organization redefines no governance
This layer adds no new governance regime and alters none. It confirms that humans inherit the existing regime unchanged ([human participation principles](01-human-participation-principles.md)). "Human governance" is not a separate governance — it is the one regime, seen through human occupants.

### Governance aligns with Director Authority
A human occupant's governance expresses the Director's standards; it never sits above the Director ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). Where a human occupant's action would exceed governance or its seat's authority, governance blocks it and forces escalation ([escalation model](../cross-organization-collaboration/05-escalation-model.md)). A human occupant gets no leniency from being human.

### Human participation is fully traceable
A human occupant's seat, authority, ownership, accountability, collaborations, and escalations can all be reconstructed and audited ([accountability model](05-accountability-model.md)). Traceability is structural and occupant-agnostic — it does not weaken for human occupants.

### One regime keeps the organization one
Because humans and AI occupants fall under the same governance, the organization stays a single structure with a single accountability line to the Director ([human participation principles](01-human-participation-principles.md)). A separate human governance would fork the organization — which is exactly what this layer forbids.

### Governance preserves, it does not perform
Human governance checks, records, and escalates. It reasons about no work and executes no task. It is the accountability layer over human participation — not an actor in reasoning or execution.

## What human governance preserves

- **Director Authority** — enforced over human occupants; committing and beyond-grant matters escalate to it.
- **One organization** — humans and AI under one regime, no parallel org.
- **Accountability and ownership** — preserved for human occupants as for any occupant.
- **Enterprise governance** — one regime, reaching humans, no exemptions.
- **Traceability** — human participation is auditable.

## Inputs

- The **human role, authority, collaboration, and accountability models** ([02](02-human-role-model.md)–[05](05-accountability-model.md)) — what governance governs.
- The **enterprise governance regime** ([9A](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)) — the standards applied to human occupants.

## Outputs

- A **governance and traceability frame for human occupants** — the one regime applied to humans, no exemptions — that keeps the organization single and accountable.

## Boundaries

- Governs **human occupants as seat-holders, not concrete roles** — it names none.
- Performs **no reasoning, work, authentication, or enforcement mechanism** — it checks, records, and escalates; runtime is out of scope.
- Redefines **no governance** — it applies the existing regime.

## Future direction

As hybrid organizations grow, every human occupant falls under this one regime the moment it takes a seat — same standards, same traceability, no exemptions, no separate human governance. The regime may deepen its checks over time ([future evolution](07-future-evolution.md)), but it stays enterprise-wide, Director-topped, and traceable. The organization grows hybrid; governance stays one.
