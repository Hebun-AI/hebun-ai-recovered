# 07 — Future Evolution

## Purpose

Future Evolution describes **how Human Organization deepens over time** without violating anything Phase 9F established. It marks the seams where later phases attach — concrete human roles, hybrid organizations of every mix — and states what must never change as they do.

## Architectural role

This document is the bridge from the human-organization architecture to everything built on it. It does not design those things; it defines the invariants they must respect and the structure humans will participate within.

## Where human organization deepens

### Concrete human roles
This layer defines *how humans participate*; it names no role or job title ([human role model](02-human-role-model.md)). Later phases define concrete human roles — each occupying a defined seat, carrying the seat's full obligations, under the seat's authority and governance. The structure does not change to admit them.

### Hybrid organizations of any mix
The organization supports any mix of human and AI occupants ([human–AI collaboration](04-human-ai-collaboration.md)). Later phases populate real hybrid organizations — human managers over AI specialists, AI managers over human specialists, mixed departments throughout. Each mix is ordinary; none is special-cased.

### Occupancy assignment and change (runtime, out of scope here)
How a human is assigned to a seat, how occupancy changes, and how that is authenticated and permissioned are **runtime** concerns, behind the Director gate ([README](README.md)). This layer fixes only the architecture: the seat is occupant-agnostic, a human carries its full obligations, one occupant of record per seat. The mechanisms come later without changing these facts.

### Richer collaboration, accountability, and governance
Human–AI collaboration, accountability, and governance may gain more structure as hybrid organizations grow ([human–AI collaboration](04-human-ai-collaboration.md), [accountability model](05-accountability-model.md), [human governance](06-human-governance.md)). They deepen within the same rules; they never acquire a separate human regime, new authority, or an exemption.

## Invariants — what never changes

No matter how human participation evolves:

- **Humans are participants, not exceptions.** One organization, never two ([human participation principles](01-human-participation-principles.md)).
- **The seat defines the rules, not the occupant.** A human carries the seat's full obligations ([human role model](02-human-role-model.md)).
- **Humans and AI are equal as occupants.** Neither is default, neither is exception, nature is never an authority ([human–AI collaboration](04-human-ai-collaboration.md)).
- **A human occupant's authority is the seat's — delegated, bounded, revocable.** Never above the Director ([human authority](03-human-authority.md)).
- **Accountability and ownership are preserved for human occupants.** Upward, to the Director, single per seat ([accountability model](05-accountability-model.md)).
- **Humans fall under the one governance regime.** No separate human governance, no exemption ([human governance](06-human-governance.md)).
- **Human participation is traceable.** As auditable as any occupant's.
- **Committing actions stay behind the Director's approval.** Human occupancy grants no exception.

## Inputs

- The **whole Phase 9F architecture** ([01](01-human-participation-principles.md)–[06](06-human-governance.md)) — the human-participation structure future work extends.

## Outputs

- A **map of the seams** — where later phases attach — and the **invariants** they must preserve.

## Boundaries

- Defines **no concrete human role, job title, assignment mechanism, authentication, permission, UI, runtime, or workflow** — it points to where they attach, and never builds them.
- Introduces **no new authority, reasoning, execution, or separate governance** into the human-organization layer.

## Future direction

Phase 9F is the architecture of human participation. What follows fills it — concrete human roles, real hybrid organizations, the runtime that assigns and permissions occupancy — each behind the Director gate, each under the invariants above. Human participation becomes real by occupancy, not by redesign, and never by forking the organization. The structure holds; humans and AI grow inside it as one enterprise.
