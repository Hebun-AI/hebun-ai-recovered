# 01 — Organization Principles

## Purpose

The Organization Principles are the constitution of Enterprise Organization — the commitments the organizational structure must obey. The organization is the highest business structure of Hebun AI; these principles are what keep that structure coherent, accountable, and always subordinate to the Director. Any organization that violates one of these is not doing Enterprise Organization.

## Architectural role

These principles constrain all the organizational topics that follow (model, boundaries, authority, coordination, governance). Every subsequent document inherits them first. They keep the organization structural, bounded, authority-preserving, governed, and traceable.

## The principles

### 1. The organization structures; it does not think or act
The organization defines hierarchy, authority, responsibility, coordination, and governance. It performs no reasoning ([Director Intelligence](../director-reasoning/README.md)) and no work ([Execution](../director-execution/README.md)). It is the frame those layers operate inside — never a participant in them.

### 2. Director Authority is the top of the hierarchy
Every level of the organization sits beneath the Director. Authority delegated to any unit is delegated *from* the Director and is always revocable by the Director. No organizational unit ever holds authority the Director cannot override ([authority model](04-authority-model.md)).

### 3. Authority is delegated, never originated
Organizational units do not create authority; they receive it. Delegation flows downward from the Director through the hierarchy, and every unit's authority is bounded by what was delegated to it. A unit may never act beyond its delegated authority ([authority model](04-authority-model.md)).

### 4. Responsibility is explicit and owned
Every outcome in the enterprise belongs to a defined organizational level. There are no orphan responsibilities and no shared-until-blamed ownership. Who is accountable for what is a structural fact, not a runtime negotiation ([organizational model](02-organizational-model.md)).

### 5. The structure is open to departments, agents, and humans
The hierarchy provides defined *places* — for multiple departments, for future manager agents, for future specialist agents, and for human participants — without naming or defining any of them. The structure admits new participants without being redesigned ([organizational model](02-organizational-model.md), [future evolution](07-future-evolution.md)).

### 6. The organization is governed as a whole
Enterprise governance applies uniformly across every level and unit. No part of the organization is outside governance, and governance is not re-invented per unit ([enterprise governance](06-enterprise-governance.md)).

### 7. Organizational authority and responsibility are traceable
Where authority sits, how it was delegated, and who is responsible for an outcome can always be reconstructed and audited. Traceability is structural — it does not depend on any execution being observed ([enterprise governance](06-enterprise-governance.md)).

### 8. The organization never redesigns plans or decisions
When a plan or decision is needed, the organization routes it to Director Intelligence; it does not produce or alter one. The organization frames *where* planning and deciding happen and *who owns the result* — it never does the planning or deciding itself ([organizational boundaries](03-organizational-boundaries.md)).

## Inputs

- The **completed Director Intelligence architecture** (Phase 7) — the thinking the structure serves.
- The **completed Execution architecture** (Phase 8) — the acting the structure serves.
- **Director Authority** — the source of all delegated organizational authority.

## Outputs

- A **principled frame** every organizational topic operates within — the standard the structure is held to.

## Boundaries

- These principles **define no concrete department, agent, or workflow** — they state what the structure must obey, not what it contains.
- They **describe no runtime or mechanism** — organizational machinery is a later phase behind the Director gate.

## Future direction

Future enterprises built on this structure may add many departments and agents — but they will still obey these principles: structural not operational, Director-topped, delegated authority, explicit responsibility, open to participants, wholly governed, traceable, never re-planning. The enterprise grows; the constitution holds.
