# 06 — Enterprise Governance

## Purpose

Enterprise Governance defines **how the whole organization is governed and kept traceable**. It is the layer that makes the enterprise accountable as a single entity: one set of governance commitments applying uniformly across every level, and a traceability that lets any authority or responsibility be reconstructed and audited.

## Architectural role

Governance sits over the entire organizational model ([02](02-organizational-model.md)), authority model ([04](04-authority-model.md)), and coordination structure ([05](05-organizational-coordination.md)). Where those define how the enterprise is arranged and authorized, governance defines the standards that arrangement must always satisfy and how compliance is proven.

## The model

### Governance is enterprise-wide and uniform
There is one governance regime, and it applies to every level — enterprise, department, manager, specialist. No unit is outside governance, and no unit invents its own. Governance is a property of the organization as a whole ([organization principles](01-organization-principles.md)).

### Governance aligns with Director Authority
Governance never sits above the Director; it expresses the Director's standards across the enterprise. It constrains delegated units, not the Director. Where a unit's action would exceed governance, governance blocks it and escalates — ultimately to the Director ([authority model](04-authority-model.md)).

### Every authority and responsibility is traceable
Where authority sits, how it was delegated, who owns an outcome, and how coordination was arranged can all be reconstructed. Traceability is structural — it holds whether or not any execution is observed, because it is a property of the organization, not of a run of work ([organization principles](01-organization-principles.md)).

### Governance is consistent with execution and reasoning governance
The enterprise's governance frames the governance already present downstream — the governance alignment in Director Intelligence ([decision governance](../director-decision/05-governance-alignment.md)) and the tool and execution governance in Phase 8 ([tool governance](../tool-execution/06-tool-governance.md)). Enterprise governance is the outermost ring; those are governance applied within specific domains. They agree by construction: all express Director-anchored standards.

### Governance preserves, it does not perform
Governance checks, records, and escalates. It reasons about no work and executes no task. It is the enterprise's conscience and audit trail — not an actor in reasoning or execution.

## What governance preserves

- **Director Authority** — governance never overrides it; it enforces it across the enterprise.
- **Bounded, revocable delegation** — governance ensures no unit exceeds its grant.
- **Explicit ownership** — governance ensures every outcome has a traceable owner.
- **Traceability** — governance ensures the whole structure can be audited.

## Inputs

- The **organizational model**, **authority model**, and **coordination structure** ([02](02-organizational-model.md), [04](04-authority-model.md), [05](05-organizational-coordination.md)) — what governance governs.
- **Director Authority** — the source of the standards governance enforces.

## Outputs

- A **governance and traceability frame** — uniform standards and a reconstructable record — that holds the enterprise accountable as one entity.

## Boundaries

- Governs **seats and structure, not concrete participants** — it names no department, agent, or human.
- Performs **no reasoning and no work** — it checks, records, and escalates.
- Describes **no runtime or mechanism** — only the governance concern.

## Future direction

As the enterprise adds departments, agents, and human roles, each falls under the same governance the moment it takes a seat — no exceptions, no per-unit regimes. Governance may deepen its checks over time ([future evolution](07-future-evolution.md)), but it stays enterprise-wide, Director-anchored, and traceable. The enterprise grows; accountability stays whole.
