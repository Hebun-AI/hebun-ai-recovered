# 08 — Agent Lifecycle

## Purpose

The Agent Lifecycle defines governance states for the constitutional admissibility of an Agent. It is not a Runtime state machine, provisioning sequence, workflow, deployment process, or identity schema.

## Lifecycle States

- **Proposed:** a bounded participation need and archetype are stated.
- **Created:** a constitutional Agent identity proposal exists outside canonical organizational ownership.
- **Registered:** governance records its identity, Scope, archetype, accountability, and limitations.
- **Eligible:** constitutional and dependency review supports bounded participation.
- **Participating:** a specific participation envelope admits contribution.
- **Suspended:** participation is unavailable pending evidence, governance, safety, Scope, or authority review.
- **Retired:** future participation is no longer admissible under that identity.
- **Replaced:** another governed Agent may fulfill the role without inheriting authority or altering canonical meaning.

Registration is not participation, eligibility is not authorization, and participation is not execution.

## Governance Review

Review must consider constitutional conformance, Scope currency, provenance behavior, limitation disclosure, escalation behavior, authority separation, lifecycle evidence, and replacement impact.

## Rules

- **P17-LIFECYCLE-001:** Every Agent must have a governed lifecycle identity distinct from its role archetype.
- **P17-LIFECYCLE-002:** Creation must not imply registration or eligibility.
- **P17-LIFECYCLE-003:** Suspension must stop new participation without rewriting historical evidence.
- **P17-LIFECYCLE-004:** Retirement must not erase provenance or accountability.
- **P17-LIFECYCLE-005:** Replacement must not inherit authority, approval, or ownership by continuity.
- **P17-LIFECYCLE-006:** Lifecycle transitions require governed evidence and applicable human review.

## Enterprise Example

Replacing an Architecture Validator changes the participating Agent reference. It does not change validation rules, canonical documents, past findings, or Director authority.
