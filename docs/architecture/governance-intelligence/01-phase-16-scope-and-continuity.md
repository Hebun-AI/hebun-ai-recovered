# 01 — Phase 16 Scope and Continuity

## Purpose

Phase 16A establishes the minimum canonical architecture for read-only governance eligibility evaluation of one Phase 14 Reasoning Output Package.

## Canonical Dependencies

Phase 16A preserves:

- Phase 12E Governance Scope, authority, policy, validation, boundary, and Director-reservation concepts;
- Phase 13 evidence, provenance, classification, privacy, Tenant, and Processing Output lineage;
- Phase 14 immutable Reasoning Output, Trace, assumptions, conflicts, confidence, and limitations;
- Phase 15 Query, Intent, Objective, Scope, Context, constraints, and Request Package provenance.

Governance cannot redo or correct these completed responsibilities.

## Foundation Scope

Phase 16A defines:

- Governance Intelligence logical components;
- Reasoning Output admission;
- policy references;
- authority applicability;
- compliance evaluation;
- governance eligibility evaluation;
- strict boundaries;
- Governance Outcome Package;
- foundation review readiness.

## Authority

Governance Intelligence observes and validates represented authority but never creates, transfers, exercises, infers, or amplifies authority. A Governance Outcome cannot grant permission, approval, exception, or business authority.

## Non-Goals

This foundation defines no business decision, recommendation, response answer, reasoning, retrieval, evidence generation, policy creation, executable policy evaluation, enforcement, authorization, execution, agent, tool, workflow, prompt, API, database, Runtime, infrastructure, or AWS architecture.

## Rules

- **P16A-001:** Every Governance Case must bind one immutable Reasoning Output Package version.
- **P16A-002:** Governance Intelligence evaluates declared-use eligibility, never reasoning correctness.
- **P16A-003:** Policy, authority, compliance, privacy, and governance references must be canonical, pre-qualified, and traceable.
- **P16A-004:** Governance must not modify evidence, reasoning Results, Trace, Query artifacts, or canonical sources.
- **P16A-005:** Governance Outcome must not become approval, authorization, recommendation, decision, or execution.
- **P16A-006:** Missing governance basis, conflict, and uncertainty must remain explicit.
- **P16A-007:** Phase 16A must not define Phase 17 or later architecture.

## Enterprise Example

A Reasoning Output Package contains a supported impact finding and requests no action. Governance evaluates whether presenting that finding for a declared organizational use is eligible under applicable confidentiality, authority, and policy constraints. It does not reassess the inference or decide what the organization should do.
