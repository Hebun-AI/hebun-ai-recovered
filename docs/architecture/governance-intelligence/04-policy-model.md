# 04 — Policy Model

## Purpose

The Policy Model defines how approved policy references constrain governance eligibility without creating, interpreting beyond explicit text, executing, or enforcing policy.

## Policy Reference Contract

Every Policy Reference records:

- stable identity, canonical source, version, lifecycle, and authority;
- effective and expiry interval;
- Tenant, organization, domain, jurisdiction, audience, and use Scope;
- obligations, prohibitions, conditions, and explicit exceptions;
- precedence and conflict references;
- provenance, citation anchors, classification, and validation status.

## Applicability

Policy applicability is established before evaluation by matching declared use and Governance Scope to the policy's explicit boundaries. Recency, popularity, ownership, Query wording, or confidence cannot create applicability.

## Policy Conflicts

Conflicting policies remain independently visible. An explicit canonical precedence rule may classify applicability within its Scope; otherwise the Outcome is conflicted, insufficient, withheld, or Review Required. Governance Intelligence cannot resolve policy normatively.

## Rules

- **POLICY-001:** Every applied policy must be approved, current for Scope, versioned, and traceable.
- **POLICY-002:** Policy applicability must precede eligibility evaluation.
- **POLICY-003:** Policy wording, obligations, prohibitions, conditions, and exceptions must remain intact.
- **POLICY-004:** Missing policy must not be fabricated or replaced by organizational custom.
- **POLICY-005:** Policy conflict must remain visible and cannot be silently merged.
- **POLICY-006:** Governance must not create, amend, waive, repeal, interpret normatively, execute, or enforce policy.
- **POLICY-007:** Policy alignment is not approval, authorization, or correctness.

## Enterprise Example

Two policies govern different audiences. Governance may determine that only the internal-use policy applies to an internal declared use; it cannot extend that permission to external disclosure.

## Boundaries

No policy language, policy engine, executable rule, enforcement point, registry, or policy-authoring process is defined.
