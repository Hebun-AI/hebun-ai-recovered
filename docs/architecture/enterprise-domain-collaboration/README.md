# Phase 19 — Enterprise Domain Collaboration Constitution

## Purpose

Phase 19 defines how the nine Enterprise Domain Intelligence components may collaborate constitutionally while remaining independent, non-authoritative, non-executing, Tenant-bounded, and governed.

Collaboration means the governed eligibility to contribute or consume attributable intelligence for a declared multi-domain analytical purpose. It is not Agent communication, data-access permission, task delegation, shared memory, orchestration, workflow, Runtime, or execution.

## Scope

Phase 19 defines collaboration rights, contribution and consumption rules, multi-domain analysis participation, composition constraints, escalation cooperation, shared-objective context, dependency constraints, isolation, boundary protection, Director visibility, Governance participation, prohibited relationships, and review criteria.

## Phase Objective

Phase 19 establishes the constitutional collaboration model between the nine Phase 18 Enterprise Domain Intelligence components. It permits governed intelligence contribution, consumption, composition, and escalation cooperation while preserving domain independence, source authority, information ownership, access authorization, Tenant and classification boundaries, Governance, Decision Support, and Director final authority.

The objective is constitutional collaboration only. It is not operational communication or execution.

## Canonical Position

```text
Phase 18 — independent Domain Intelligence constitutions
        ↓ preserves every domain boundary
Phase 19 — constitutional collaboration relationships
        ↓ future phases remain separately gated
Phase 20+ — memory, coordination, and Runtime are not defined here
```

This is constitutional dependency layering, not a communication path, sequence, protocol, or operational topology.

## Architecture Summary

Every domain may participate in multi-domain analysis when purpose, Scope, provenance, classification, Tenant, authority, and Governance conditions permit. Contribution never transfers ownership or canonical-source authority. Consumption never grants access. Composition never becomes Reasoning, Decision Support, approval, command, or execution.

Executive Intelligence may synthesize attributable domain intelligence, but it remains a participant and cannot become a Reasoning Engine, Decision Support authority, organizational command point, or substitute Director.

## Constitutional Invariants

- Collaboration ≠ Authority
- Collaboration ≠ Ownership
- Collaboration ≠ Data-access authorization
- Contribution ≠ Ownership transfer
- Consumption ≠ Canonical-source authority
- Composition ≠ Reasoning
- Synthesis ≠ Decision Support authority
- Shared objective ≠ Shared authority
- Visibility ≠ Approval
- Domain consensus ≠ Director decision
- Domain independence is preserved
- Director = Final Authority

## Boundary Summary

- Information contribution, consumption, ownership, canonical-source authority, data-access authorization, Tenant authorization, classification authorization, and provenance responsibility remain distinct.
- Collaboration eligibility grants none of these automatically.
- No domain may command, override, assign work to, redefine, or create authority over another domain.
- Executive synthesis remains attributable and cannot become Reasoning, Decision Support authority, approval, command, or execution.
- Governance and Director visibility are review obligations, not outcomes or approvals.
- Phase 20 memory and coordination and Phase 21+ Runtime remain outside Phase 19.
