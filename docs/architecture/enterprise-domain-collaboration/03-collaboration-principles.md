# 03 — Collaboration Principles

## Purpose

These principles define the normative rules for organizational cooperation, information contribution and consumption, cross-domain analysis, composition, isolation, visibility, and governance.

## Organizational Cooperation

1. **P19-PRINCIPLE-001 — Independent Participation:** Each domain participates as an independent constitutional intelligence component.
2. **P19-PRINCIPLE-002 — No Command:** No domain may command or assign work to another domain.
3. **P19-PRINCIPLE-003 — No Override:** No domain may override another domain's boundaries, interpretation, dissent, or escalation.
4. **P19-PRINCIPLE-004 — Shared Objective Boundary:** A shared organizational objective supplies analytical context, not shared ownership or authority.

## Information Contribution

5. **P19-PRINCIPLE-005 — Attributable Contribution:** Every contribution must identify domain, source, provenance, Scope, lifecycle, classification, Tenant, purpose, uncertainty, and limitations.
6. **P19-PRINCIPLE-006 — No Authority Transfer:** Contribution must not transfer canonical-source authority, ownership, approval, or permission.
7. **P19-PRINCIPLE-007 — Purpose Compatibility:** Intelligence may contribute only when its authorized purpose is compatible with the multi-domain purpose.

## Information Consumption

8. **P19-PRINCIPLE-008 — Separate Access Authority:** Collaboration eligibility must not grant data access.
9. **P19-PRINCIPLE-009 — Meaning Preservation:** A consuming domain must not broaden, rewrite, suppress, or detach source meaning.
10. **P19-PRINCIPLE-010 — Minimization:** Consumption must be limited to the least information required by the admitted analytical Scope.

## Analysis and Composition

11. **P19-PRINCIPLE-011 — Analysis Participation:** Domains may contribute premises, context, constraints, and interpretations to governed analysis without becoming the Reasoning Engine.
12. **P19-PRINCIPLE-012 — Composition Traceability:** Every composed statement must remain reconstructable to its domain contributions.
13. **P19-PRINCIPLE-013 — No Manufactured Consensus:** Composition must preserve conflict, dissent, and uncertainty.
14. **P19-PRINCIPLE-014 — Executive Constraint:** Executive synthesis must not become decision, command, approval, or Director authority.

## Governance, Visibility, and Isolation

15. **P19-PRINCIPLE-015 — Governance Preservation:** No collaboration may bypass or reinterpret Governance Outcomes.
16. **P19-PRINCIPLE-016 — Director Visibility:** Material strategic, authority, cross-domain, and committing implications must be visible to the Director.
17. **P19-PRINCIPLE-017 — Tenant Isolation:** Cross-Tenant collaboration is prohibited absent separately governed authority and compatible purpose.
18. **P19-PRINCIPLE-018 — Classification Isolation:** Restricted information remains isolated unless separately authorized for the receiving Scope.
19. **P19-PRINCIPLE-019 — Escalation Before Assumption:** Missing authority, permission, evidence, or clarity requires escalation, not inference.
20. **P19-PRINCIPLE-020 — Non-Operational Constitution:** No principle may be interpreted as a protocol, task, workflow, shared memory, Runtime behavior, or execution instruction.

## Conformance

Any relationship that violates independence, authority, access, provenance, Tenant, classification, Governance, Director visibility, or non-operational boundaries is constitutionally inadmissible.
