# 01 — Phase 19 Overview

## Purpose

Phase 19 establishes the constitutional collaboration model among the nine Phase 18 Enterprise Domain Intelligence components.

## Architectural Mission

Make multi-domain intelligence possible without merging domain identities, transferring ownership, creating authority, granting access, producing hidden consensus, or introducing operational behavior.

## Required Outcomes

- collaboration rights and obligations;
- contribution and consumption rules;
- cross-domain analysis participation;
- non-authoritative intelligence composition;
- escalation cooperation;
- shared-objective context without shared command;
- dependency and isolation rules;
- Director and Governance visibility;
- a descriptive Collaboration Matrix;
- explicit prohibited relationships.

## Inherited Domains

Executive, Finance, Human Resources, Sales, Marketing, Legal & Compliance, Operations, Customer Success, and Platform & Technology Intelligence retain their complete Phase 18 identities and boundaries.

## Explicit Exclusions

No Agent messaging, protocol, transport, event, queue, API, task delegation, shared memory, storage, synchronization, workflow, planning engine, orchestration, Runtime, execution, tools, Computer Use, automation, external integration, access-control implementation, schema, prompt, model, code, or infrastructure is defined.

## Rules

- **P19-OVERVIEW-001:** Every collaboration relationship must preserve Phase 18 domain independence.
- **P19-OVERVIEW-002:** Collaboration must remain constitutional and descriptive, never operational.
- **P19-OVERVIEW-003:** Collaboration must not create authority, ownership, permission, instruction, or execution.
- **P19-OVERVIEW-004:** Phase 19 must not pre-empt Phase 20 or Runtime architecture.
- **P19-OVERVIEW-005:** Every material relationship must remain visible to applicable Director and Governance boundaries.

## Enterprise Example

Finance Intelligence may consume attributable Sales pipeline intelligence for multi-domain analysis. That relationship neither grants access to Sales systems nor lets Finance redefine the Sales finding, command Sales, or authorize a financial or commercial action.
