# 04 — Domain Collaboration Relationships

## Purpose

This document defines the constitutional collaboration posture of each Phase 18 domain without defining messages, access, tasks, storage, ordering, or mechanisms.

## Executive Intelligence

May synthesize attributable contributions from all domains for an admitted enterprise analytical purpose. Must preserve domain identity, conflict, uncertainty, and Governance restrictions and cannot manufacture consensus. Cannot command domains, redefine contributions, approve decisions, become Reasoning or Decision Support authority, or replace the Director.

## Finance Intelligence

May consume authorized commercial, workforce, marketing, operational, customer, legal, and platform intelligence for financial analysis and may contribute financial constraints and implications. Cannot authorize spend, redefine source-domain findings, or turn financial exposure into organizational command.

## Human Resources Intelligence

May consume minimized Executive, Finance, Operations, Legal, and Platform context for workforce analysis and contribute bounded workforce-capacity or organizational-health intelligence. Personal and protected information remains isolated; no domain relationship authorizes individual-level use.

## Sales Intelligence

May consume authorized Marketing, Finance, Legal, Operations, Customer Success, and Platform intelligence and contribute attributable demand, pipeline, and commercial context. Cannot command campaigns, service, capacity, pricing, or customer action.

## Marketing Intelligence

May consume authorized Sales, Customer Success, Finance, Legal, Platform, and Executive context and contribute market, audience, brand, attribution, and demand intelligence. Cannot activate audiences, redefine customer intent, or initiate campaigns.

## Legal & Compliance Intelligence

May consume declared purpose, data, activity, jurisdiction, and Scope context from every domain and contribute attributable obligations, constraints, ambiguity, and review requirements. Cannot become Governance authority, waive obligations, command domains, or issue binding legal judgment.

## Operations Intelligence

May consume authorized Sales, Finance, HR, Legal, Customer Success, Platform, and Executive context and contribute capacity, constraint, quality, and continuity intelligence. Cannot schedule, allocate, dispatch, remediate, or control operations.

## Customer Success Intelligence

May consume authorized Sales, Marketing, Operations, Finance, Legal, and Platform context and contribute adoption, outcome, service, and retention intelligence. Cannot contact customers, commit service, redefine account ownership, or trigger intervention.

## Platform & Technology Intelligence

May consume declared purpose, demand, classification, risk, and constraint context from every domain and contribute architecture, dependency, lifecycle, security, resilience, and platform intelligence. Cannot grant access, operate platforms, change architecture, or invoke technology.

## Relationship Classes

- **Eligible informational:** Domain semantics support contribution or consumption, subject to separate authority.
- **Conditionally isolated:** Relevance exists, but Tenant, purpose, classification, privilege, personal-data, or authority conditions may prohibit use.
- **Governance-visible:** Policy, legal, privacy, security, compliance, or classification implications require Governance visibility.
- **Director-visible:** Strategic, cross-domain conflict, authority, ownership, or committing implications require Director visibility.
- **Constitutionally prohibited:** Command, override, assignment, redefinition, authority transfer, unauthorized access, execution, or boundary bypass.

## Rules

- **P19-RELATIONSHIP-001:** Every relationship must use one or more declared constitutional classes.
- **P19-RELATIONSHIP-002:** Eligibility must not imply availability or access.
- **P19-RELATIONSHIP-003:** A domain-specific prohibition survives every collaboration relationship.
- **P19-RELATIONSHIP-004:** Executive synthesis does not create hierarchical command over intelligence domains.
- **P19-RELATIONSHIP-005:** Legal or Platform relevance does not create Governance, access, or enforcement authority.

## Explicit Non-Responsibilities

No relationship specifies who sends, receives, calls, routes, stores, synchronizes, schedules, or executes anything.
