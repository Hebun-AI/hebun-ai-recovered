# 07 — Explicitly Prohibited Relationships

## Purpose

This document defines relationship classes that are constitutionally invalid regardless of domain, purpose, urgency, intelligence quality, consensus, or future implementation capability.

## Authority and Ownership Prohibitions

- one domain commanding or overruling another;
- one domain assigning work, responsibility, ownership, or authority to another;
- collaboration creating shared authority, committee authority, or majority-rule authority;
- Executive Intelligence becoming a hierarchy over domain intelligence;
- Legal & Compliance Intelligence becoming Governance authority;
- Platform & Technology Intelligence becoming access or technical authority.

## Information Prohibitions

- contribution transferring information ownership or source authority;
- consumption occurring without separate data-access authorization;
- stripping provenance, classification, lifecycle, Tenant, uncertainty, or limitations;
- redefining another domain's intelligence;
- using restricted information beyond purpose or Scope;
- treating repeated claims or consensus as canonical truth.

## Intelligence Boundary Prohibitions

- composition becoming the Reasoning Engine;
- synthesis becoming Decision Support authority;
- collaboration bypassing Governance or Decision Support;
- a domain approving a decision or replacing the Director;
- visibility being represented as permission, approval, or authorization.

## Operational Prohibitions

- collaboration represented as Agent messaging, task delegation, handoff, routing, protocol, event, queue, API, shared memory, storage, synchronization, workflow, planning, orchestration, Runtime, tool use, Computer Use, automation, external integration, or execution;
- a matrix cell treated as an executable edge or access-control rule;
- an escalation treated as a task or command.

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-pattern | Constitutional defect |
|---|---|
| “Executive Intelligence directs Finance to revise its finding.” | command and redefinition |
| “Finance may read Sales data because the matrix says `I`.” | collaboration mistaken for access |
| “All domains agree, therefore the proposal is approved.” | consensus mistaken for authority |
| “Legal flagged it, therefore Governance denied it.” | domain intelligence replacing Governance |
| “Platform can access it, therefore Platform may use it.” | technical access mistaken for permission |
| “The joint analysis triggered remediation.” | intelligence converted into execution |

## Rules

- **P19-PROHIBITED-001:** A prohibited relationship is invalid even when all participating domains consent.
- **P19-PROHIBITED-002:** Urgency must not waive authority, access, Tenant, classification, or Governance boundaries.
- **P19-PROHIBITED-003:** Technical feasibility must not legitimize an invalid relationship.
- **P19-PROHIBITED-004:** Domain consensus must not create approval, decision, or canonical truth.
- **P19-PROHIBITED-005:** Any detected prohibited relationship requires termination and governed escalation.

## Future Compatibility

Phases 20–24 may add memory, coordination, communication, and Runtime mechanisms only if these prohibited constitutional relationships remain impossible.
