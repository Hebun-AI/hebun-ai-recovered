## Coverage Validation

Phase 19 covers collaboration principles, organizational cooperation, contribution, consumption, analysis participation, composition, escalation cooperation, shared objectives, dependency constraints, isolation, boundary protection, Director visibility, Governance boundaries, invariants, prohibited relationships, and the canonical Matrix.

## Quality Criteria

- README and `01–09` are present, sequential, unique, non-empty, and indexed.
- All relative links resolve.
- Rule identities are unique.
- All nine domains appear consistently.
- Matrix legend and cells are internally valid.
- No orphan document or duplicate section exists.
- Phase 18 boundaries remain intact.
- No future architecture or implementation leaks into Phase 19.

## Residual Risks

- matrix eligibility may be mistaken for access permission;
- composition may be mistaken for Reasoning or Decision Support;
- Executive synthesis may be mistaken for command;
- Governance visibility may be mistaken for a Governance Outcome;
- future implementations may turn descriptive relationships into operational edges;
- enterprise scale may pressure Tenant, classification, privilege, and provenance boundaries.

## Rules

- **P19-REVIEW-001:** Every mandate requirement must map to a document and unique Rule Identity.
- **P19-REVIEW-002:** Missing domains, invalid matrix markers, broken links, or duplicate identities block readiness.
- **P19-REVIEW-003:** Authority, access, Runtime, communication, memory, orchestration, workflow, or execution leakage blocks readiness.
- **P19-REVIEW-004:** Phase 17 and Phase 18 constitutional inheritance is mandatory.
- **P19-REVIEW-005:** Director review is required before Phase 19 closure.
- **P19-REVIEW-006:** Phase 20 requires a separate Director gate.
