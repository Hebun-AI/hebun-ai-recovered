# 02 — Enterprise Domain Collaboration Constitution

## Purpose

This constitution is inherited by every Phase 19 collaboration relationship and every future realization that uses one.

## Constitutional Identity

A collaboration relationship is a governed, purpose-bounded eligibility for independently constituted domains to contribute or consume attributable intelligence in a multi-domain analytical context.

It is not an Agent relationship, organizational reporting line, ownership transfer, authority delegation, access grant, message, task, handoff, workflow edge, shared-memory link, Runtime binding, or execution instruction.

## Collaboration Rights

A domain may:

- declare that its intelligence is relevant to a governed multi-domain analytical purpose;
- contribute intelligence whose use is already authorized;
- consume intelligence when separate source, access, purpose, classification, Tenant, and Governance conditions are satisfied;
- preserve a distinct interpretation or dissent;
- reject incompatible, insufficient, unauthorized, or out-of-Scope intelligence;
- participate in escalation without surrendering its identity or authority boundary.

## Collaboration Obligations

Every relationship must preserve source identity, canonical-source authority, information ownership, provenance, classification, lifecycle, Tenant, purpose, uncertainty, limitations, domain interpretation, and applicable Governance Outcome.

## Independence

No domain may command, override, assign work to, redefine, absorb, impersonate, or create authority over another domain. Repeated contribution, centrality, expertise, consensus, or Executive synthesis does not weaken independence.

## Director and Governance Visibility

Material cross-domain conflict, sensitive information reuse, cross-Tenant concern, Governance restriction, authority ambiguity, strategic synthesis, or possible committing implication must remain visible to the applicable human, Governance, or Director boundary.

## Rules

- **P19-CONSTITUTION-001:** Collaboration eligibility must not be treated as data-access authorization.
- **P19-CONSTITUTION-002:** Contribution must not transfer information ownership or canonical-source authority.
- **P19-CONSTITUTION-003:** Consumption must preserve source meaning and limitations.
- **P19-CONSTITUTION-004:** Composition must not become Reasoning, Decision Support authority, approval, or execution.
- **P19-CONSTITUTION-005:** No domain may command, override, assign, or redefine another domain.
- **P19-CONSTITUTION-006:** Domain dissent and conflict must remain attributable.
- **P19-CONSTITUTION-007:** Director and Governance visibility must not be interpreted as approval.
- **P19-CONSTITUTION-008:** Collaboration must fail closed when permission, Scope, purpose, classification, Tenant, or authority is insufficient.

## Implementation Boundary

This constitution defines no mechanism for communication, access, enforcement, persistence, routing, synchronization, or execution.
