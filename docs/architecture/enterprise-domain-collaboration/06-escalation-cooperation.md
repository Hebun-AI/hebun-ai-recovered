# 06 — Escalation Cooperation

## Purpose

Escalation cooperation defines how multiple domains preserve and present a shared unresolved condition to the proper authority without delegating tasks, negotiating an outcome, or creating collective authority.

## Cooperation Conditions

Domains may participate in one escalation when the unresolved matter spans domain interpretations, contains conflicting evidence, crosses ownership boundaries, involves restricted information, depends on missing authority, or has material strategic, legal, compliance, privacy, security, Tenant, or committing implications.

## Required Escalation Content

- participating domains and their independent Scopes;
- shared analytical purpose;
- each domain's attributable contribution and interpretation;
- canonical sources, provenance, classification, lifecycle, and Tenant;
- agreements, disagreements, missing evidence, and uncertainty;
- applicable Governance Outcomes and unresolved constraints;
- exact authority question;
- required Director, Governance, or qualified human visibility;
- which participation must stop pending resolution.

## Independence Preservation

One domain must not speak for another, rewrite another's contribution, suppress dissent, select a shared outcome, or appoint itself escalation owner. Administrative consolidation, if ever implemented later, cannot change constitutional authorship.

## Recipients

- **Director visibility:** strategy, enterprise priority, ownership, final decision, approval, roadmap, or execution authorization.
- **Governance visibility:** policy, permission, classification, privacy, compliance, legal, security, or Governance Outcome ambiguity.
- **Qualified human visibility:** protected people matters, binding legal judgment, safety, or another reserved professional responsibility.

Visibility may overlap. It does not create approval or require a particular decision.

## Termination

A domain must stop the affected collaboration when continuation would require missing access, permission, authority, Governance resolution, or Director judgment. Silence, delay, or participation by another domain is not consent.

## Rules

- **P19-ESCALATION-001:** Joint escalation must preserve independent domain authorship.
- **P19-ESCALATION-002:** Escalation cooperation must not become delegation or organizational command.
- **P19-ESCALATION-003:** No domain may resolve an authority conflict on behalf of another.
- **P19-ESCALATION-004:** Material conflict and dissent must remain visible to recipients.
- **P19-ESCALATION-005:** Participation must stop when safe continuation depends on escalation.
- **P19-ESCALATION-006:** Recipient visibility must not be represented as approval.

## Implementation Exclusion

No notification, channel, routing, priority, queue, SLA, workflow, message, event, or escalation service is defined.
