# 01 — Phase 24 Overview

## Purpose

Establish constitutional Runtime Governance and Operational Resilience for deviations, failures, uncertainty, control, escalation, recovery, and degraded operation.

## Architectural Mission

Preserve constitutional authority, policy compatibility, safety, traceability, accountability, and bounded continuity without converting Governance into management or resilience into implementation.

## Required Outcomes

- a Runtime Governance Constitution;
- policy applicability and enforcement boundaries;
- constitutional Runtime compliance;
- control eligibility and authorization separation;
- failure classification and escalation;
- recovery authority and Human Override;
- Safe Degradation and Operational Resilience;
- explicit technology and implementation boundaries.

## Continuity

Phase 24 consumes Phase 21 Runtime responsibility, Phase 22 operational facts and progression, and Phase 23 evidence, Metrics, conditions, and Alerts. It neither changes those artifacts nor creates missing authority.

## Explicit Exclusions

No retry algorithm, circuit breaker, bulkhead, Kubernetes, Auto Scaling, Chaos Engineering, self-healing implementation, deployment architecture, infrastructure platform, API, protocol, queue, technology selection, tool calling, Computer Use, or code is defined.

## Rules

- **P24-OVERVIEW-001:** Runtime Governance and Operational Resilience must remain constitutional and implementation-independent.
- **P24-OVERVIEW-002:** Every control or recovery eligibility claim must trace to authority, policy, and evidence.
- **P24-OVERVIEW-003:** Operational continuity must never override constitutional compliance.
- **P24-OVERVIEW-004:** Human and Director authority must remain superior to Runtime behavior.
- **P24-OVERVIEW-005:** Future implementation requires a separate Director gate.

## Enterprise Example

Monitoring evidence may show a Runtime deviation. Governance may determine that a bounded suspension control is eligible, but only valid authority may authorize it and Runtime may not invent recovery.
