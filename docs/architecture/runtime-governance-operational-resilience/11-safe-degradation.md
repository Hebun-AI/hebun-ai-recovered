# 11 — Safe Degradation

## Purpose

Define reduced operational posture that preserves constitutional integrity when full operation cannot remain valid.

## Safe Degradation Identity

Safe Degradation is an authorized, bounded reduction of Runtime responsibility, service level, outcome completeness, or operational availability to preserve safety and constitutional compliance.

Safe Degradation is not silent failure, uncontrolled partial execution, availability optimization, self-healing, retry, recovery implementation, policy bypass, or acceptance of invalid operation.

## Integrity Priority

Safe Degradation preserves authority, policy, Governance, Tenant, classification, evidence, Memory, Workflow, State, Event, Scheduling, Monitoring, traceability, and Human Override boundaries before operational availability.

## Required Context

A degradation posture declares evidence, failure class, policy, authority, affected Scope, retained responsibility, prohibited behavior, expected limitations, visibility, exit criteria, escalation, and accountability.

## Rules

- **P24-DEGRADATION-001:** Safe Degradation preserves constitutional integrity before operational availability.
- **P24-DEGRADATION-002:** Degradation requires explicit policy and authority.
- **P24-DEGRADATION-003:** Reduced operation must not broaden responsibility or hide failure.
- **P24-DEGRADATION-004:** Limitations and affected outcomes must remain visible.
- **P24-DEGRADATION-005:** Exit from degradation requires evidence and valid authority.
- **P24-DEGRADATION-006:** Safe Degradation must not prescribe implementation mechanisms.

## Enterprise Example

Runtime may operate in a read-only constitutional posture when committing-action authority is invalid, preserving integrity at the cost of availability.
