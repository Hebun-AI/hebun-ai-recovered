# 10 — Human Override

## Purpose

Define the constitutional supremacy and audit obligations of authorized human intervention over Runtime behavior.

## Human Override Identity

Human Override is an explicit intervention by an authorized human boundary that supersedes a Runtime determination, Control posture, Scheduling eligibility, degradation posture, or recovery proposal within declared authority.

Human Override is not autonomous Runtime, informal preference, hidden manual action, universal authority, policy erasure, evidence rewriting, or unbounded emergency power.

## Authority

The human actor must possess applicable authority for the affected Scope. Override preserves identity, rationale, evidence, policy context, affected responsibility, duration, limitations, consequences, accountability, and review obligations.

Director authority remains final where reserved. A lower human boundary cannot override the Director or exceed its own authority.

## Rules

- **P24-HUMAN-001:** Human Override supersedes Runtime decisions.
- **P24-HUMAN-002:** Override must have explicit human identity, authority, Scope, and rationale.
- **P24-HUMAN-003:** Override must preserve evidence and must not silently rewrite policy.
- **P24-HUMAN-004:** Emergency context must not create unlimited authority.
- **P24-HUMAN-005:** Override duration and termination basis must remain explicit.
- **P24-HUMAN-006:** Human Override ≠ Autonomous Runtime.

## Enterprise Example

An authorized human may terminate a recovery proposal that threatens constitutional integrity. Runtime cannot reject that intervention based on availability objectives.
