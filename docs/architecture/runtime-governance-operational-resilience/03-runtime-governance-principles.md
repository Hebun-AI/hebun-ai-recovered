# 03 — Runtime Governance Principles

## Purpose

Define durable principles for constitutional Runtime Governance.

## Principles

1. **Constitution first:** canonical architecture bounds every Governance outcome.
2. **Director subordinate:** Runtime Governance never replaces final authority.
3. **Approved policy only:** unapproved rules cannot constrain Runtime.
4. **Evidence based:** evaluation depends on attributable evidence.
5. **No fabrication:** missing evidence remains missing.
6. **Least control:** eligibility is limited to the minimum justified control class.
7. **Fail closed:** unresolved authority, policy, or evidence blocks control authorization.
8. **Conflict visible:** incompatible policies and evidence remain explicit.
9. **Traceable:** every outcome preserves policy, evidence, authority, and version.
10. **Implementation independent:** mechanisms cannot redefine Governance meaning.

## Rules

- **P24-GOVERNANCE-PRINCIPLE-001:** Governance must preserve constitutional authority.
- **P24-GOVERNANCE-PRINCIPLE-002:** Governance must enforce only approved policy boundaries.
- **P24-GOVERNANCE-PRINCIPLE-003:** Policy applicability must remain Scope-, Tenant-, purpose-, and version-aware.
- **P24-GOVERNANCE-PRINCIPLE-004:** Strong operational pressure must not weaken constitutional constraints.
- **P24-GOVERNANCE-PRINCIPLE-005:** Governance outcome must not imply control execution.
- **P24-GOVERNANCE-PRINCIPLE-006:** Exceptions require explicit authority and preserved history.

## Enterprise Example

Service urgency cannot make an inapplicable or expired policy valid, and availability pressure cannot bypass Director authority.
