# 02 — Runtime Governance Constitution

## Purpose

Define the universal constitutional contract for governing Runtime behavior.

## Constitutional Identity

Runtime Governance is the evidence-based evaluation and bounded authorization context through which approved policies constrain Runtime admission, realization, control, degradation, escalation, recovery, and closure.

Runtime Governance is not management, Director authority, business decision, operational execution, automation, policy implementation, control mechanism, Runtime platform, or infrastructure.

## Constitutional Duties

Runtime Governance must:

- preserve canonical architecture and Director authority;
- identify applicable approved policies and versions;
- evaluate attributable Runtime evidence;
- determine compliance and control eligibility within existing authority;
- preserve Tenant, classification, Scope, purpose, and accountability;
- expose uncertainty, conflicts, exceptions, and missing evidence;
- maintain traceability across evidence, policy, authority, and outcomes;
- fail closed when constitutional compatibility is insufficient.

## Authority Limits

Governance may authorize control only where existing constitutional authority permits. It cannot rewrite architecture, fabricate evidence, create Director authority, execute control, modify Memory, reinterpret approval, or silently grant exceptions.

## Rules

- **P24-GOVERNANCE-001:** Every Governance evaluation must cite applicable policy, authority, and evidence.
- **P24-GOVERNANCE-002:** Governance may authorize control but never rewrite constitutional architecture.
- **P24-GOVERNANCE-003:** Governance evaluates evidence but never fabricates evidence.
- **P24-GOVERNANCE-004:** Governance uncertainty and conflict must remain explicit.
- **P24-GOVERNANCE-005:** Runtime Governance must remain subordinate to Director authority.
- **P24-GOVERNANCE-006:** Governance meaning must remain independent of implementation technology.

## Enterprise Example

Governance may determine that continued operation violates an approved policy and that suspension is eligible. It does not itself perform suspension or alter the policy.
