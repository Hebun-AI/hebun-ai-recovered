## Executive Summary

Phase 7B established the **Director Cognitive Model** — the definition of *how* Director Reasoning thinks. Where Phase 7A defined *why* reasoning exists and the principles it obeys, Phase 7B defines the **ordered cognitive lifecycle** every reasoning engine must move through: from observing the situation to presenting a Director-ready recommendation.

The model defines the sequence Director Reasoning follows **before any execution is considered**. The lifecycle ends at the Director Gate — reasoning thinks all the way to a recommendation and stops; the Director decides; execution belongs to later layers and later phases, entirely outside this one.

This phase defined **architecture only**. No algorithms, no prompts, no implementation, no runtime, no execution logic. It builds on the certified Phase 5–6 baseline and the Phase 7A reasoning philosophy without modifying either.

## Architectural Achievements

Phase 7B established the ordered cognitive lifecycle (no new stage is introduced in this closure):

- **Observation** — grounding reasoning in what is actually true.
- **Context Building** — giving that understanding meaning in its circumstances.
- **Goal Identification** — fixing the organizational objective reasoning serves.
- **Constraint Analysis** — bounding the field and flagging gated committing actions early.
- **Option Generation** — producing a genuine field of alternatives.
- **Trade-off Analysis** — weighing gain, cost, opportunity, risk, and organizational impact.
- **Recommendation** — packaging an explainable, decision-ready judgment.
- **Director Gate** — terminating the cycle at the Director's decision.
- **Future Evolution** — deepening each stage while the lifecycle and its stopping point stay fixed.

The lifecycle is **sequential** (stages are ordered and dependent, none skipped), **evidence-based** (each stage grounded in the knowledge layers), **explainable** (the recommendation traces end-to-end through the stages), and **advisory** (it produces judgment, never action — it ends at the Director's decision).
