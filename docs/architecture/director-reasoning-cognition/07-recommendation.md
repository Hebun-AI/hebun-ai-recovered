# 07 — Recommendation

*The stage after Trade-off Analysis.*

## What it means

Recommendation is where reasoning **forms its judgment and prepares it for the Director**. The weighed options converge into a clear recommendation — which course reasoning suggests, why, with what confidence, and what it would cost. This is the culmination of the cognitive lifecycle: everything the earlier stages produced is assembled into a single, decision-ready package.

A recommendation is a *conclusion*, not an action. It is reasoning's best judgment, offered to the Director — complete, honest, and advisory.

## Why it exists

Because the point of all the preceding stages is to arrive at usable judgment. Observation, context, goals, constraints, options, and trade-offs exist to make *this* — a sound recommendation — possible. This stage exists to package reasoning's judgment in the form the Director can act on: the Phase 7A principle **explainable recommendations** ([first principles](../director-reasoning/02-first-principles.md)) is fully realized here. A recommendation the Director cannot interrogate is a recommendation the Director cannot own.

## Required inputs

- **Weighed options** from Trade-off Analysis, with their ordering, confidence, and uncertainties.
- The **goals, constraints, and context** the recommendation must reflect and be explainable against.

## Expected outputs

A **decision-ready recommendation**, containing:

- the **recommended course** — reasoning's suggested option (which may be "do nothing");
- the **reasoning** — the evidence and trade-offs that lead to it, traceable to the earlier stages;
- the **alternatives** considered, so the Director sees the real field, not just the pick;
- the **confidence** and the **material uncertainties**, stated honestly;
- the **commitment status** — whether enacting it is on the free side of the Director Gate or requires explicit approval.

## Transition criteria

Reasoning may move to the Director Gate when:

- a clear recommendation is formed, with its reasoning explainable end-to-end;
- the alternatives, confidence, and uncertainties are presented, not hidden;
- the recommendation's commitment status is marked.

If the recommendation is not yet explainable, or hides its alternatives or uncertainty, reasoning stays here. Only a complete, honest, explainable recommendation is ready for the Director.
