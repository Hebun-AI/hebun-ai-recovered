# 01 — Observation → Understanding

*The first stage of the reasoning lifecycle.*

## What it means

Observation is where reasoning **takes in what is true** before doing anything with it. The engine reads the relevant state from the knowledge layers — what exists (the model), how it connects (the graph), what has happened (memory) — and forms an initial, faithful picture of the situation. Its output is **Understanding**: a grounded grasp of *what is going on*, prior to any goal, option, or judgment.

Observation and Understanding are one movement: observation is the intake, understanding is what the intake produces. Reasoning cannot begin to judge until it has understood.

## Why it exists

Because judgment must rest on reality, not assumption. The Phase 7A principle **evidence before conclusion** ([first principles](../director-reasoning/02-first-principles.md)) begins here: reasoning is not permitted to leap to options or recommendations before it has actually looked at the situation. Skipping observation produces confident answers to imagined problems. This stage forces reasoning to *see clearly first*.

## Required inputs

- The **trigger** — the question, opportunity, event, or standing concern that set reasoning in motion.
- Access to the **knowledge layers** — Organizational Model, Relationship Graph, Organizational Memory — as read-only evidence.

## Expected outputs

- **Understanding** — a faithful, evidence-grounded account of the current situation relevant to the trigger: the entities and relationships in play, the pertinent history, and the plain facts, with provenance intact.
- An explicit note of **what is not yet known** — gaps in the picture, surfaced rather than hidden ([explicit uncertainty](../director-reasoning/02-first-principles.md)).

## Transition criteria

Reasoning may move to Context Building when:

- the situation has been observed from the actual knowledge layers, not assumed;
- the understanding is grounded in traceable evidence;
- known gaps are named rather than glossed.

If the picture is still guessed rather than observed, reasoning stays here. Understanding that is not yet grounded is not yet ready to be contextualized.
