# 03 — Goal Identification

*The stage after Context Building.*

## What it means

Goal Identification is where reasoning establishes **what it is trying to achieve**. With the situation understood and contextualized, the engine determines the objective the reasoning serves — what a good outcome would look like for the organization in this situation. Everything downstream (options, trade-offs, recommendation) is measured against these goals.

Goals are not invented by reasoning in a vacuum. They derive from the organization's direction, the Director's intent, and the situation itself. Reasoning identifies and makes them explicit; it does not impose goals of its own.

## Why it exists

Because reasoning without a goal is aimless — options cannot be generated or judged without knowing what they are for. And because of the Phase 7A principle **organization before optimization** ([first principles](../director-reasoning/02-first-principles.md)): the goal must be the organization's, not a local metric. This stage exists to fix the target explicitly, so that later stages optimize toward the right thing and the Director can see *what outcome the reasoning is pursuing*.

## Required inputs

- **Contextualized understanding** from Context Building.
- The organization's **direction and the Director's intent** as reflected in the model, memory (including learned preferences and prior decisions), and any standing objectives.

## Expected outputs

- **Identified goals** — an explicit statement of what a good outcome is in this situation, framed at the organizational level.
- Where multiple goals apply, their **relative importance**, and any tension between them noted for later trade-off analysis.

## Transition criteria

Reasoning may move to Constraint Analysis when:

- the objective is explicit and framed in the organization's interest, not a local proxy;
- the goals trace to the organization's direction and the Director's intent, not to reasoning's own preference;
- competing goals, if any, are surfaced.

If reasoning does not yet know what it is trying to achieve, it stays here. Options generated against an unclear goal are noise.
