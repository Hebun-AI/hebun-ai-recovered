# 02 — Context Building

*The stage after Observation.*

## What it means

Context Building is where reasoning **situates the understanding in its circumstances**. Observation established *what is going on*; context building establishes *what it means, here and now*. The engine surrounds the raw understanding with the frames that give it significance — the organizational setting, the business situation, the relevant history, the moment in time.

The same fact means different things in different contexts. Context Building is the stage that supplies those differences before any goal or option is considered.

## Why it exists

Because of the Phase 7A principle **context before action** ([first principles](../director-reasoning/02-first-principles.md)). An understanding stripped of context is a fact without meaning — and reasoning that acts on context-free facts answers the wrong question. This stage exists to ensure that everything downstream (goals, options, trade-offs) is judged *in situation*, not in the abstract. It draws directly on the memory semantic layer's context dimensions ([memory context](../memory-semantics/02-memory-context.md)).

## Required inputs

- **Understanding** from Observation — the grounded picture of the situation.
- The **context dimensions** available from the knowledge layers — organizational, business, temporal, project, workflow, and actor context, plus relevant remembered precedent.

## Expected outputs

- **Contextualized understanding** — the situation read through its frames: where it sits in the organization, what the business circumstances are, what history bears on it, what moment it occupies.
- A note of **which contexts matter most** for this situation, and any context that is missing or uncertain.

## Transition criteria

Reasoning may move to Goal Identification when:

- the understanding has been placed in its organizational, business, temporal, and historical context;
- the relevant precedent from memory has been brought to bear;
- the contexts that most shape the situation are identified.

If the understanding is still being read in the abstract — without its frames — reasoning stays here. A situation not yet contextualized cannot yet have goals meaningfully drawn from it.
