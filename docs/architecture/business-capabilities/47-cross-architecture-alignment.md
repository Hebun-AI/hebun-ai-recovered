# 47 — Cross-Architecture Alignment

## Purpose

Validate how Business Capability Architecture relates to existing Hebun architectures without replacing, redefining, or absorbing them. Phase 10 contributes the business-ability perspective and stable attachment points; source architectures retain their original responsibilities.

## Phase 7 Alignment

Director Reasoning remains the cognitive and authority-supporting layer. Phase 10 provides stable Capability facts, network context, and interpreted insight. It does not form final judgment, redesign plans, or execute.

**Result: Aligned.**

## Phase 8 Alignment

Execution Agents remain bounded performers of approved work; Execution Orchestration remains responsible for coordinating execution mechanics; Execution State remains transient continuity context. Phase 10F governs eligibility and attachment but does not schedule tasks or operate infrastructure.

**Result: Aligned.**

## Phase 9 Alignment

Enterprise Organization defines who owns outcomes and how Director authority delegates. Business Capability Architecture defines what the enterprise can do. Governance and ownership cross as attachments while identity remains separate.

**Result: Aligned.**

## Non-Replacement Rule

Phase 10 does not supersede Director Intelligence, Execution, Enterprise Organization, Product Capability Architecture, Execution State, Governance, or Observability. It connects them from the business-capability perspective through stable identities, explicit boundaries, governed attachments, and provenanced evidence.

## Overall Alignment Result

**ALIGNED — NO CROSS-ARCHITECTURE OWNERSHIP CONFLICT**
