# 05 — Escalation Model

## Purpose

The Escalation Model defines **how beyond-authority matters rise** through the organization during collaboration — the paths by which a matter that exceeds the collaborating units' delegated authority reaches a level that can authorize it, ultimately the Director. It is the enterprise's answer to a limit: escalate, never overreach.

## Architectural role

Escalation is the release valve of collaboration. Where the interaction model ([02](02-organizational-interaction-model.md)), ownership transfer ([03](03-ownership-transfer.md)), and cross-department coordination ([04](04-cross-department-coordination.md)) each stay within the participants' authority, escalation is what happens when a matter exceeds it. It unifies the escalation the manager ([manager escalation](../manager-architecture/06-escalation-governance.md)) and specialist ([specialist reporting](../specialist-architecture/06-reporting-and-governance.md)) architectures each defined into one cross-organization model.

## The model

### Escalation follows the hierarchy upward
A matter escalates from the unit that reached its limit to the level above it — specialist to manager, manager to enterprise level, enterprise level to Director ([enterprise organizational model](../enterprise-organization/02-organizational-model.md)). Escalation always travels the authority structure; it never jumps outside it or inverts it.

### What escalates
- **Beyond-authority matters** — anything requiring authority the collaborating units were not granted.
- **Committing actions** — anything requiring the Director's approval to commit ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)).
- **Cross-boundary matters** — collaboration or transfer that spans departments beyond a single manager's grant ([cross-department coordination](04-cross-department-coordination.md), [ownership transfer](03-ownership-transfer.md)).
- **Reasoning, planning, or decisions** — anything needing Director Intelligence, which is routed, not resolved by the collaborating units ([Director Intelligence](../director-reasoning/README.md)).

### Escalation preserves accountability
A unit that escalates does not shed its ownership or accountability; escalating is how it responsibly handles what it cannot authorize itself ([collaboration principles](01-collaboration-principles.md)). The escalating unit still answers for raising the right matter at the right time.

### Escalation reaches the Director for the highest matters
Committing actions and matters that exceed all delegated authority rise to the Director ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). The Director is the top of every escalation path; no collaboration resolves a Director-level matter below the Director.

### Escalation is traceable
Every escalation — what was raised, by whom, to whom, why — can be reconstructed and audited ([collaboration governance](06-collaboration-governance.md)). Escalation is part of the collaboration's traceable record, not an informal aside.

## Inputs

- The **authority bounds** of the collaborating units ([9C](../manager-architecture/03-manager-authority.md), [9D](../specialist-architecture/04-specialist-authority-boundaries.md)) — what defines a limit.
- The **hierarchy** ([9A](../enterprise-organization/02-organizational-model.md)) — the path escalation travels.

## Outputs

- **Escalation paths** — the defined routes beyond-authority matters travel upward, ultimately to the Director — that keep collaboration from ever overreaching.

## Boundaries

- Escalates through **seats and levels, not concrete units** — it names none.
- Performs **no reasoning, work, or orchestration** — it raises and routes; it does not resolve or execute.
- Manufactures **no authority** — it moves a matter to where authority exists, rather than creating it.
- Describes **no runtime or mechanism** — only the structure of escalation.

## Future direction

As human and AI participants collaborate across a growing enterprise, escalation applies unchanged: up the hierarchy, to the level that can authorize, ultimately the Director, accountability retained, fully traceable. More participants and more collaboration mean more escalation paths — same rule at every one. The enterprise grows; nothing overreaches.
