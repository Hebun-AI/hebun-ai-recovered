# 06 — Collaboration Governance

## Purpose

Collaboration Governance defines **how cross-organization collaboration stays governed and traceable**. Collaboration is where units meet and ownership can move — the highest-risk surface for authority to leak or accountability to blur. This document applies the enterprise-wide governance regime to that surface, holding every interaction, transfer, coordination, and escalation to the Director's standards and making all of them auditable.

## Architectural role

Governance sits over the whole collaboration architecture — the interaction model ([02](02-organizational-interaction-model.md)), ownership transfer ([03](03-ownership-transfer.md)), cross-department coordination ([04](04-cross-department-coordination.md)), and escalation ([05](05-escalation-model.md)). It is the enterprise governance ([9A](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)) applied to cross-unit collaboration, consistent with department ([9B](../department-architecture/06-department-governance.md)), manager ([9C](../manager-architecture/06-escalation-governance.md)), and specialist ([9D](../specialist-architecture/06-reporting-and-governance.md)) governance.

## The model

### Collaboration falls under enterprise governance — no exceptions
There is one governance regime, and every collaboration is inside it ([enterprise governance](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)). Collaboration invents no governance of its own and is never exempt. Governance reaches every interaction, transfer, coordination, and escalation.

### Governance aligns with Director Authority
Collaboration governance expresses the Director's standards across cross-unit work; it never sits above the Director ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). Where a collaboration would exceed governance or the participants' authority, governance blocks it and forces escalation ([escalation model](05-escalation-model.md)).

### Governance enforces ownership preservation and clean transfer
Governance holds collaboration to the ownership rules: one owner always, ownership preserved unless explicitly and authorizedly transferred, accountability carried with ownership ([ownership transfer](03-ownership-transfer.md)). It is how the ownership rules are enforced in practice, not merely declared.

### Governance enforces authority paths
Governance ensures collaboration flows through the hierarchy and never bypasses organizational authority ([collaboration principles](01-collaboration-principles.md)). A collaboration that tries to reach around a manager's authority or a unit's delegation is blocked and escalated.

### Every collaboration is traceable
Who collaborated with whom, what ownership moved, through which authority, what escalated — all can be reconstructed and audited. Traceability is structural — a property of the collaboration architecture, not of any run of work ([collaboration principles](01-collaboration-principles.md)).

### Governance preserves, it does not perform
Collaboration governance checks, records, and escalates. It reasons about no work and executes no task. It is the accountability layer over collaboration — not an actor in reasoning or execution.

## What collaboration governance preserves

- **Director Authority** — enforced across every collaboration; committing and beyond-grant matters escalate to it.
- **Department ownership, Manager accountability, Specialist responsibility** — none dissolved by collaborating.
- **Ownership-transfer integrity** — one owner always, transfers explicit, authorized, and accountable.
- **Enterprise governance** — one regime, reaching every collaboration, no exemptions.
- **Traceability** — every collaboration is auditable.

## Inputs

- The **collaboration interaction, transfer, coordination, and escalation models** ([02](02-organizational-interaction-model.md)–[05](05-escalation-model.md)) — what governance governs.
- The **enterprise governance regime** ([9A](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)) — the standards applied to collaboration.

## Outputs

- A **collaboration governance and traceability frame** — uniform standards and a reconstructable record — that holds cross-unit collaboration accountable within the enterprise.

## Boundaries

- Governs **collaboration between seats, not concrete units** — it names none.
- Performs **no reasoning, work, or orchestration** — it checks, records, and escalates.
- Describes **no runtime or mechanism** — only the governance concern over collaboration.

## Future direction

As human and AI participants collaborate across a growing enterprise, each collaboration falls under this governance the moment it happens — same regime, same traceability, no exemptions. Collaboration governance may deepen its checks over time ([future evolution](07-future-evolution.md)), but it stays enterprise-anchored, Director-topped, and traceable. Collaboration widens; accountability stays whole.
