# 02 — Core Capabilities

What Personal Enterprise Mode can do. Each capability is described at the **product level** — what it is for the Director, not how it works. All of them stop at the Director Gate for any committing action ([03 — Director Gates](03-director-gates.md)).

## Discovery and ideation

### Opportunity discovery
Hebun continuously scans for business opportunities worth the owner's attention — gaps, trends, underserved needs — and surfaces the strongest as decision-ready proposals rather than raw noise.

### SaaS opportunity discovery
A focused strand of discovery: identifying software-product openings — recurring problems, pricing gaps, tooling niches — and framing each as a candidate venture.

### AI product ideation
Generating ideas for AI-native products specifically — where a model, an agent, or an automation creates a product a person would pay for. Ideation produces concepts; it commits to nothing.

### Market research
Assembling the evidence behind an opportunity: demand signals, audience size, willingness to pay, adjacent solutions. Research grounds every proposal so the Director decides on facts, not hunches.

### Competitive intelligence
Understanding who already occupies a space, how they position, and where the openings are. Competitive intelligence sharpens a proposal's angle before the Director ever sees it.

## Design and strategy

### YouTube channel creation planning
Turning a content opportunity into a concrete channel plan — concept, audience, format, cadence, positioning — prepared to the point where the only remaining step is the Director's decision to launch.

### Digital product creation
Designing digital products (courses, templates, tools, media) from concept through offer definition — everything short of publishing or selling.

### Content strategy
Shaping how the owner's ideas reach an audience: themes, pillars, formats, and sequencing across a venture or the whole portfolio.

### Publishing strategy
Planning *where and when* content and products go out — channels, timing, distribution — as a prepared plan awaiting approval, never an autonomous release.

## Operate and optimize

### Business monitoring
Watching the running portfolio — what's live, what's performing, what's stalling — and surfacing what needs the Director's attention, grounded in the organization's memory of how things have gone.

### Revenue optimization
Continuously looking for ways existing ventures could earn more — pricing, offers, funnels, audience — and proposing optimizations for the Director to approve ([04 — Revenue Engine](04-revenue-engine.md)).

### Portfolio management
Holding the whole set of ventures in view as one portfolio: relative performance, resource attention, which to grow, hold, or retire — as advice to the Director, who decides.

---

## The common shape of every capability

Across all twelve, the pattern is identical:

1. **Hebun does the standing work** — discovers, researches, designs, prepares, monitors, forecasts.
2. **Hebun produces a decision-ready proposal** — grounded in memory, structured against the graph, sharpened by reasoning.
3. **The Director decides** — every commitment, launch, spend, or publish is the Director's, at the gate.

Every capability is powerful up to the point of commitment and stops there. The capabilities give Hebun reach and initiative; the Director Gates keep authority where it belongs.
