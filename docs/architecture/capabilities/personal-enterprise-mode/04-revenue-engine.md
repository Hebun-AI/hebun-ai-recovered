# 04 — Revenue Engine

The Revenue Engine is the always-on heart of Personal Enterprise Mode: the standing process by which Hebun **continuously searches for ways the owner could earn more**. It never sleeps, never loses the thread, and never commits — it surfaces opportunity, the Director acts on it.

This document describes the engine as a **product behavior**, not an implementation. No mechanism, no algorithm, no runtime.

## What it continuously searches for

The Revenue Engine scans five horizons at once:

### New businesses
Whole new ventures the owner could start — a channel, a product line, a service — spotted from trends, gaps, and the owner's own strengths and history.

### New products
Additional products within or adjacent to existing ventures — a next course, a companion tool, a new tier — that extend what already works.

### New audiences
Untapped audiences the owner's existing assets could reach — an adjacent niche, a new platform, an underserved segment — that expand reach without new products.

### New markets
Broader market openings — a geography, a vertical, a shift in demand — where the owner's capabilities could travel.

### Optimization opportunities
Ways existing ventures could earn more *as they are* — pricing, offers, funnels, retention, positioning — the compounding gains hiding in what already runs.

## How it works as a product behavior

- **Continuous, not periodic.** The engine runs as a standing background attention, always scanning, so opportunities are caught as they emerge rather than at review time.
- **Memory-grounded.** Every candidate is weighed against the organization's remembered past ([Phase 6](../../memory/README.md)) — what the owner tried, what worked, what was rejected — so proposals build on experience instead of repeating it.
- **Structure-aware.** Opportunities are framed against the owner's portfolio graph ([Phase 5](../../relationship-graph/README.md)) — which venture they extend, which audience they touch — not as free-floating ideas.
- **Reasoning-filtered.** Director Reasoning ([Phase 7]) sorts the raw field into the few proposals actually worth the Director's attention, so the engine surfaces signal, not volume.

## What the engine produces

The engine's output is a **stream of decision-ready opportunities**, ranked and grounded — never actions. For each, Hebun has already done the research, sketched the design, and forecast the outcome ([02 — Core Capabilities](02-core-capabilities.md)). What reaches the Director is *"here is an opportunity, here is why, here is the prepared plan"* — and nothing has been launched, spent, or published.

## The engine and the gates

The Revenue Engine is the clearest expression of the mode's constitution:

- It **searches, prepares, and forecasts** endlessly — all on the free side of the [Director Gate](03-director-gates.md).
- It **never launches, spends, or publishes** — every act of *capturing* an opportunity is the Director's, at the gate.

The engine gives the owner an inexhaustible pipeline of grounded opportunity. Turning any of it into revenue is always the Director's decision. **Tireless search, gated commitment** — that is the Revenue Engine.
