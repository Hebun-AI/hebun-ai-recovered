# 06 — Boundaries

The boundaries of Personal Enterprise Mode — what it is, what it is not, and the single principle that governs it.

## The governing principle

**This capability augments the Director. It never replaces the Director.**

Everything else in this document follows from that one sentence. Personal Enterprise Mode makes the owner more capable — reaching further, remembering more, moving faster — but the owner remains the sole authority, decision-maker, and source of commitment. Hebun is amplification, not substitution.

## Augments — what the mode adds

- **Reach.** Hebun works opportunities the owner has no time to chase.
- **Memory.** Hebun brings the organization's full remembered past to every proposal.
- **Continuity.** Hebun holds the portfolio in view always, not just at review.
- **Preparation.** Hebun takes ventures to the edge of decision, so the owner spends attention only on the choice itself.
- **Judgment support.** Hebun sharpens options with research, forecasting, and reasoning — so the Director decides better, faster.

The mode's value is entirely in making the Director's own decisions stronger and easier.

## Never replaces — what stays with the Director

- **Authority.** Every committing action ([03 — Director Gates](03-director-gates.md)) is the Director's, always.
- **Ownership.** The ventures, the money, the legal standing, the public name — all the owner's.
- **Final judgment.** Hebun proposes and advises; the Director chooses. A better-learned Hebun ([05 — Learning Loop](05-learning-loop.md)) proposes better — it never chooses instead.
- **Direction.** What to build, what to pursue, what the portfolio becomes — set by the Director. Hebun serves that direction; it does not set it.

## Boundaries against confusion

Personal Enterprise Mode is **not**:

- **Not a reasoning architecture.** It consumes Director Reasoning (Phase 7); it is not that engine.
- **Not an agent architecture.** It orchestrates agents toward business outcomes; it is not the agent framework.
- **Not a runtime architecture.** It sits above runtime as a product capability; it defines no runtime.
- **Not an autonomous business.** It is a partner to an owner, not a self-running enterprise. Remove the Director and the mode has nothing to serve.
- **Not a source of authority.** Its usefulness grows with learning; its authority stays at zero. That asymmetry is permanent and intentional.

## Why the boundary is absolute

An AI business partner with real reach is only safe if the authority line is unmovable. If augmentation could quietly slide into replacement — if enough learning or enough success let Hebun start deciding — the owner would have handed away control without ever choosing to. The boundary prevents exactly that: **however capable Hebun becomes, the Director remains the one who decides.**

The more powerful the mode grows, the more this boundary matters, and the more strictly it holds.
