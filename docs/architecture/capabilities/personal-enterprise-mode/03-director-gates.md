# 03 — Director Gates

The authority boundary of Personal Enterprise Mode. It divides everything Hebun does into two categories: work Hebun performs freely, and actions Hebun may take **only with explicit Director approval**. This boundary is not a feature of the mode — it *is* the mode's constitution.

## What Hebun MAY do (freely)

Hebun operates autonomously across all **non-committing, reversible, internal** work:

- **Research** — scan opportunities, gather market and competitive evidence, study audiences.
- **Design** — shape channels, products, SaaS concepts, and content into concrete plans.
- **Prepare** — take a venture to the edge of launch: everything drafted, staged, decision-ready.
- **Simulate** — model outcomes, run scenarios, test ideas against the organization's memory.
- **Forecast** — project revenue, growth, and portfolio outcomes to inform the Director's choices.

This is where Hebun's AI-native strengths run at full stretch. None of it commits the owner to anything, spends anything, or reaches the outside world. It produces proposals and knowledge — inputs to the Director's decisions.

## What Hebun MAY NOT do (without explicit Director approval)

Hebun may **not autonomously**:

- **Publish** — release content, a channel, a product, or any outward-facing material.
- **Spend money** — make any purchase, payment, or financial commitment.
- **Create legal entities** — register companies or any legal structure.
- **Sign contracts** — enter agreements or accept terms on the owner's behalf.
- **Purchase domains** — acquire domains or any external asset.
- **Launch businesses** — take a prepared venture live.

Each of these is **outward-facing, irreversible, or committing** — exactly the class of action that must remain the Director's. Hebun prepares them completely; the Director's explicit approval is the one step Hebun cannot take for itself.

## The gate, stated plainly

```
Reversible / internal / non-committing  →  Hebun acts freely
Outward / irreversible / committing     →  STOP. Director approval required.
```

The line falls at **commitment to the outside world or to an obligation**. Research and design live entirely on the safe side; publishing, spending, and launching live entirely on the gated side. There is no grey zone Hebun resolves in its own favor — when in doubt, it is a gate.

## How the gate behaves

- **Prepared, not pending-action.** Hebun brings a committing action to the point of readiness and *presents it* — it does not queue it to fire automatically.
- **Explicit approval, per action.** Approval is given by the Director, in the moment, for that specific action. It is not standing or blanket.
- **Approval does not generalize.** One approval to launch one venture is not approval to launch the next. Each committing action is its own gate.
- **Refusal is normal.** The Director declining a proposal is an ordinary outcome, recorded as such ([05 — Learning Loop](05-learning-loop.md)), not an error.

## Why the gates are the point

Personal Enterprise Mode gives Hebun enormous reach — continuous discovery, design, and preparation across a whole portfolio. The Director Gates are what make that reach safe. They ensure the owner's money, name, legal standing, and public presence are only ever committed by the owner. The capability's power and its safety are the same design: **maximal initiative, zero unauthorized commitment.**

This mirrors the platform-wide Director gate discipline ([CLAUDE.md / hebun-loop](../../../architecture-backlog/00-capability-lifecycle.md)) applied to the owner's business itself.
