# 05 — Learning Loop

Personal Enterprise Mode gets better at serving *this* Director over time. The Learning Loop is how: Hebun learns the owner's preferences, patterns, and history so its proposals sharpen with every interaction — **without ever acquiring the owner's authority.** Learning changes what Hebun *proposes*, never what Hebun is *allowed to do*.

This is a product behavior described at the architecture level. It builds on Organizational Memory ([Phase 6](../../memory/README.md)) and the Learning Engine ([backlog 19](../../../architecture-backlog/19-learning-engine.md)); it defines no mechanism here.

## What Hebun learns

### Director preferences
The owner's taste — the kinds of ventures, audiences, risk levels, and styles the Director gravitates toward, and those they consistently avoid. Over time Hebun proposes more of what fits and less of what doesn't.

### Strategic patterns
The recurring shape of the Director's strategy — how they sequence bets, when they double down, what signals move them. Hebun learns to frame proposals in the way the Director actually decides.

### Rejected ideas
Every proposal the Director declines is a lesson. Hebun remembers *what* was rejected and, where discernible, *why* — so it stops re-surfacing the same misses and learns the edges of the owner's interest.

### Successful ventures
What worked — which ventures earned, grew, or delighted the owner — becomes the pattern Hebun looks to repeat and extend. Success teaches the engine where to point.

### Portfolio evolution
How the owner's portfolio changes over time — what was launched, grown, held, retired — so Hebun's proposals fit the portfolio's current shape and trajectory, not a stale picture.

## How it learns

- **From memory, append-first.** Learning reads the durable record of decisions and outcomes ([Phase 6](../../memory/README.md)); it never rewrites the past, only draws patterns from it.
- **From approvals and refusals equally.** Both a "yes" and a "no" are signal. The gate is not just a control point — it is the richest learning surface, teaching Hebun the owner's judgment.
- **Continuously, quietly.** Learning runs alongside the Revenue Engine, steadily improving relevance without demanding the owner's attention.

## The invariant: learning never changes authority

This is the line the Learning Loop must never cross:

- **Learning changes proposals, not permissions.** No amount of learning lets Hebun act on the gated side of the [Director Gate](03-director-gates.md). A well-learned preference makes Hebun *propose* better; it never makes Hebun *decide*.
- **Preference is not pre-authorization.** Learning that the Director "always approves this kind of thing" does **not** become standing approval. Each committing action is still its own gate ([03](03-director-gates.md)). Hebun may say "you've approved similar before"; it may not skip the asking.
- **The Director stays the source of authority.** Learning makes Hebun a better partner. It never makes Hebun the owner. The more Hebun learns, the more useful — and the more strictly gated — it remains.

The Learning Loop's promise: **Hebun grows into the owner's ways without ever growing into the owner's authority.**
