# 07 — Future Evolution

How Personal Enterprise Mode is expected to evolve and integrate over time — **at the vision level only**. No implementation, no prompts, no APIs, no databases, no runtime. Each integration deepens the mode's capability while preserving its governing principle: augment the Director, never replace ([06 — Boundaries](06-boundaries.md)).

Each area below is future work, behind its own Director gate, following the [Capability Lifecycle](../../../architecture-backlog/00-capability-lifecycle.md).

## Integration with Director Reasoning

As Director Reasoning (Phase 7) matures, Personal Enterprise Mode grows sharper. Deeper reasoning means better-filtered opportunities, more insightful forecasts, and proposals that reflect the Director's judgment more faithfully. The mode consumes reasoning as it deepens; it never modifies it. The trajectory: from surfacing opportunities to *understanding* them the way the Director would — while every commitment stays gated.

## Integration with Enterprise Modes

Personal Enterprise Mode is the first of a possible **family of enterprise modes**. Where the personal mode serves a single owner's portfolio, future modes could serve other contexts — a small team, a specific vertical, a particular business posture — each a product capability built on the same foundations (graph, memory, reasoning, orchestration) with its own scope.

The personal mode establishes the pattern the family follows: composed on top of the baseline, governed by Director Gates, augmenting rather than replacing the human authority at its center. New enterprise modes extend the reach of the concept; none relaxes the boundary.

## Integration with Autonomous Operations

The furthest horizon. As autonomous capabilities emerge across the platform, Personal Enterprise Mode could operate more of the standing work end-to-end — deeper continuous monitoring, richer preparation, tighter optimization loops.

This horizon is where the boundary matters most. Greater autonomy in *preparation and operation* must never become autonomy in *commitment*. Even at its most autonomous, the mode's outward, irreversible, and committing actions remain gated to the Director. Autonomous operations extend how much Hebun can *do on the safe side of the gate*; they never move the gate. The more the mode can operate on its own, the more absolutely the [Director Gates](03-director-gates.md) hold.

## The invariant across all evolution

Through every future integration, one thing does not change:

- The mode composes the platform's layers; it never modifies them.
- Committing actions stay gated to the Director, per action, without exception.
- Capability grows; authority stays with the owner.
- Augmentation deepens; replacement never happens.

Personal Enterprise Mode can become far more capable over time. It cannot become the Director. That is the fixed point every future version is built around.
