# 06 — Future Evolution

Where the Director Interface is headed — **at the vision level only**. No implementation, no APIs, no runtime, no architecture changes. Each step deepens the partnership while preserving the one thing that never changes: the Director's authority.

## Toward a Jarvis-like executive interface

The long-term vision for the Director Interface is a **Jarvis-like executive partner** — an interface so capable, present, and anticipatory that the Director operates their entire organization through natural collaboration with Hebun. Not a tool the Director uses, but a partner the Director works with: always available, always aware, always one step ahead in surfacing what matters.

Concretely, the vision points toward an interface that is:

- **Ambient** — present across every modality ([03](03-multimodal-interface.md)), reachable by voice, glance, or word wherever the Director is, without friction.
- **Anticipatory** — proactively surfacing the right briefing, warning, or recommendation before the Director asks ([04](04-proactive-assistance.md)), because it understands the Director's patterns and context.
- **Conversational at depth** — able to discuss the whole organization fluidly, moving from a question to a decision to an instruction as naturally as talking to a trusted chief of staff.
- **Continuous** — a standing partner in the Director's day, not a session that starts and stops.

This is the "Jarvis" idea in its true sense: not autonomy, but an extraordinarily capable interface to an intelligence that serves an owner who remains firmly in command.

## The line that never moves: Director Authority

The critical distinction — and the reason a Jarvis-like interface is safe to pursue: **Jarvis-like capability is not Jarvis-like autonomy.** However ambient, anticipatory, and capable the interface becomes, it holds no authority of its own ([05 — Boundaries](05-boundaries.md)).

- A more anticipatory interface surfaces better recommendations — it never accepts them for the Director.
- A more ambient interface makes approval frictionless — it never makes approval automatic. A one-word approval is still the Director deciding.
- A more capable interface deepens collaboration — it never converts collaboration into the interface acting alone.

The interface may evolve toward feeling like a mind at the Director's side. It never evolves toward being the one in charge. Every irreversible action, at every stage of this evolution, still passes through the Director's explicit approval ([Director Authority](../../director-reasoning/05-director-authority.md)).

## The invariant across all evolution

- The interface consumes reasoning, memory, and orchestration; it replaces none of them.
- The interface presents and relays; it never decides or acts on its own.
- The interface's capability may grow toward a Jarvis-like partner; its authority stays at zero.
- The Director remains the source of authority and the only one who commits the organization to irreversible acts.

The Director Interface can become the most capable executive interface imaginable. It cannot become the executive. That fixed point — a brilliant, ever-present partner to an owner who stays in command — is what every future version is built around.
