# 02 — Interaction Model

How the Director and Hebun collaborate through the interface. These are the **modes of interaction** the Director Interface supports — described as product behaviors, not implementation. No prompts, no workflows-as-code, no APIs.

Every mode presents what the deeper systems produce and relays the Director's intent back; none of them holds authority or acts autonomously ([05 — Boundaries](05-boundaries.md)).

## Natural conversation

The Director interacts with Hebun in ordinary language — asking, exploring, directing, thinking out loud. Conversation is the connective tissue of the interface: the Director can move fluidly from a question to a decision to an instruction without changing tools or modes. Hebun answers from reasoning and memory; the conversation is the surface, not the intelligence behind it.

## Voice interaction

The same conversation, spoken. The Director can talk to Hebun and be answered — hands-free, on the move, in the moments between other work. Voice is a presentation of the interaction, not a separate capability: what is said flows to the same reasoning and returns through the same judgment. (The Voice Layer as a channel is a related backlog item; here it is one modality of the executive interface.)

## Visual dashboards

The Director sees the state of their world — portfolio, ventures, health, alerts — rendered visually. Dashboards make the organization's current picture legible at a glance, surfacing what reasoning and memory hold in a form an executive can absorb quickly. They present; they do not compute the underlying state.

## Executive briefings

Hebun composes and presents concise, decision-oriented summaries — what changed, what matters, what needs attention. Briefings are the interface's way of respecting the Director's time: instead of the Director digging, Hebun brings the essential picture forward, drawn from memory and shaped by reasoning. (This surfaces capabilities like the Executive Brief Generator through the interface.)

## Decision requests

When a choice is genuinely the Director's, the interface brings it forward as a clean **decision request** — the options, the trade-offs, the recommendation, and its confidence, laid out so the Director can decide well and quickly. A decision request is reasoning's judgment ([decision principles](../../director-reasoning/04-decision-principles.md)) presented for the Director's choice. It asks; it does not pre-decide.

## Approval workflows

For anything irreversible or committing, the interface carries the Director's **explicit approval** — presenting the prepared action, capturing the Director's yes or no, and relaying that decision onward. Approval workflows are the interface's expression of the Director Gate: the action is prepared beneath, presented here, and proceeds only on the Director's explicit approval ([Director Authority](../../director-reasoning/05-director-authority.md)). The interface is where approval is *asked and given* — never where it is assumed.

---

## The shape of every interaction

Across all six modes, the pattern holds:

1. **Deeper systems produce** — reasoning judges, memory recalls, orchestration prepares.
2. **The interface presents** — as conversation, voice, dashboard, briefing, decision request, or approval prompt.
3. **The Director directs or decides** — and the interface relays that intent back down.

The interface is the two-way surface of the partnership: everything Hebun knows and judges flows *up* to the Director legibly, and everything the Director wants flows *down* clearly. It is where collaboration happens — and where authority stays with the Director.
