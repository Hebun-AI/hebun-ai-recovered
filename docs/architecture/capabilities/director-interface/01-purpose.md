# 01 — Purpose

## The executive operating layer

Director Interface is the layer where the Director *operates* Hebun. Beneath it, Hebun holds structure, memory, judgment, and the ability to coordinate work. But none of that reaches the Director except through an interface — and the quality of that interface determines whether Hebun feels like a genuine operating partner or a system to be queried.

Director Interface is that interface, elevated to an **executive operating layer**: not a place to type questions, but the surface through which an executive runs their organization with an AI partner. It is where the Director's attention meets Hebun's capability.

## Why it is a distinct capability

Hebun's depth is invisible without a surface worthy of it. Reasoning can produce excellent judgment; memory can hold the whole organizational past; orchestration can coordinate real work — but if the Director experiences all of this through a thin chat box, the partnership is crippled at the last inch.

The Director Interface exists so that the last inch is not the weak link. It is designed as a first-class capability because:

- **The Director's time is the scarcest resource.** The interface must make every interaction efficient — surfacing what matters, hiding what doesn't, asking only when a decision is genuinely the Director's.
- **Executive work is multimodal and continuous.** A Director does not sit at one screen; they move, travel, glance, speak. The interface must meet them wherever they are ([03 — Multimodal Interface](03-multimodal-interface.md)).
- **Collaboration is two-way.** The Director directs Hebun; Hebun proactively surfaces to the Director ([04 — Proactive Assistance](04-proactive-assistance.md)). The interface carries both directions.

## What the interface is for

- **Present** — surface Hebun's judgments, briefings, and portfolio state clearly and at the right moment.
- **Converse** — let the Director ask, explore, and direct in natural interaction.
- **Decide** — bring decisions to the Director cleanly, with the reasoning attached.
- **Approve** — carry the Director's explicit approval into action for anything irreversible.
- **Alert** — proactively notify, summarize, warn, and remind, without ever acting unbidden.

## What the interface is not

- **Not a chatbot.** It is an executive operating surface, not a conversation toy.
- **Not an agent.** It does not act autonomously; it presents and relays.
- **Not reasoning.** It shows judgment; it does not form it.
- **Not memory.** It surfaces history; it does not hold or alter it.
- **Not an authority.** It carries the Director's decisions; it makes none of its own.

The purpose, stated once: **be the executive operating layer through which the Director experiences and directs everything Hebun can do — accessible, proactive, and always subordinate to the Director's authority.**
