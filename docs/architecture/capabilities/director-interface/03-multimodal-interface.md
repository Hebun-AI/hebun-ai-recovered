# 03 — Multimodal Interface

The Director Interface meets the Director wherever they are, through whatever surface fits the moment. This document describes the **modalities** the interface is envisioned to support — as product vision, not implementation. No device APIs, no runtime, no channel code.

The principle across all modalities: **one partner, many surfaces.** The Director experiences a single Hebun through whichever modality suits the moment; the modality is presentation, never a separate intelligence.

## Voice

Spoken interaction — the Director talks, Hebun answers. Voice fits the moments between other work: driving, walking, thinking aloud. It makes Hebun reachable hands-free and continuously, turning idle moments into working ones. Voice is a modality of the same interface, not a different assistant.

## Text

Written interaction — precise, quotable, reviewable. Text fits deliberate work: reading a briefing, composing a considered instruction, reviewing a decision request in detail. It is the modality of record, where exactness matters more than immediacy.

## Dashboard

Visual, at-a-glance state — the Director *sees* their world. The dashboard fits situational awareness: portfolio health, alerts, what's running, what changed. It is the modality of overview, absorbing complex state into a legible picture the Director can scan in seconds.

## Mobile

The interface in the Director's pocket — the full partner, portable. Mobile fits the executive who is rarely at a desk: approvals on the move, briefings on the go, a glance at the portfolio anywhere. It carries the interaction model ([02](02-interaction-model.md)) into the Director's daily movement.

## Wearable

The lightest touch — a glance, a nudge, a spoken word on the wrist. Wearable fits the most time-sensitive, lowest-friction moments: a warning that can't wait, a one-tap approval, a brief spoken exchange. It is the modality of immediacy, present without demanding attention.

## Future interfaces

The modality list is open. As new surfaces emerge — ambient, spatial, or forms not yet common — the Director Interface is designed to extend to them. Each new modality is another surface onto the same Hebun, added without changing what Hebun is. The interface grows in reach; the partner behind it stays one.

---

## What multimodality does and does not change

- **Does change:** *how* the Director reaches Hebun — the surface, the moment, the friction. More modalities mean Hebun is available in more of the Director's life.
- **Does not change:** *what* Hebun is or *what authority the interface holds*. Every modality presents the same reasoning, the same memory, the same recommendations — and every modality routes irreversible actions through the same explicit Director approval. A one-tap approval on a wearable is still an explicit approval; a spoken "yes" is still the Director deciding.

Multimodality expands access; it never expands the interface's authority. However many surfaces Hebun reaches the Director through, the boundary is identical on all of them: present and relay, never decide or commit.
