# 07 — Future Evolution

How Director Verification is expected to evolve — **at the architecture level only**. No algorithms, no prompts, no implementation, no runtime, no execution. The verification topics defined here are the stable frame; evolution deepens *how well each is performed*, never *whether the boundaries hold*.

Each direction below is future work, behind its own Director gate, following the [Capability Lifecycle](../../architecture-backlog/00-capability-lifecycle.md).

## Deeper verification, same architecture

Future verification engines will perform each topic more capably — sharper self-critique, more thorough consistency validation, more penetrating risk verification, more rigorous decision assurance, more precise readiness judgment. But they will still follow the **same architecture**: independently consume reasoning, planning, and decision outputs; identify contradictions and missing evidence; validate cross-phase consistency; verify organizational alignment; determine readiness — read-only, non-executing, authority-preserving. A verification engine that rewrote the chain, executed, or rubber-stamped would not be more advanced — it would be broken. The architecture and its boundaries are the invariant every future engine inherits.

## Completing the Director Intelligence chain

Verification is the final pre-execution layer of Director Intelligence. Together, Phases 7A–7F form the full chain: **why** the Director reasons, **how** it thinks, the **mechanisms** that realize thinking, how reasoning becomes **plans**, how plans become **decisions**, and how decisions are **independently verified** before the Director considers execution. The natural next phase is **Execution** — running approved, verified work — which is a separate concern under the Director's authority, behind its own gate. Verification hands the Director a readiness verdict; it never runs what it verified.

## Integration with memory and learning

As Organizational Memory and the Learning Engine deepen ([Phase 6](../memory/README.md), [backlog 19](../../architecture-backlog/19-learning-engine.md)), verification learns from outcomes — which verified decisions succeeded, which slipped through, which "not ready" calls were vindicated. This sharpens future critique without changing verification's role: it learns to critique better, never to critique less. And its findings, recorded as memory, feed the organization's growing experience.

## The invariant across all evolution

Through every future version of Director Verification:

- Verification **independently reviews the whole chain** — reasoning, planning, decision — read-only.
- Verification **surfaces contradictions, gaps, and misalignment honestly** — never suppressed, never rubber-stamped.
- Verification **produces a readiness verdict** but **never executes**, **never modifies the chain**, and **never bypasses Director Authority**.
- Capability may grow without limit; the **independent-critic, defer-to-the-Director structure** does not change.

Director Verification can become far more capable. It cannot execute work, rewrite the chain it audits, or decide for the Director. That fixed foundation is what every future verification engine is built around.
