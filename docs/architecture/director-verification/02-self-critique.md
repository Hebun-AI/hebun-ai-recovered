# 02 — Self-Critique

## Purpose

Self-Critique is where verification **turns critical evaluation inward on the chain's own output** — deliberately looking for what is wrong with the reasoning, plan, and decision, rather than confirming they are right. It is the disciplined search for one's own errors: the missing evidence, the unexamined assumption, the leap that was never justified. Self-critique is what keeps Director Intelligence honest about its own work.

## Architectural role

Self-Critique is the core stance of the verification layer — the posture the other verification topics (consistency, risk, assurance) apply in their specific domains. It is where the reasoning mechanisms ([Phase 7C](../director-reasoning-mechanisms/README.md)) are turned back on the reasoning itself: hypothesis generation used to imagine how the conclusion could be wrong; evidence evaluation used to test whether the conclusion actually holds. It embodies the [first principle](../director-reasoning/02-first-principles.md) *evidence before conclusion* as an audit rather than a production discipline.

## Inputs

- The **outputs of Reasoning, Planning, and Decision** — the work to be critiqued.
- The **evidence** the chain rested on ([knowledge layers](../memory/README.md)) — to test whether the conclusions are actually supported.
- The **assumptions** the chain made, surfaced for challenge.

## Outputs

- A **critique** — identified weaknesses: unsupported claims, missing evidence, unexamined assumptions, unjustified leaps.
- **Challenged assumptions** — the load-bearing assumptions named and tested, with the result.
- Where the chain **holds up**, an explicit account of *why* it survives critique — not a rubber stamp, but a reasoned confirmation.

## Boundaries

- Self-Critique **critiques; it does not rewrite**. It identifies flaws in the chain; correcting them is the producing layer's job, and the Director's call ([verification principles](01-verification-principles.md)).
- It **seeks flaws honestly** — its purpose is to find what is wrong, not to justify what was produced. A self-critique that set out to confirm would defeat itself.
- It **produces no action** and **defines no method** — this document establishes that self-critique exists and its role, not any critique technique.

## Future direction

Future verification engines may self-critique more penetratingly — imagining more failure modes, testing assumptions more rigorously, catching subtler unsupported leaps. The discipline is fixed: look inward for error honestly, rewrite nothing, confirm only what survives real challenge. Penetration grows; the honesty holds.
