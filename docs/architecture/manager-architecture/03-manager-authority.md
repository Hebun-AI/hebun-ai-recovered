# 03 — Manager Authority

## Purpose

Manager Authority defines **the delegated authority a Manager holds and its bounds**. A Manager is an authority seat, and this document says exactly where that authority comes from, how far it reaches, and how it stays revocable — so a Manager can govern a department without ever escaping the Director.

## Architectural role

Where the model ([02](02-manager-model.md)) defines the seat, authority defines *what the seat may do*. Delegation ([04](04-delegation-model.md)) hands a portion of this authority downward; oversight, reporting, and escalation ([05](05-oversight-and-reporting.md), [06](06-escalation-governance.md)) operate within it. Authority is the source the rest of the Manager's governance draws on.

## The model

### A Manager's authority is delegated, never originated
A Manager receives its authority from above — from the enterprise level, itself delegated from the Director ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). A Manager creates no authority. Its authority is always traceable up to the Director.

### A Manager's authority is bounded by its grant
A Manager may act only within the authority delegated to it, and never beyond. The grant defines the ceiling; a Manager cannot raise it on its own. Where a matter exceeds the grant, the Manager escalates ([escalation & governance](06-escalation-governance.md)) rather than overreaching.

### A Manager's authority is bounded by its department
A Manager's authority reaches over the one department it governs and no further ([manager principles](01-manager-principles.md)). It holds no authority in another department's domain. Department and authority share a boundary — a Manager is authoritative exactly where its department is accountable.

### A Manager's authority is revocable
Any delegation to a Manager can be withdrawn — by the Director, or by the enterprise level acting within its own delegated authority ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). No Manager holds authority the Director cannot reclaim.

### Committing authority stays with the Director
A Manager's authority lets it be *accountable* for outcomes that include committing or irreversible actions, but the commitment itself rests on the Director's approval ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md), consistent with [execution principles](../director-execution/01-execution-principles.md)). A Manager never manufactures a committing authority the Director did not grant.

### Authority is paired with accountability
A Manager's authority matches its accountability for the department: authorized to the extent it answers for the domain, accountable to the extent it is authorized. Neither exceeds the other.

## Preserving Director Authority

- A Manager sits **beneath** the enterprise level and the Director — never at or above them.
- Its authority is **delegated, bounded, and revocable** — traceable up, reclaimable at will.
- **Committing actions** rest on Director approval — no Manager grant substitutes for it.

This is how a Manager "preserves Director Authority": structurally, by construction, at the seat.

## Inputs

- The **manager model** ([02](02-manager-model.md)) — the seat that holds the authority.
- The **enterprise authority model** ([9A](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)) — the source and rules of delegation.

## Outputs

- A **defined authority envelope** — what a Manager may do, within what bound, revocable when — that delegation, oversight, and escalation rely on.

## Boundaries

- Grants authority to **the Manager seat, not a concrete manager** — it names no occupant.
- Defines **no decision, plan, or work** — it is the authority under which Director Intelligence decides and plans and Execution acts, never those acts themselves.
- Describes **no runtime or mechanism** — only the structure of authority.

## Future direction

As human and AI managers fill the seat, each receives this same bounded, revocable, department-scoped authority. The authority model does not change to admit them — it already defines how any Manager is authorized. Different occupants, same envelope: authority from the Director, bounded, revocable, committing-actions-gated.
