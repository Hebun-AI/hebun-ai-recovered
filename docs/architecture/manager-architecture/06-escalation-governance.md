# 06 — Escalation & Governance

## Purpose

Escalation & Governance defines **how a Manager escalates what exceeds its authority** and **how a Manager stays governed and traceable**. Escalation is a Manager's correct response to a limit; governance is the enterprise-wide regime that holds a Manager to its bounds. Together they keep a Manager from ever overreaching and make its whole governance auditable.

## Architectural role

Escalation is the sharp end of reporting ([05](05-oversight-and-reporting.md)) — what a Manager raises when a matter passes its grant. Governance is the enterprise regime ([enterprise governance](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)) applied at the Manager seat. This document closes the Manager's governance loop: authority ([03](03-manager-authority.md)) and delegation ([04](04-delegation-model.md)) define what a Manager may do; escalation and governance define what it does at the edge and how it is held accountable.

## The model — escalation

### A Manager escalates rather than overreaches
When a matter exceeds a Manager's delegated authority, the Manager raises it to the level above — ultimately toward the Director — instead of acting beyond its grant ([manager authority](03-manager-authority.md)). Escalation, not overreach, is the correct response to a limit.

### What a Manager escalates
- **Beyond-authority matters** — anything requiring authority the Manager was not granted.
- **Committing actions** — anything requiring the Director's approval to commit ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)).
- **Cross-department matters** — anything that reaches into another department's domain, which coordinates through the hierarchy ([department coordination](../department-architecture/05-department-coordination.md)).
- **Reasoning, planning, or decisions** — anything needing Director Intelligence, which the Manager routes rather than resolves ([manager principles](01-manager-principles.md)).

### Escalation preserves accountability
Escalating does not shed a Manager's ownership of its department's outcomes; it is how the Manager responsibly handles what it cannot authorize itself ([oversight & reporting](05-oversight-and-reporting.md)). A Manager stays accountable for raising the right things at the right time.

## The model — governance

### A Manager falls under enterprise governance — no exceptions
There is one governance regime, and every Manager is inside it ([enterprise governance](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)). A Manager invents no governance of its own and is never exempt.

### Governance aligns with Director Authority
A Manager's governance expresses the Director's standards at the seat; it never sits above the Director ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). Where a Manager's action would exceed governance or its grant, governance blocks it and forces escalation.

### A Manager is fully traceable
A Manager's authority, delegations, oversight, reports, and escalations can all be reconstructed and audited. Traceability is structural — a property of the seat, not of any run of work ([department governance](../department-architecture/06-department-governance.md)).

## What escalation & governance preserve

- **Director Authority** — enforced at the seat; beyond-grant and committing matters escalate to it.
- **Bounded, revocable delegation** — a Manager never exceeds or over-delegates its grant.
- **Enterprise governance** — one regime, reaching the Manager, no exemptions.
- **Traceability** — the Manager's whole governance is auditable.

## Inputs

- The **manager authority and delegation models** ([03](03-manager-authority.md), [04](04-delegation-model.md)) — the bounds that define what must escalate.
- The **enterprise governance regime** ([9A](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)) — the standards applied at the seat.

## Outputs

- **Escalations** — beyond-grant matters raised upward toward the Director.
- A **governance and traceability frame** — the Manager held to its bounds and made auditable.

## Boundaries

- Escalates and governs **the Manager seat, not a concrete manager** — it names no occupant.
- Performs **no reasoning, work, or orchestration** — it raises and records; it does not resolve or execute.
- Describes **no runtime or mechanism** — only the structure of escalation and governance.

## Future direction

As human and AI managers fill the seat, escalation and governance apply unchanged — the moment a Manager exists, it escalates at its limit and falls under the enterprise regime. Governance may deepen its checks over time ([future evolution](07-future-evolution.md)), but it stays enterprise-anchored, Director-topped, and traceable. Managers multiply; none escapes its bounds.
