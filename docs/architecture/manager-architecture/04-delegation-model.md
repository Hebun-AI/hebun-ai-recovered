# 04 — Delegation Model

## Purpose

The Delegation Model defines **how a Manager delegates authority downward** and **the boundaries of that delegation**. A Manager governs a department in part by handing bounded authority to the specialist seats beneath it. This document says how that delegation works and where it stops — so delegation extends a Manager's reach without ever exceeding, or escaping, its own grant.

## Architectural role

Where authority ([03](03-manager-authority.md)) defines what a Manager holds, delegation defines what a Manager *passes down*. Oversight ([05](05-oversight-and-reporting.md)) watches what was delegated; escalation ([06](06-escalation-governance.md)) handles what delegation cannot cover. Delegation is how a Manager's governance reaches the work without the Manager performing it.

## The model

### A Manager delegates only authority it holds
A Manager cannot delegate an authority it was not itself granted ([manager authority](03-manager-authority.md)). Delegation passes down a portion of the Manager's own bounded authority — never more. The grant a specialist seat receives is a subset of the Manager's grant.

### Delegation is downward and narrowing
A Manager delegates to the specialist seats within its department ([enterprise organizational model](../enterprise-organization/02-organizational-model.md)). Authority narrows as it descends: each delegated grant is bounded, and the sum of what is delegated never exceeds what the Manager holds. Delegation flows down the hierarchy, never sideways into another department or upward.

### Delegation transfers authority, not ownership or accountability
When a Manager delegates, the specialist seat gains bounded authority to act within the department's domain — but the Manager still **owns** the department's outcomes and remains **accountable** upward for them ([manager principles](01-manager-principles.md)). Delegation extends reach; it does not shed responsibility. A Manager answers for what it delegated.

### Delegation is revocable
A Manager can withdraw a delegation it made, just as the Manager's own authority is revocable from above ([manager authority](03-manager-authority.md)). No delegated grant is permanent or beyond recall.

### Committing actions are not delegated away
A Manager cannot delegate the authority to commit what only the Director may approve. Delegated grants may make a specialist seat *responsible* for work that includes committing actions, but the commitment still rests on Director approval ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). Delegation never manufactures committing authority.

## Delegation boundaries

- **No delegation exceeds the Manager's grant** — the ceiling is the Manager's own authority.
- **No delegation crosses into another department** — delegation stays within the governed department.
- **No delegation transfers ownership or accountability** — those stay with the Manager.
- **No delegation manufactures committing authority** — that stays behind Director approval.

## Inputs

- The **manager authority envelope** ([03](03-manager-authority.md)) — the ceiling on what can be delegated.
- The **department's specialist seats** ([9A model](../enterprise-organization/02-organizational-model.md), [9B model](../department-architecture/02-department-model.md)) — the places delegation reaches.

## Outputs

- A **defined delegation structure** — what a Manager may pass down, to which seats, within what bound, revocable when, with ownership retained — that oversight and escalation rely on.

## Boundaries

- Delegates to **specialist seats, not concrete specialists** — it names no occupant.
- Defines **no work and no reasoning** — it passes authority, not tasks or judgment.
- Describes **no runtime or mechanism** — only the structure of delegation.

## Future direction

As specialist agents and humans fill the seats beneath a Manager, each receives a bounded, revocable delegation. The delegation model does not change to admit them — it already defines how any occupant is granted authority. More delegations, same rules: downward, narrowing, ownership retained, committing-actions-gated.
