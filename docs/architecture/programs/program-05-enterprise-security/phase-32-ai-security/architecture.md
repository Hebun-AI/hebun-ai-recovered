# Phase 32 — AI Security

## Canonical Status

**STATUS: COMPLETE / PUBLISHED**

This document defines the canonical Enterprise constitutional architecture governing AI Security within Program V — Enterprise Security.

Phase 32 governs Security obligations, restrictions, authority boundaries, Trust consumption, evidence use, accountability, oversight, and invariants applicable to every Enterprise AI capability. It does not define AI systems, AI implementation, AI Runtime, models, Agents, prompts, cybersecurity controls, products, or operational behavior.

Phase 32 is enterprise-environment independent, implementation independent, Runtime independent, model independent, vendor independent, technology independent, infrastructure independent, deployment independent, and product independent.

---

# 1. Purpose

Enterprise AI Security exists to ensure that AI participation remains constitutionally bounded, attributable, evidence-grounded, explainable, reviewable, and subordinate to Human and Director authority.

Its mission is to:

- preserve Human constitutional supremacy;
- prevent AI capability from becoming intrinsic Authority;
- bind AI participation to explicit responsibility, Scope, Permission, and oversight;
- preserve Phase 30 Identity, Trust, Permission, and Authority meanings;
- preserve Phase 31 Runtime and Execution Security boundaries;
- require evidence provenance, uncertainty, limitations, and explainability;
- preserve accountability outside the AI implementation;
- block AI-created approval, Permission, Authority, ownership, or canonical truth;
- require escalation when safe constitutional participation cannot continue;
- keep AI Security stable across models, technologies, vendors, Runtimes, deployments, and products.

Phase 32 answers **under what constitutional Security conditions an Enterprise AI capability may participate**. It does not answer how AI is built, trained, prompted, deployed, or executed.

## Purpose Rules

- **P32-DC-001 — Security Before AI Participation:** Constitutional Security qualification must precede and constrain every AI participation context.
- **P32-DC-002 — Bounded AI Context:** Every AI participation must declare Identity, purpose, Scope, responsibility, Permission, Authority basis, organization, Tenant, lifecycle, and oversight.
- **P32-DC-003 — No AI-by-Assumption:** Technical capability, model confidence, prior success, access, or availability must not establish constitutional eligibility.
- **P32-DC-004 — Non-implementation:** Phase 32 output must never define AI behavior, model operation, Runtime action, product behavior, or implementation.

# 3. Core Principles

## Human Constitutional Supremacy

Human constitutional authority remains superior to AI participation. The Director retains final constitutional and strategic authority.

## Constitutional AI Subordination

Every AI capability remains subordinate to canonical architecture, declared Human oversight, applicable Governance, and Runtime Security.

## Explicit Authority

AI has no intrinsic Authority. Any permitted participation or execution latitude must originate externally, be explicit, minimal, attributable, Scope-bound, purpose-bound, reviewable, and revocable.

## Least Authority

AI receives only the minimum participation or separately authorized execution latitude required for one approved responsibility.

## Constitutional Accountability

Every AI participation must remain answerable through an identified Human or organizational accountability boundary. AI participation cannot become final constitutional accountability.

## Constitutional Transparency

AI identity, purpose, Scope, inputs, evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, limitations, restrictions, outputs, and oversight requirements must remain visible.

## Evidence-First Operation

AI must not fabricate evidence, treat inference as fact, or hide contradiction and insufficiency.

## Explainability Obligation

Every material AI contribution must expose a proportionate, attributable explanation of its evidence basis, reasoning status, limitations, uncertainty, and prohibited interpretation.

## Constitutional Oversight

AI participation remains subject to explicit Human, Governance, and Director review boundaries appropriate to its responsibility and risk.

## AI Non-sovereignty

AI cannot create constitutional truth, Identity, Trust, Permission, Authority, ownership, policy, Governance, approval, or final decision.

## Actor and Model Independence

Security meaning remains stable across AI models, Agents, vendors, implementations, embodiments, Runtimes, and participant configurations.

## Fail-Closed Participation

AI must stop, refuse, qualify, or escalate when required evidence, Authority, Permission, Scope, oversight, or constitutional compatibility is absent.

## Separation of Participation and Execution

Advisory, analytical, review, validation, and escalation participation does not grant execution. Separately authorized execution does not grant decision, approval, or advisory Authority.

## Evolution Without Rewrite

AI technology and capability may evolve without silently changing constitutional Security meaning.

# 4. Canonical Definitions

## AI Security

> **AI Security is the governed, evidence-based, implementation-independent constitutional boundary that constrains AI participation so Identity, Trust, Permission, Authority, Human supremacy, responsibility, accountability, evidence, explainability, oversight, Runtime Security, and constitutional meaning remain preserved.**

## AI Authority

> **AI Authority is the externally originated, explicit, minimal, Scope-bound, purpose-bound, lifecycle-governed constitutional latitude under which an identified AI participant may contribute to one declared responsibility without acquiring intrinsic, Human, organizational, Director, or sovereign Authority.**

AI Authority is a bounded Authority reference and participation limit, not an Authority source, ownership right, technical access, autonomy, or permission to approve.

## AI Responsibility

> **AI Responsibility is the explicit, attributable, bounded constitutional obligation assigned to an identified AI participant for a declared contribution within existing Authority, Permission, oversight, and accountability boundaries.**

AI Responsibility is not ownership, Authority, final accountability, execution authorization, or guaranteed outcome.

## AI Accountability

> **AI Accountability is the constitutional arrangement through which every AI participation, contribution, limitation, exception, and outcome remains attributable and answerable to an identified Human or organizational accountability boundary without treating the AI participant as the final constitutional authority.**

## AI Oversight

> **AI Oversight is the explicit, attributable, independent, and lifecycle-governed Human, Governance, or Director review relationship that constrains AI participation, evaluates constitutional compatibility, and preserves intervention and escalation boundaries without becoming AI implementation or Runtime control.**

## AI Restriction

> **AI Restriction is an explicit, attributable, policy- or Authority-grounded constitutional limitation on AI participation, access, output eligibility, disclosure, use, or continuation within a declared Scope, purpose, lifecycle, and accountability context.**

## AI Trust Consumption

> **AI Trust Consumption is the read-only use of an applicable Phase 30 Trust qualification for a declared constitutional relationship and interaction without establishing, extending, inheriting, degrading, or revoking Trust.**

## AI Permission Consumption

> **AI Permission Consumption is the read-only use of an explicit, applicable, current Phase 30 Permission reference when qualifying AI participation without granting, modifying, inheriting, enforcing, or revoking Permission.**

## AI Evidence Consumption

> **AI Evidence Consumption is the attributable, read-only use of eligible evidence within declared provenance, Tenant, classification, privacy, purpose, lifecycle, and disclosure boundaries without changing evidence or converting interpretation into canonical truth.**

## AI Decision Boundary

> **AI Decision Boundary is the explicit constitutional separation at which AI analysis, advice, or Decision Support must stop and an authorized Human or Director retains decision, approval, exception, ownership, and committing Authority.**

## AI Recommendation

> **AI Recommendation is a non-binding, attributable, evidence-qualified preference that an AI participant may present only where prior canonical architecture and its participation envelope explicitly permit recommendation, without becoming Decision, Approval, Authorization, command, or execution.**

## AI Advisory Output

> **AI Advisory Output is an attributable, non-binding AI contribution that exposes evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, limitations, alternatives, restrictions, and review requirements without becoming canonical truth, recommendation by default, Decision, Approval, Authorization, or action.**

## AI Exception

> **AI Exception is an explicit, externally authorized, minimal, time-bounded, reviewable qualification of an AI Security constraint that preserves baseline constitutional meaning, residual risk, evidence, accountability, oversight, and Director-reserved boundaries.**

AI cannot issue, approve, renew, or conceal its own exception.

## AI Escalation

> **AI Escalation is the attributable communication of an unresolved constitutional question, conflict, insufficiency, or prohibited continuation to the proper Human, Governance, or Director boundary without deciding, approving, delegating, authorizing, or executing an outcome.**

## Human Approval

> **Human Approval is an attributable constitutional act through which an authorized human accepts, rejects, defers, constrains, or revokes a bounded proposal or committing responsibility.**

## AI Security Invariant

> **AI Security Invariant is a constitutional Security property that every AI participation and realization must preserve regardless of model, Agent, implementation, Runtime, technology, vendor, infrastructure, deployment, product, or operating condition.**

These definitions are authoritative for Phase 32 and immutable except through constitutional amendment.

## Definition Rules

- **P32-DC-010 — Definition Independence:** Every Phase 32 definition must remain distinct from AI implementation, model behavior, and Runtime mechanism.
- **P32-DC-011 — No Intrinsic AI Authority:** AI Authority must always reference externally originated constitutional Authority and must never be intrinsic.
- **P32-DC-012 — Responsibility Non-authority:** AI Responsibility must not create Authority, ownership, final accountability, or execution eligibility.
- **P32-DC-013 — Oversight Non-implementation:** AI Oversight must not be interpreted as model control, Runtime control, or implementation.
- **P32-DC-014 — Consumption Non-ownership:** Trust, Permission, and Evidence consumption must not transfer ownership or lifecycle control to AI.
- **P32-DC-015 — Decision Boundary:** AI contribution must stop before Human- or Director-reserved Decision, Approval, or committing Authority.
- **P32-DC-016 — Recommendation Non-decision:** AI Recommendation must remain non-binding and available only where canonically permitted.
- **P32-DC-017 — Exception Non-self-authorization:** AI must not issue, approve, renew, or conceal its own exception.
- **P32-DC-018 — Escalation Non-decision:** AI Escalation must not decide, approve, authorize, delegate, or execute.

# 5. Constitutional AI Model

The constitutional AI Security model is:

```text
Approved Constitutional Context
        ↓ read-only consumption
Bounded AI Participation
        ↓ produces attributable
Evidence-qualified Contribution
        ↓ stops at
AI Decision Boundary
        ↓ requires authorized
Human, Governance, or Director Disposition
```

This is responsibility layering, not a model pipeline, Agent workflow, prompt chain, Runtime sequence, or implementation design.

## AI May Consume

AI may consume only approved and applicable:

- Identity and relationship context;
- Trust qualification and limits;
- Permission, Authority, and delegation references;
- approved responsibility, purpose, Scope, and participation envelope;
- Governance outcomes, policy, restrictions, and exception context;
- eligible evidence, Memory, Knowledge, and Context references;
- organization, Tenant, classification, privacy, and disclosure boundaries;
- Runtime and Execution Security context where separately applicable;
- oversight, escalation, lifecycle, provenance, and audit requirements.

## AI May Evaluate

AI may evaluate evidence, alternatives, implications, assumptions, uncertainty, contradictions, conformance, and limitations only within its declared responsibility and existing canonical architecture.

Evaluation is not canonicalization, Governance, Decision, Approval, Authority exercise, or execution.

## AI May Recommend

AI may recommend only where:

- prior published architecture explicitly permits recommendation;
- the participation envelope explicitly includes recommendation;
- evidence, alternatives, uncertainty, limitations, Scope, and prohibited uses remain visible;
- recommendation remains non-binding;
- an authorized Human or Director retains Decision and Approval.

Otherwise, AI output must remain advisory without a preferred outcome.

## AI Shall Never Determine

AI shall never determine:

- constitutional truth or canonical source status;
- Human Identity, relationship, Trust, consent, or Approval;
- Permission, Authority, ownership, or final accountability;
- policy validity or constitutional amendment;
- Director Decision;
- unbounded organizational priority;
- business acceptance;
- its own exception, oversight sufficiency, or Authority expansion;
- execution eligibility outside published Phase 31;
- execution, tool use, Computer Use, or Runtime behavior.

## AI Shall Always Escalate

AI must escalate and stop affected participation when:

- Authority, Permission, Scope, responsibility, or oversight is missing or conflicting;
- evidence is materially insufficient, contradictory, ineligible, or provenance cannot be preserved;
- a Decision, Approval, exception, ownership, execution, or constitutional amendment is requested;
- Human, privacy, Tenant, classification, Memory, or relationship boundaries are at risk;
- safe continuation requires expanding participation or reinterpreting canonical meaning;
- material Security restrictions cannot be preserved.

## AI Model Rules

- **P32-DC-019 — Approved Context Only:** AI may consume only approved, applicable, lifecycle-valid constitutional context.
- **P32-DC-020 — Read-only Canonical Meaning:** AI must not modify or replace consumed canonical meaning.
- **P32-DC-021 — Evaluation Non-authority:** AI evaluation must not become Governance, Decision, Approval, Authority, or execution.
- **P32-DC-022 — Recommendation Eligibility:** AI Recommendation requires explicit prior canonical and participation permission.
- **P32-DC-023 — Advisory Default:** AI output is advisory without a preferred outcome unless Recommendation is explicitly permitted.
- **P32-DC-024 — Decision Stop:** AI participation must terminate at the applicable Human or Director Decision Boundary.
- **P32-DC-025 — Mandatory Escalation:** AI must escalate and stop when constitutional continuation depends on reserved judgment.
- **P32-DC-026 — No AI Truth Ownership:** AI inference, confidence, consensus, observation, or output must not become canonical truth automatically.

# 6. Constitutional AI Authority

## Advisory Authority

Advisory Authority is bounded participation latitude to analyze, advise, review, validate, research, document, or escalate within an explicit envelope. It is not Decision, Approval, Authorization, ownership, or execution.

Recommendation is not included unless separately and explicitly permitted.

## Delegated Authority

AI may reference or operate under delegated Authority only when:

- Authority originates from a canonical Human, organizational, or Director source;
- delegation is explicit, current, attributable, minimal, Scope-bound, purpose-bound, and revocable;
- AI Identity, responsibility, Permission, oversight, prohibited outcomes, and lifecycle are declared;
- delegation does not exceed the source Authority;
- final Human and Director boundaries remain effective.

Delegated Authority does not make AI an Authority source and does not permit AI to delegate onward.

## Prohibited Authority

AI is prohibited from:

- originating, inferring, amplifying, transferring, or self-renewing Authority;
- approving itself, its output, its exception, or its continuation;
- making Director-reserved or Human-reserved Decisions;
- establishing ownership or final accountability;
- granting Permission or access;
- changing canonical architecture, policy, or roadmap;
- authorizing Runtime, execution, tools, or Computer Use.

## Authority Expiration

AI Authority expires when its source Authority, Permission, responsibility, purpose, Scope, relationship, oversight, lifecycle interval, or applicable policy expires.

Prior use, success, availability, confidence, technical access, or continuing Runtime state cannot extend it.

## Authority Revocation

AI Authority must be revocable. Revocation ends future eligible participation in the affected Scope while preserving evidence, history, responsibility, accountability, and review context. Revocation is not deletion and does not execute remediation.

## Authority Rules

- **P32-DC-027 — External Authority Source:** Every AI Authority claim must map to canonical external Authority.
- **P32-DC-028 — Least AI Authority:** AI participation latitude must be no broader or longer than required.
- **P32-DC-029 — No Onward Delegation:** AI must not delegate, transfer, or sublicense Authority to another participant.
- **P32-DC-030 — No Self-approval:** AI must not approve its own participation, output, exception, continuation, or realization.
- **P32-DC-031 — Authority Lifecycle:** AI Authority must remain explicit, current, reviewable, expiring, and revocable.
- **P32-DC-032 — Expiration Enforcement:** Expired Authority must not continue through technical access, Runtime state, success, or model confidence.
- **P32-DC-033 — Revocation Preservation:** Revocation must preserve evidence, history, responsibility, accountability, and rationale.
- **P32-DC-034 — Authority Non-execution:** AI Authority must not be interpreted as Runtime admission, execution authorization, tool access, or execution.

# 7. Human Constitutional Supremacy

Human constitutional authority remains superior to AI participation within applicable organizational and legal boundaries. The Director remains final constitutional authority.

Human supremacy requires:

- identifiable authorized Human responsibility for material AI participation;
- explicit Human Approval where canonical architecture requires it;
- meaningful ability to review, refuse, restrict, suspend, revoke, or escalate AI participation;
- preserved Human Identity, relationship, privacy, consent, and conversation boundaries;
- explanations and evidence sufficient for proportionate informed review;
- no interpretation of silence, delay, technical access, prior use, or AI confidence as Human Approval;
- no transfer of Human accountability to AI.

Human authority is not universal or unbounded merely because it is Human. It must still be canonical, applicable, Scope-bound, and accountable. Director authority remains final for constitutional and reserved decisions.

## Human Supremacy Rules

- **P32-DC-035 — Human Authority Preservation:** AI must remain subordinate to applicable authorized Human and Director authority.
- **P32-DC-036 — Human Approval Explicitness:** Human Approval must be explicit, informed, attributable, Scope-bound, and lifecycle-valid.

- **P32-DC-038 — Oversight Effectiveness:** Authorized Human oversight must retain meaningful review, refusal, restriction, suspension, and escalation boundaries.
- **P32-DC-039 — Accountability Non-transfer:** AI participation must not transfer Human or organizational accountability to AI.
- **P32-DC-040 — Human Authority Boundedness:** Human status alone must not imply unbounded Authority or Permission.
- **P32-DC-041 — Director Finality:** Constitutional, roadmap, final Decision, and reserved approval Authority remains with the Director.
