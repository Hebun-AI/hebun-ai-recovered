# 1. Executive Summary

Enterprise Wisdom is the constitutionally bounded Enterprise capability that represents enduring, attributable, evidence-supported, Director-governed intellectual guidance derived from accumulated Enterprise Knowledge and validated principle candidates — without becoming Knowledge, an Enterprise Principle, a Constitutional Principle, a Decision, Governance, Policy, or Authority, and without reasoning, deciding, governing, amending, or performing action. Enterprise Wisdom is always advisory; it never becomes Authority.

Phase 50 establishes the Enterprise Wisdom identity, ontology, lifecycle, qualification, validation, maturity, continuity, provenance, traceability, accountability, governance boundaries, and evolution — while preserving Enterprise Memory, Enterprise Knowledge, Program VI Evidence/Reasoning/Decision Intelligence, Program VIII Organizational Intelligence, published Phase 48 Assimilation, upstream Phase 49 principle candidates, Runtime, Human Architecture, and Director Authority ownership.

Enterprise Wisdom is:

- attributable and provenance-preserving;
- derived read-only from eligible governed Knowledge references and qualified principle candidates;
- purpose- and Scope-bound;
- explainable, traceable, and uncertainty-preserving;
- advisory, non-authoritative, non-decisional, non-governing, non-executing;
- maturity-aware without ever becoming permanent, certain, or true;
- fail-closed;
- Human-supremacy-preserving and Director-authority-preserving;
- technology, vendor, model, Runtime, infrastructure, product, and implementation independent.

Phase 50 does not define Intellectual Evolution & Director Learning Continuity (Phase 51). It establishes the wisdom foundation whose attributable, advisory outputs may become eligible inputs to that future architecture after its separate Director gate.

# 2. Architectural Purpose

Enterprise Wisdom exists to provide a stable constitutional layer through which the Enterprise can represent enduring, attributable intellectual guidance without allowing that guidance to become Knowledge, principle, decision, policy, authority, truth, or action.

Its purpose is to:

- establish Enterprise Wisdom as a constitutional, advisory capability rather than a model, scoring engine, or store;
- define what enterprise wisdom is and how it is qualified, validated, matured, continued, and evolved;
- consume eligible governed Knowledge references and qualified principle candidates read-only, preserving their identity, ownership, and provenance;
- preserve provenance, attribution, Scope, uncertainty, contradiction, and limitation for every wisdom;
- represent wisdom maturity and continuity without ever asserting permanence, certainty, or truth;
- maintain a bounded wisdom lifecycle distinct from Knowledge validity, principle enactment, Reasoning, Decision, and Governance;
- provide attributable, explainable, advisory wisdom outputs to separately governed downstream Intellectual Evolution architecture;
- preserve Human review, Human Approval, Human Override, and Director-reserved authority.

Enterprise Wisdom answers: **what enduring, attributable, advisory intellectual guidance can be represented from accumulated Knowledge and validated principle candidates within one bounded wisdom context — without becoming Knowledge, principle, Decision, Governance, Policy, Authority, truth, or action?**

It does not answer what is true, what must be adopted as principle, what should be decided, governed, or executed.

## Purpose Rules

- **P50-EW-005 — Declared Purpose:** Every wisdom context must declare an eligible purpose and Scope before wisdom qualification begins.
- **P50-EW-006 — Source-derived:** Wisdom must derive only from eligible governed Knowledge references and qualified principle candidates, never from fabrication or inferred authority.
- **P50-EW-007 — Wisdom–Knowledge Separation:** Representing wisdom is distinct from establishing Knowledge; wisdom creates no Knowledge validity.
- **P50-EW-008 — Wisdom–Principle Separation:** Representing wisdom is distinct from enacting a principle; wisdom creates no Enterprise or Constitutional Principle.

## Evolution Without Rewrite

# 4. Architecture

The constitutional Enterprise Wisdom model is:

```text
Declared Wisdom Context (purpose · Scope · authority basis · organization · Tenant)
        +
Eligible Governed Knowledge References (read-only) + qualified Principle Candidates
        +
Applicable Governance, Security, privacy, and classification constraints
        ↓
Wisdom Qualification (an attributable, advisory wisdom is proposed, never enacted)
        ↓
Wisdom Validation (eligibility, provenance, Scope, explanation, boundary checks)
        ↓
Wisdom Maturity Assessment (endurance and validation depth, never permanence)
        ↓
Qualified Enterprise Wisdom (attributable, advisory, non-authoritative, reviewable)
        ↓
Wisdom Continuity (attributable persistence across contexts, never authority)
        ↓
Eligible Human review or downstream Intellectual Evolution consumption
        ↓
Wisdom Evolution / Supersession / Retirement (attributable, history-preserving)
```

This model is an architectural relationship, not a Workflow, scoring algorithm, model trace, Runtime sequence, or implementation.

No downstream consumer is compelled to accept a wisdom. Consumption remains separately governed by that consumer's eligibility, Authority, purpose, Scope, and lifecycle. A qualified wisdom is attributable, advisory intellectual guidance — not Knowledge, not a principle, not a Decision, not Policy, not Authority, not truth.

## Enterprise Wisdom Ontology

The Enterprise Wisdom ontology is constitutional, not a data schema. It distinguishes, at minimum:

- **Wisdom Identity** — the attributable identity of one enterprise wisdom;
- **Wisdom Statement** — the attributable, advisory expression of enduring guidance, with uncertainty;
- **Wisdom Basis** — the eligible governed Knowledge references and qualified principle candidates the wisdom derives from;
- **Wisdom Scope** — the constitutional limits of applicability;
- **Wisdom Maturity** — the attributable endurance and validation depth, never permanence;
- **Wisdom Continuity** — the attributable persistence of wisdom across contexts, never authority;
- **Wisdom Provenance** — source, attribution, version, effective period, and lifecycle;
- **Wisdom Relationships** — supersession, contradiction, and refinement relationships to other wisdom.

The ontology defines constitutional meaning and boundaries only; it prescribes no representation, storage, schema, or format.

## Wisdom Eligibility and Governance

A wisdom is eligible only when its context, source basis, provenance, classification, Permission, and Scope are valid. Governance constraints (Policy, privacy, classification, Tenant, Permission) remain active and immutable; wisdom consumes them and never approves, waives, or enforces them, and never becomes Policy.

## Wisdom Maturity and Continuity Boundaries

Wisdom maturity records endurance and validation depth; it must not certify permanence, truth, or authority. Wisdom continuity records attributable persistence across contexts; it must not accumulate into Authority, Decision, Governance, or a self-justifying precedent. Neither maturity nor continuity may convert advisory guidance into a binding constraint.

## Architecture Rules

# 8. Enterprise Wisdom Lifecycle

The constitutional lifecycle is:

```text
Context Declared
        ↓
Source Basis Qualified (read-only)
        ↓
Constraints Bound
        ↓
Wisdom Qualified (attributable · advisory · not enacted)
        ↓
Wisdom Validated
        ↓
Wisdom Maturity Assessed (endurance · validation depth · never permanence)
        ↓
Qualified Enterprise Wisdom
        ↓
Wisdom Continuity Preserved (persistence · never authority)
        ↓
Review Eligible
        ↓
Evolved / Superseded / Retired — or — Closed or Escalated
        ↓
Audited Record Preserved
```

Lifecycle meanings are architectural:

- **Context Declared:** purpose, Scope, authority, organization, Tenant, and accountability are explicit.
- **Source Basis Qualified:** eligible governed Knowledge references and qualified principle candidates are confirmed read-only.
- **Constraints Bound:** applicable Governance, Security, privacy, and classification limits are attached.
- **Wisdom Qualified/Validated:** an attributable, advisory wisdom is proposed and assessed for eligibility, provenance, Scope, explanation, and boundaries.
- **Wisdom Maturity Assessed:** endurance and validation depth are represented without asserting permanence or truth.
- **Wisdom Continuity Preserved:** attributable persistence across contexts is maintained without accumulating authority.
- **Evolved/Superseded/Retired:** wisdom changes with preserved history, never rewriting Knowledge or the record.
- **Closed or Escalated:** lifecycle evidence is preserved without triggering action.

## Lifecycle Rules

- **P50-EW-062 — No Automatic Progression:** No lifecycle state advances automatically through confidence, maturity, consensus, availability, or completion.
- **P50-EW-063 — Context Before Sources:** Source qualification must not precede a complete declared context.
- **P50-EW-064 — Constraints Before Qualification:** Applicable constraints must be bound before wisdom qualification.
- **P50-EW-065 — Sources Before Wisdom:** Wisdom must not precede eligible Knowledge references and qualified principle candidates.

- **P50-EW-067 — Review Non-approval:** Review eligibility must not become Human Approval, Governance approval, principle enactment, Decision, or Authority.
- **P50-EW-068 — Continuity Non-precedent:** Wisdom continuity must not become binding precedent, Authority, or Governance.
- **P50-EW-069 — Evolution History:** Evolution, supersession, and retirement must preserve prior wisdom history and provenance.
- **P50-EW-070 — Closure Non-action:** Lifecycle closure or escalation must not trigger principle enactment, Policy, Workflow, Runtime, automation, or execution.
