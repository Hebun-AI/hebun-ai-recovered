# 1. Executive Summary

Knowledge Assimilation & Multi-Source Synthesis is the constitutionally bounded Enterprise capability that receives intellectual inputs, relates multiple governed sources, identifies convergence and divergence, preserves provenance, and prepares synthesis — without becoming Enterprise Knowledge, discovering principles, producing wisdom, reasoning, deciding, recommending, or performing action.

Phase 48 establishes the Knowledge Assimilation identity, the Multi-Source Synthesis identity, the assimilation lifecycle, source eligibility, source provenance, source normalization, source relationship boundaries, cross-source synthesis, conflict/convergence/divergence identification, traceability, accountability, validation, and governance boundaries — while preserving Enterprise Memory, Enterprise Knowledge, Program VI Evidence/Reasoning/Decision Intelligence, Program VIII Organizational Intelligence, Runtime, Human Architecture, and Director Authority ownership.

- attributable and provenance-preserving;
- derived read-only from eligible governed intellectual sources;
- purpose- and Scope-bound;
- explainable, traceable, and uncertainty-preserving;

# 2. Architectural Purpose

Knowledge Assimilation & Multi-Source Synthesis exists to provide a stable constitutional layer through which the Enterprise can receive intellectual inputs and relate them across multiple governed sources without allowing that assimilation to become Knowledge, truth, principle, wisdom, authority, or action.

Its purpose is to:

- establish assimilation as a constitutional capability rather than an ingestion pipeline, parser, model, or store;
- define what an assimilated intellectual source is and how it is received, normalized, and related to other sources;
- consume eligible governed intellectual sources read-only, preserving each source's identity, ownership, and provenance;
- identify cross-source convergence, divergence, and conflict without resolving, concluding, or judging truth;
- prepare synthesis material that is explicitly not a conclusion, principle, or decision;
- maintain a bounded assimilation lifecycle distinct from Memory admission, Knowledge validity, Reasoning, and principle discovery;
- provide attributable, explainable assimilation and synthesis-preparation outputs to separately governed downstream Intellectual Evolution phases;
- preserve Human review, Human Approval, Human Override, and Director-reserved authority.

Knowledge Assimilation answers: **what attributable intellectual material can be received from eligible governed sources, and how do those sources relate — converge, diverge, or conflict — within one bounded assimilation context, without becoming Knowledge, principle, wisdom, Reasoning, Decision, or action?**

It does not answer what is true, what principle holds, what the Enterprise should conclude, decide, or execute.

## Purpose Rules

- **P48-KA-005 — Declared Purpose:** Every assimilation context must declare an eligible purpose and Scope before source assimilation begins.
- **P48-KA-006 — Source-derived:** Assimilation must derive only from eligible governed intellectual sources, never from fabrication or inferred admission.
- **P48-KA-007 — Assimilation–Knowledge Separation:** Assimilating a source is distinct from admitting Knowledge; assimilation creates no Enterprise Knowledge.
- **P48-KA-008 — Synthesis–Conclusion Separation:** Preparing synthesis is distinct from concluding; synthesis preparation produces no conclusion, principle, or Decision.
- **P48-KA-009 — Future-phase Separation:** Phase 48 must not define Principle/Pattern & Comparative Intelligence, Enterprise Wisdom, or Intellectual Evolution & Director Learning Continuity.

# 3. Design Principles

## Human Constitutional Supremacy

Human agency, judgment, responsibility, review, Approval, and Override remain constitutionally available. Assimilation volume, source count, confidence, or apparent agreement cannot replace them.

## Director Authority

The Director retains final constitutional Authority for reserved matters, constitutional interpretation, amendments, and roadmap governance. No assimilation or synthesis becomes Director authority.

## Assimilation Is Not Knowledge

Assimilation receives and relates intellectual inputs; it never redefines, admits, replaces, or governs Enterprise Knowledge or Enterprise Memory.

## Synthesis Is Not Conclusion

Multi-Source Synthesis prepares related material; it never concludes, discovers a principle, judges truth, decides, or recommends as authority.

## Correlation Is Not Causation; Similarity Is Not Equivalence

Cross-source relationships are constitutional observations of convergence, divergence, or conflict. They assert no causation, no equivalence, and no truth.

## Source Agreement Is Not Truth; Source Conflict Is Not Error

Convergence across sources does not certify truth, and divergence or conflict does not certify error. Both are preserved as attributable observations with uncertainty.

## Evidence and Provenance

Every assimilated source and every cross-source relationship must trace to eligible governed sources, with preserved identity, attribution, version, Scope, and effective period.

## Explainability and Uncertainty

Every material assimilation and synthesis-preparation output must preserve sufficient explanation of its basis, assumptions, limitations, contradictions, and uncertainty for constitutional review, without exposing protected information.

## Attribution and Accountability

Every assimilation, normalization, relationship, and validation act must remain attributable to an eligible authority, actor, purpose, and Scope.

## Least Authority

Assimilation capability, source access, confidence, or apparent consensus creates no undeclared Authority or Permission.

## Fail-Closed Assimilation

Missing or incompatible Identity, Authority, Scope, provenance, classification, Permission, source eligibility, or required review prevents a valid assimilation or synthesis preparation.

## Independence

Knowledge Assimilation & Multi-Source Synthesis remains technology, model, method, vendor, Runtime, infrastructure, product, and implementation independent.

## Evolution Without Rewrite

Assimilated sources and cross-source relationships may evolve, be superseded, or be retired without silently rewriting Memory, Knowledge, prior assimilation history, or constitutional meaning.

# 4. Architecture

The constitutional Knowledge Assimilation & Multi-Source Synthesis model is:

```text
Declared Assimilation Context (purpose · Scope · authority basis · organization · Tenant)
        +
Eligible Governed Intellectual Sources (read-only) — documents, books, research, videos, conversations
        +
Applicable Governance, Security, privacy, and classification constraints
        ↓
Source Assimilation (an eligible source is received and attributed)
        ↓
Source Normalization (constitutional comparability without altering source meaning)
        ↓
Multi-Source Relation (convergence · divergence · conflict identified, not resolved)
        ↓
Synthesis Preparation (related, provenance-preserving material, explicitly not a conclusion)
        ↓
Assimilation Validation (eligibility, provenance, Scope, explanation, boundary checks)
        ↓
Qualified Assimilation Output (attributable, non-authoritative, non-concluding, reviewable)
        ↓
Eligible Human review or downstream Intellectual Evolution consumption
        ↓
Assimilation Evolution / Supersession / Retirement (attributable, history-preserving)
```

This model is an architectural relationship, not a Workflow, ingestion pipeline, parser, model trace, Memory admission pipeline, Runtime sequence, or implementation.

No downstream consumer is compelled to accept an assimilation or synthesis output. Consumption remains separately governed by that consumer's eligibility, Authority, purpose, Scope, and lifecycle. A qualified assimilation output is attributable related intellectual material — not Knowledge, not Memory, not Evidence, not a principle, not a conclusion, not a Decision.

## Assimilation Ontology

The assimilation ontology is constitutional, not a data schema. It distinguishes, at minimum:

- **Assimilation Identity** — the attributable identity of one assimilation context and its outputs;
- **Intellectual Source** — an eligible governed source received read-only, with preserved identity and provenance;
- **Source Attribution** — the origin, authorship, version, and eligibility of a source;
- **Normalized Source Representation** — a constitutional comparability view that never alters source meaning;
- **Cross-Source Relationship** — an attributable convergence, divergence, or conflict observation between sources;
- **Synthesis Preparation** — related, provenance-preserving material offered without conclusion;
- **Assimilation Provenance** — source, attribution, version, effective period, and lifecycle;
- **Assimilation Relationships** — supersession, contradiction, and refinement relationships to other assimilations.

The ontology defines constitutional meaning and boundaries only; it prescribes no representation, storage, schema, or format.

## Source Eligibility and Governance

A source is eligible only when its identity, attribution, provenance, classification, Permission, and Scope are valid. Governance constraints (Policy, privacy, classification, Tenant, Permission) remain active and immutable; assimilation consumes them and never approves, waives, or enforces them. A candidate source that fails eligibility is restricted, deferred, or escalated — never silently admitted.

## Multi-Source Relation Boundaries

Cross-source relation identifies convergence, divergence, and conflict as attributable observations. It must not resolve conflict, select a winning source, rank truth, infer causation from correlation, or treat similarity as equivalence. Conflict is preserved, not corrected; agreement is preserved, not certified.

## Architecture Rules

- **P48-KA-022 — Declared Context Required:** Assimilation requires one complete declared context before any source is assimilated.
- **P48-KA-023 — Eligible Source Required:** Only eligible governed intellectual sources may be assimilated.
- **P48-KA-024 — Constraint Applicability:** Applicable Governance, Security, privacy, classification, and Tenant constraints must remain active and immutable.
- **P48-KA-025 — Assimilation Before Relation:** A source must be assimilated and attributed before it is related to other sources.

- **P48-KA-028 — Synthesis Non-conclusion:** Synthesis Preparation must remain related material and must not become a conclusion, principle, or Decision.
- **P48-KA-029 — Validation Before Qualification:** No assimilation output is qualified before eligibility, provenance, Scope, explanation, and boundary validation complete.
- **P48-KA-030 — Ontology Non-schema:** The assimilation ontology defines constitutional meaning only and prescribes no representation, storage, or schema.
- **P48-KA-031 — Consumer Independence:** A qualified assimilation output must not compel Human, downstream phase, or any consumer.

# 5. Canonical Definitions

## Knowledge Assimilation

> **Knowledge Assimilation is the constitutionally bounded Enterprise capability that receives and attributes eligible governed intellectual sources read-only, without redefining Enterprise Knowledge or Memory and without becoming Evidence, Reasoning, principle, wisdom, Decision, Authority, or action.**

## Multi-Source Synthesis

> **Multi-Source Synthesis is the constitutionally bounded relation of multiple assimilated sources — identifying convergence, divergence, and conflict and preparing related, provenance-preserving material — that never resolves conflict, concludes, discovers a principle, judges truth, decides, or recommends as authority.**

## Assimilation Context

> **An Assimilation Context is the bounded constitutional description of one assimilation purpose, Scope, organization, Tenant, authority basis, source basis, constraints, lifecycle, review boundary, and accountability.**

## Intellectual Source

> **An Intellectual Source is an eligible governed input — such as a document, book, research paper, video, or conversation — consumed read-only with preserved identity, attribution, ownership, provenance, and classification, and never treated as admitted Knowledge or Evidence.**

## Source Attribution

> **Source Attribution is the preserved origin, authorship, version, eligibility, and classification of an intellectual source, maintained independently of Memory and Knowledge provenance.**

## Normalized Source Representation

> **A Normalized Source Representation is a constitutional comparability view of a source that preserves the source's meaning without altering, correcting, reinterpreting, or admitting it, and prescribes no format, storage, or schema.**

## Cross-Source Relationship

> **A Cross-Source Relationship is an attributable observation of convergence, divergence, or conflict between assimilated sources that asserts no causation, no equivalence, and no truth, and resolves nothing.**

## Convergence

> **Convergence is an attributable observation that multiple sources align on a matter, preserved with uncertainty and never certified as truth.**

## Divergence

> **Divergence is an attributable observation that multiple sources differ on a matter, preserved with uncertainty and never certified as error.**

## Conflict Identification

> **Conflict Identification is the attributable recognition of contradiction between sources, preserved as an observation without resolution, ranking, or truth judgment.**

## Synthesis Preparation

> **Synthesis Preparation is attributable, provenance-preserving related material offered for downstream governed consumption that is explicitly not a conclusion, principle, wisdom, Evidence, Decision, or command.**

## Candidate Knowledge

> **Candidate Knowledge is attributable assimilated material that is not, and must never be treated as, Enterprise Knowledge; it holds no validity, truth, or authority by virtue of being assimilated.**

## Assimilation Validation

> **Assimilation Validation is the attributable constitutional evaluation of context completeness, source eligibility, provenance, Scope adherence, explanation, uncertainty, and boundaries, without becoming truth certification, Knowledge admission, Governance approval, Decision, or Runtime admission.**

## Assimilation Output

> **An Assimilation Output is an attributable, Scope-bound, explainable expression of assimilated sources, cross-source relationships, and synthesis preparation that is never Knowledge, Memory, Evidence, a principle, a conclusion, a Decision, Approval, command, or execution artifact.**

# 8. Assimilation Lifecycle

The constitutional lifecycle is:

```text
Context Declared
        ↓
Source Basis Qualified (read-only)
        ↓
Constraints Bound
        ↓
Sources Assimilated (attributed)
        ↓
Sources Normalized (meaning preserved)
        ↓
Cross-Source Relations Identified (convergence · divergence · conflict)
        ↓
Synthesis Prepared (related material · not a conclusion)
        ↓
Assimilation Validated
        ↓
Assimilation Qualified
        ↓
Review Eligible
        ↓
Evolved / Superseded / Retired — or — Closed or Escalated
        ↓
Audited Record Preserved
```

Lifecycle meanings are architectural:

- **Context Declared:** purpose, Scope, authority, organization, Tenant, and accountability are explicit.
- **Source Basis Qualified:** eligible governed intellectual sources are confirmed read-only.
- **Constraints Bound:** applicable Governance, Security, privacy, and classification limits are attached.
- **Sources Assimilated/Normalized:** eligible sources are received, attributed, and made comparable without altering meaning.
- **Cross-Source Relations Identified:** convergence, divergence, and conflict are observed and preserved without resolution.
- **Synthesis Prepared:** related, provenance-preserving material is assembled and explicitly held short of conclusion.
- **Assimilation Validated/Qualified:** eligibility, provenance, Scope, explanation, and boundaries are assessed; the output becomes attributable, non-authoritative, and reviewable.
- **Evolved/Superseded/Retired:** assimilation changes with preserved history, never rewriting Memory, Knowledge, or the record.
- **Closed or Escalated:** lifecycle evidence is preserved without triggering action.

## Lifecycle Rules

- **P48-KA-059 — No Automatic Progression:** No lifecycle state advances automatically through confidence, source count, availability, or completion.
- **P48-KA-060 — Context Before Sources:** Source qualification must not precede a complete declared context.
- **P48-KA-061 — Constraints Before Assimilation:** Applicable constraints must be bound before sources are assimilated.
- **P48-KA-062 — Sources Before Relation:** Cross-source relation must not precede assimilated, normalized sources.
- **P48-KA-063 — Relation Before Synthesis:** Synthesis preparation must not precede identified cross-source relationships.
- **P48-KA-064 — Validation Before Qualification:** No assimilation is qualified before required validation completes.
- **P48-KA-065 — Review Non-approval:** Review eligibility must not become Human Approval, Governance approval, Decision, or Authority.
- **P48-KA-066 — Evolution History:** Evolution, supersession, and retirement must preserve prior assimilation history and provenance.
- **P48-KA-067 — Closure Non-action:** Lifecycle closure or escalation must not trigger Knowledge admission, Workflow, Runtime, automation, or execution.
