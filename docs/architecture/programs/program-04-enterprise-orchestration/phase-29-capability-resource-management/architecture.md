# Phase 29 — Capability & Resource Management

## Canonical Status

**STATUS: COMPLETE / PUBLISHED**

This document defines the canonical architectural identities of Enterprise Capability Management and Enterprise Resource Management within Program IV — Enterprise Orchestration.

Phase 29 consumes the published identities of Enterprise Work, Enterprise Task, Enterprise Activity, Enterprise Planning, Enterprise Prioritization, Enterprise Delegation, and Enterprise Coordination. It also consumes the canonical Business Capability Architecture without modifying it.

Phase 29 defines organizational architecture only. It authorizes no Capability creation, Runtime resource allocation, scheduling, workforce planning, Human Resources Management, infrastructure management, cloud resource management, Agent Runtime, workflow, execution, Events, messaging, API, database, interface, dashboard, Security mechanism, or implementation.

---

# 1. Architectural Identity

## Enterprise Capability Management Identity

**Enterprise Capability Management** is a governed Program-level organizational architectural function that establishes attributable capability-demand interpretation and capability-eligibility context for authorized Enterprise Work while preserving every canonical Enterprise Capability identity, relationship, lifecycle, ownership, and realization boundary.

Capability Management exists because Work, Tasks, Activities, Planning, Prioritization, Delegation, and Coordination may express organizational demand that must be related to capabilities the enterprise already has or constitutionally recognizes. That relationship must remain visible and governable without allowing Program IV to create, redefine, classify, own, realize, or execute a canonical Enterprise Capability.

Enterprise Capability Management is not a canonical Enterprise Capability object.

## Enterprise Resource Management Identity

**Enterprise Resource Management** is a governed Program-level organizational architectural function that establishes attributable resource-eligibility, constraint, availability-context, responsibility, and accountability relationships for an authorized Enterprise Capability Management context without allocating, reserving, scheduling, consuming, deploying, or executing any Resource.

Resource Management exists because capability demand may depend on eligible organizational means whose constraints and accountability must remain visible before any future realization. It preserves architectural meaning without becoming workforce planning, allocation, infrastructure management, scheduling, or Runtime control.

Enterprise Resource Management is not a Resource, allocation mechanism, Runtime service, or execution controller.

## Identity Rules

- **P29-CR-001 — Function-Level Identity:** Capability Management and Resource Management are Program-level organizational architectural functions, not enterprise primitives.
- **P29-CR-002 — Upstream Preservation:** Both functions must preserve all consumed Program IV identities and relationships.
- **P29-CR-003 — Capability Architecture Preservation:** Capability Management must consume canonical Business Capability Architecture without redefining it.
- **P29-CR-004 — Stable Management Identity:** Participant, technology, organizational, or Runtime change must not replace either canonical management identity.
- **P29-CR-005 — Non-authority:** Neither function creates authority, permission, approval, allocation eligibility, or execution eligibility.

# 2. Purpose

Enterprise Capability Management exists to make the relationship between organizational demand and canonical Enterprise Capabilities:

- explicit;
- attributable;
- traceable to authorized Work and its downstream context;
- governed without transferring Capability ownership;
- evaluable without fabricating missing Capabilities;
- independent from realization and Runtime.

Enterprise Resource Management exists to make resource-related organizational context:

- explicit without allocating Resources;
- attributable without defining identity systems;
- constrained without implementing constraints;
- accountable without performing management operations;
- comparable without creating scheduling or optimization;
- usable by future constitutional consumers without becoming Runtime state.

Together they close Program IV’s architectural responsibility by relating governed organizational demand to existing capability meaning and eligible resource context without crossing into implementation or execution.

## Purpose Rules

- **P29-CR-006 — Demand Clarity:** Capability demand must remain explicit, attributable, and traceable to authorized Work context.
- **P29-CR-007 — Eligibility Clarity:** Capability and Resource eligibility findings must expose their basis, constraints, uncertainty, and authority limits.
- **P29-CR-008 — Management Separation:** Management context must remain distinct from ownership, allocation, realization, and execution.

# 3. Canonical Definitions

## Enterprise Capability Management

> **Enterprise Capability Management is the governed, attributable, actor-neutral, and implementation-independent Program-level organizational architectural function that consumes authorized Enterprise Work, Task, Activity, Planning, Prioritization, Delegation, and Coordination context to interpret capability demand and establish eligibility relationships with existing canonical Enterprise Capabilities, while preserving Capability identity, ownership, lifecycle, network relationships, intelligence, and realization boundaries without creating, redefining, classifying, approving, binding, realizing, or executing a Capability.**

## Enterprise Resource Management

> **Enterprise Resource Management is the governed, attributable, actor-neutral, and implementation-independent Program-level organizational architectural function that consumes an authorized Enterprise Capability Management context to establish resource-eligibility, constraint, responsibility, accountability, and availability-context relationships while preserving Resource independence and organizational boundaries without allocating, reserving, scheduling, optimizing, consuming, deploying, or executing Resources.**

These are the authoritative definitions of Enterprise Capability Management and Enterprise Resource Management for Hebun AI.

They are immutable except through the amendment rules of the Enterprise Constitution and Program IV Constitution. No downstream Program, Runtime, implementation, organizational convention, or participant may silently replace or reinterpret them.

# 4. Architectural Principles

## Canonical-Capability Preserving

Enterprise Capability Management treats the published Business Capability Architecture as immutable canonical input.

## Demand-Oriented

Capability Management interprets organizational demand. It does not invent a Capability to satisfy demand.

## Eligibility-Oriented

Management findings express context-bound eligibility, not approval, assignment, availability guarantees, or execution authorization.

## Resource-Independent

Enterprise Resource Management governs relationships concerning Resources without becoming or owning the Resources it describes.

## Accountability-Preserving

Responsibility and accountability remain explicit across demand, eligibility, constraint, and management relationships.

## Context-Bound

Every finding must retain applicable Work, Task, Activity, Planning, Prioritization, Delegation, Coordination, Capability, organizational, and Governance context.

## Evidence-Grounded

Missing Capability or Resource evidence remains missing. Neither function may fabricate eligibility, availability, fitness, or feasibility.

## Actor-Neutral

Canonical meaning remains stable across human, AI, hybrid, organizational, and technical participation.

## Governable

Both functions remain subject to policy, authority, review, conflict, escalation, and amendment boundaries.

## Traceable

Demand, sources, relationships, criteria, findings, constraints, uncertainty, changes, and disposition remain attributable.

## Runtime-Independent

Runtime may later consume approved management context but cannot define its identity, authority, or conclusions.

## Technology and Implementation Independent

Neither function depends on algorithms, APIs, databases, platforms, infrastructure, models, interfaces, vendors, or execution engines.

## Evolvable

Management context may evolve through attributable Governance while preserving prior meaning, evidence, authority boundaries, and history.

Enterprise Work remains the foundational value-oriented responsibility defined by Phase 25.

Capability Management may interpret capability demand arising from authorized Work. Resource Management may establish resource context only through authorized Capability Management. Neither function may:

- redefine Work identity, purpose, ownership, outcome, evidence, or completion;
- treat demand or eligibility as Work approval;
- complete, close, execute, or measure success for Work.

- **P29-CR-019 — Work Independence:** Enterprise Work remains canonical without Capability Management or Resource Management.
- **P29-CR-020 — Work Non-redefinition:** Both functions must inherit Work unchanged.
- **P29-CR-021 — Work Outcome Boundary:** Capability or Resource eligibility must not imply Work completion or organizational success.

# 11. Capability–Resource Relationship

Enterprise Capability Management and Enterprise Resource Management have one canonical dependency direction:

Enterprise Capability Management

↓

Enterprise Resource Management

Capability Management remains independently valid. Resource Management requires an authorized Capability Management context.

Capability Management provides:

- attributable capability demand;
- canonical Capability references;
- eligibility findings;
- applicable constraints;
- responsibility and accountability context;
- evidence, uncertainty, and Governance applicability.

Resource Management may return informational findings concerning:

- resource eligibility;
- constraint compatibility;
- attributable availability context;
- responsibility or accountability conflicts;
- missing evidence;
- uncertainty and escalation eligibility.

Informational feedback must not reverse dependency, redefine a Capability, create or allocate a Resource, transfer accountability, create authority, or authorize execution.

- **P29-CR-031 — Directional Dependency:** Capability Management remains independently valid; Resource Management requires an authorized Capability Management context.
- **P29-CR-032 — Non-Reversing Feedback:** Resource findings may inform Capability Management but must not reverse dependency or alter Capability identity, responsibility, accountability, authority, or execution eligibility.
- **P29-CR-033 — Identity Separation:** Capability, Capability Management, Resource, and Resource Management must retain distinct identities.
- **P29-CR-034 — Relationship Traceability:** Context, findings, evidence, rationale, versions, and changes must remain attributable.

# 12. Enterprise Relationships

These relationships are conceptual. They define no Capability catalog changes, Resource schema, workforce plan, organization chart, identity record, allocation, schedule, approval, workflow, Runtime, infrastructure, or implementation.

Organization provides accountability and Governance context. Phase 29 does not define organizational structure or operational management.

Management relationships preserve upstream responsibility and accountability. Eligibility, availability, and participation do not transfer accountability.

## Resource

A Resource may provide organizational means relevant to capability demand. Phase 29 does not define a canonical Resource object model or create, own, allocate, schedule, reserve, consume, or deploy Resources.

## Human, AI, Hybrid, and Organizational Participants

Participants may contribute evidence or appear in governed context. Participant type does not alter canonical meaning or confer authority, eligibility, availability, or execution rights.

- **P29-CR-035 — Actor Neutrality:** Management meaning must remain stable across human, AI, hybrid, organizational, and technical participation.
- **P29-CR-036 — Participation–Authority Separation:** Participation must not imply authority, approval, accountability, allocation, or execution eligibility.
- **P29-CR-037 — External Architecture Boundary:** Organization, Governance, Capability, Resource, Identity, Human Architecture, and Runtime may constrain Phase 29 but are not defined by it.
