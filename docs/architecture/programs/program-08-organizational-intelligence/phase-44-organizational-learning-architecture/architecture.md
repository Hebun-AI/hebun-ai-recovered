# 1. Executive Summary

Organizational Learning is the constitutionally bounded Enterprise capability that identifies and preserves attributable learning about the Enterprise — what worked, what did not, and what the organization has come to understand about itself — without redefining Enterprise Memory, becoming Knowledge, Evidence, Reasoning, or Decision Intelligence, and without acquiring Authority or performing action.

Phase 44 establishes the Organizational Learning identity, ontology, lifecycle, eligibility, governance, provenance, traceability, accountability, validation, and evolution boundaries — while preserving Enterprise Memory, Enterprise Knowledge, Program VI Reasoning/Evidence/Decision Intelligence, Program IV Work, Program V Security, Program VII Computer Use, Runtime, Human Architecture, and Director Authority ownership.

Organizational Learning is:

- attributable and provenance-preserving;
- derived read-only from governed organizational evidence and eligible Memory references;
- purpose- and Scope-bound;
- explainable, traceable, and uncertainty-preserving;
- non-authoritative, non-decisional, non-executing;
- fail-closed;
- Human-supremacy-preserving and Director-authority-preserving;
- technology, vendor, model, Runtime, infrastructure, product, and implementation independent.

Phase 44 does not define Organizational Optimization Intelligence (Phase 45), Strategic Awareness (Phase 46), or Strategic Enterprise Evolution (Phase 47). It establishes the learning foundation whose attributable outputs may become eligible inputs to those future architectures after their separate Director gates.

## Executive Rules

- **P44-OL-001 — Constitutional Organizational Learning:** Organizational Learning must remain subordinate to the Enterprise Constitution, the Program VIII Constitution, Human supremacy, and Director Authority.
- **P44-OL-002 — Learning Non-authority:** No learning identification, preservation, validation, or output may create Authority, Permission, Decision, Approval, Runtime behavior, automation, or execution.
- **P44-OL-003 — Memory Preservation:** Organizational Learning must not redefine, replace, or absorb Enterprise Memory, Knowledge, or Evidence.
- **P44-OL-004 — Non-implementation:** Phase 44 must not define models, learning algorithms, analytics engines, retrieval, storage, Runtime behavior, or implementation.

# 2. Architectural Purpose

Organizational Learning exists to provide a stable constitutional layer through which the Enterprise can recognize and preserve attributable learning about itself without allowing that learning to become memory, knowledge, truth, authority, or action.

Its purpose is to:

- establish Organizational Learning as a constitutional capability rather than a model, store, or analytics service;
- define what an attributable organizational learning is and how it is identified, preserved, and evolved;
- consume governed organizational evidence and eligible Memory references read-only;
- preserve provenance, attribution, Scope, uncertainty, contradiction, and limitation for every learning;
- maintain a bounded learning lifecycle distinct from Memory admission, Knowledge validity, and Reasoning;
- provide attributable, explainable learning outputs to separately governed downstream Organizational Intelligence phases;
- preserve Human review, Human Approval, Human Override, and Director-reserved authority.

Organizational Learning answers: **what attributable learning about the Enterprise can be identified and preserved from eligible governed evidence within one bounded learning context — without becoming Memory, Knowledge, Evidence, Reasoning, Decision, or action?**

It does not answer what the Enterprise must remember canonically, what is true, what should be decided, or what must be executed.

## Purpose Rules

- **P44-OL-005 — Declared Purpose:** Every learning context must declare an eligible purpose and Scope before learning identification begins.
- **P44-OL-006 — Evidence-derived:** Organizational Learning must derive only from eligible governed organizational evidence and Memory references, never from fabrication or inference of admission.
- **P44-OL-007 — Learning–Memory Separation:** Identifying a learning is distinct from admitting Memory; learning creates no Memory.
- **P44-OL-008 — Learning–Knowledge Separation:** Identifying a learning is distinct from establishing Knowledge; learning creates no Knowledge validity.
- **P44-OL-009 — Future-phase Separation:** Phase 44 must not define Optimization Intelligence, Strategic Awareness, or Strategic Enterprise Evolution.

# 3. Design Principles

## Human Constitutional Supremacy

Human agency, judgment, responsibility, review, Approval, and Override remain constitutionally available. Learning volume, confidence, or usefulness cannot replace them.

## Director Authority

The Director retains final constitutional Authority for reserved matters, constitutional interpretation, amendments, and roadmap governance. No learning becomes Director authority.

## Learning Is Not Memory

Organizational Learning identifies and preserves attributable learning; it never redefines, admits, replaces, or governs Enterprise Memory.

## Learning Is Not Knowledge or Evidence

A learning is an attributable organizational understanding, not admitted Knowledge and not Evidence; it acquires neither identity by being preserved.

## Learning Is Not Reasoning or Decision

Learning records what was understood; it does not reason, conclude, decide, approve, or recommend as authority.

## Evidence and Provenance

Every learning must trace to eligible governed evidence, with preserved source, attribution, version, Scope, and effective period.

## Explainability and Uncertainty

Every material learning must preserve sufficient explanation of its basis, assumptions, limitations, contradictions, and uncertainty for constitutional review, without exposing protected information.

## Attribution and Accountability

Every learning identification, preservation, evolution, and validation act must remain attributable to an eligible authority, actor, purpose, and Scope.

## Least Authority

Learning capability, evidence access, confidence, consensus, or usefulness creates no undeclared Authority or Permission.

## Fail-Closed Learning

Missing or incompatible Identity, Authority, Scope, provenance, classification, Permission, evidence eligibility, or required review prevents a valid organizational learning.

## Independence

Organizational Learning remains technology, model, method, vendor, Runtime, infrastructure, product, and implementation independent.

## Evolution Without Rewrite

Learnings may evolve, be superseded, or be retired without silently rewriting Memory, Knowledge, prior learning history, or constitutional meaning.

## Principle Rules

- **P44-OL-010 — Human Supremacy:** Learning must not erase or automate Human judgment, review, Approval, responsibility, or Override.
- **P44-OL-011 — Director Reservation:** Reserved matters and constitutional interpretation remain under Director Authority.
- **P44-OL-012 — Memory Non-redefinition:** Learning must not redefine, admit, replace, or govern Enterprise Memory.
- **P44-OL-013 — Knowledge and Evidence Non-creation:** Learning must not create Knowledge validity or Evidence identity.
- **P44-OL-014 — Reasoning and Decision Non-creation:** Learning must not reason, conclude, decide, approve, or recommend as authority.
- **P44-OL-015 — Provenance Required:** Every learning must preserve source evidence, attribution, version, Scope, and effective period.
- **P44-OL-016 — Explainability Required:** Every material learning must expose sufficient constitutional explanation and uncertainty.
- **P44-OL-017 — Least Learning Authority:** Learning capability or access must not expand Permission, Authority, or Scope.
- **P44-OL-018 — Uncertainty Visibility:** Uncertainty, contradiction, and limitation must remain explicit and must not collapse into truth.
- **P44-OL-019 — Fail-closed:** Missing eligibility, provenance, authority, or review blocks a valid learning and requires restriction, deferral, or escalation.
- **P44-OL-020 — Independence Required:** No model, method, vendor, Runtime, infrastructure, or implementation may define Organizational Learning meaning.
- **P44-OL-021 — Evolution Non-rewrite:** Learning evolution must not silently rewrite Memory, Knowledge, or prior learning history.

# 4. Architecture

The constitutional Organizational Learning model is:

```text
Declared Organizational Learning Context (purpose · Scope · authority basis · organization · Tenant)
        +
Eligible Governed Organizational Evidence (read-only) + eligible Memory references
        +
Applicable Governance, Security, privacy, and classification constraints
        ↓
Learning Identification (an attributable organizational learning is recognized)
        ↓
Learning Preservation (provenance, attribution, Scope, uncertainty preserved)
        ↓
Learning Validation (eligibility, provenance, Scope, explanation, boundary checks)
        ↓
Qualified Organizational Learning (attributable, non-authoritative, reviewable)
        ↓
Eligible Human review or downstream Organizational Intelligence consumption
        ↓
Learning Evolution / Supersession / Retirement (attributable, history-preserving)
```

This model is an architectural relationship, not a Workflow, learning algorithm, model trace, Memory admission pipeline, Runtime sequence, or implementation.

No downstream consumer is compelled to accept a learning. Consumption remains separately governed by that consumer's eligibility, Authority, purpose, Scope, and lifecycle. A qualified learning is an attributable organizational understanding — not Memory, not Knowledge, not Evidence, not a Decision.

## Organizational Learning Ontology

The Organizational Learning ontology is constitutional, not a data schema. It distinguishes, at minimum:

- **Learning Identity** — the attributable identity of one organizational learning;
- **Learning Subject** — the bounded organizational matter the learning concerns;
- **Learning Basis** — the eligible governed evidence and Memory references the learning derives from;
- **Learning Statement** — the attributable expression of what was learned, with uncertainty;
- **Learning Scope** — the constitutional limits of applicability;
- **Learning Provenance** — source, attribution, version, effective period, and lifecycle;
- **Learning Relationships** — supersession, contradiction, and refinement relationships to other learnings.

The ontology defines constitutional meaning and boundaries only; it prescribes no representation, storage, schema, or format.

## Learning Eligibility and Governance

A learning is eligible only when its context, evidence basis, provenance, classification, Permission, and Scope are valid. Governance constraints (Policy, privacy, classification, Tenant, Permission) remain active and immutable; learning consumes them and never approves, waives, or enforces them.

## Architecture Rules

- **P44-OL-022 — Declared Context Required:** Learning requires one complete declared context before identification.
- **P44-OL-023 — Eligible Evidence Required:** Only eligible governed evidence and Memory references may ground a learning.
- **P44-OL-024 — Constraint Applicability:** Applicable Governance, Security, privacy, classification, and Tenant constraints must remain active and immutable.
- **P44-OL-025 — Identification Before Preservation:** A learning must be identified within context before it is preserved.
- **P44-OL-026 — Validation Before Qualification:** No learning is qualified before eligibility, provenance, Scope, explanation, and boundary validation complete.
- **P44-OL-027 — Ontology Non-schema:** The Organizational Learning ontology defines constitutional meaning only and prescribes no representation, storage, or schema.
- **P44-OL-028 — Consumer Independence:** A qualified learning must not compel Human, Organizational Intelligence, or any consumer.
- **P44-OL-029 — Governance Immutability:** Learning must not approve, waive, enforce, or rewrite Governance constraints.
- **P44-OL-030 — Read-only Evidence:** Learning must consume evidence and Memory references read-only, preserving their identity, ownership, and provenance.
- **P44-OL-031 — No Memory Admission:** Learning must not admit, modify, or govern Enterprise Memory.

# 8. Organizational Learning Lifecycle

The constitutional lifecycle is:

```text
Context Declared
        ↓
Evidence Basis Qualified (read-only)
        ↓
Constraints Bound
        ↓
Learning Identified
        ↓
Learning Preserved (provenance · attribution · Scope · uncertainty)
        ↓
Learning Validated
        ↓
Learning Qualified
        ↓
Review Eligible
        ↓
Evolved / Superseded / Retired — or — Closed or Escalated
        ↓
Audited Record Preserved
```

Lifecycle meanings are architectural:

- **Context Declared:** purpose, Scope, authority, organization, Tenant, and accountability are explicit.
- **Evidence Basis Qualified:** eligible governed evidence and Memory references are confirmed read-only.
- **Constraints Bound:** applicable Governance, Security, privacy, and classification limits are attached.
- **Learning Identified/Preserved:** an attributable learning is recognized and preserved with provenance and uncertainty.
- **Learning Validated/Qualified:** eligibility, provenance, Scope, explanation, and boundaries are assessed; the learning becomes attributable, non-authoritative, and reviewable.
- **Evolved/Superseded/Retired:** learning changes with preserved history, never rewriting Memory or the record.
- **Closed or Escalated:** lifecycle evidence is preserved without triggering action.

## Lifecycle Rules

- **P44-OL-054 — No Automatic Progression:** No lifecycle state advances automatically through confidence, availability, or completion.
- **P44-OL-055 — Context Before Evidence:** Evidence qualification must not precede a complete declared context.
- **P44-OL-056 — Constraints Before Identification:** Applicable constraints must be bound before learning identification.
- **P44-OL-057 — Evidence Before Learning:** A learning must not precede eligible evidence basis qualification.
- **P44-OL-058 — Validation Before Qualification:** No learning is qualified before required validation completes.
- **P44-OL-059 — Review Non-approval:** Review eligibility must not become Human Approval, Governance approval, Decision, or Authority.
- **P44-OL-060 — Evolution History:** Evolution, supersession, and retirement must preserve prior learning history and provenance.
- **P44-OL-061 — Closure Non-action:** Lifecycle closure or escalation must not trigger Memory admission, Workflow, Runtime, automation, or execution.
