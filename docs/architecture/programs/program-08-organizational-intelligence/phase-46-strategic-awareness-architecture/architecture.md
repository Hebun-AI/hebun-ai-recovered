# 1. Executive Summary

Strategic Awareness is the constitutionally bounded Enterprise capability that produces governed awareness of the Enterprise's strategic position, state, health, readiness, opportunity, risk, alignment, drift, and dependencies — without becoming Governance, Decision, Authority, or action.

Phase 46 establishes the Strategic Awareness identity, strategic context, state, health, readiness, opportunity, risk, alignment, drift, dependencies, signals, assessment, lifecycle, and governance boundaries — while preserving Phase 44 Organizational Learning, Phase 45 Organizational Optimization Intelligence, Enterprise Memory, Knowledge, Program VI Reasoning/Evidence/Decision Intelligence, Program IV Work, Program V Security, Program VII Computer Use, Enterprise Governance, Runtime, Human Architecture, and Director Authority ownership.

Strategic Awareness is:

- perceptual and assessive — it makes the Enterprise's strategic condition visible but commands nothing;
- derived read-only from Phase 44 learnings, Phase 45 optimization, governed evidence, and eligible references;
- purpose- and Scope-bound;
- explainable, traceable, and uncertainty-preserving;
- non-governing, non-decisional, non-executing;
- fail-closed;
- Human-supremacy-preserving and Director-authority-preserving;
- technology, vendor, model, Runtime, infrastructure, product, and implementation independent.

Strategic Awareness surfaces signals and assessments for eligible Human and Director consideration; it never enforces Policy, decides, approves, or acts. Awareness of a risk is not authority to mitigate it; awareness of an opportunity is not authority to pursue it.

Phase 46 does not define Strategic Enterprise Evolution (Phase 47). It establishes the awareness layer whose attributable outputs may become eligible inputs to that future architecture after its separate Director gate.

## Executive Rules

# 2. Architectural Purpose

Strategic Awareness exists to provide a stable constitutional layer through which the Enterprise can perceive its own strategic condition without allowing awareness, signal, or assessment to become governance, decision, authority, or action.

Its purpose is to:

- establish Strategic Awareness as a constitutional perceptual capability rather than a monitoring engine or alerting system;
- assess enterprise strategic position, state, health, readiness, opportunity, risk, alignment, drift, and dependencies;
- derive signals and assessments from eligible learnings, optimization outputs, and governed evidence read-only;
- preserve provenance, attribution, Scope, uncertainty, and limitation for every signal and assessment;
- provide attributable, non-authoritative awareness outputs to eligible Humans, the Director, and separately governed downstream phases;
- preserve Human review, Human Approval, Human Override, and Director-reserved authority.

Strategic Awareness answers: **what attributable awareness of the Enterprise's strategic position, change, risk, and opportunity can be assessed from eligible inputs within one bounded awareness context — without governing, deciding, or acting?**

It does not answer what Policy must change, what must be decided, what must be governed, or what must be executed.

## Purpose Rules

- **P46-SA-005 — Declared Purpose:** Every awareness context must declare an eligible purpose and Scope before assessment begins.
- **P46-SA-006 — Evidence-derived:** Strategic Awareness must derive only from eligible Phase 44 learnings, Phase 45 optimization outputs, governed evidence, and references, never from fabrication.
- **P46-SA-007 — Signal Non-command:** A strategic signal is an attributable observation, not a command, alert-to-act, Decision, or obligation.
- **P46-SA-008 — Assessment Non-decision:** A strategic assessment informs judgment; it is not a Decision, Approval, or Governance outcome.
- **P46-SA-009 — Future-phase Separation:** Phase 46 must not define Strategic Enterprise Evolution.

# 3. Design Principles

## Human Constitutional Supremacy

Human agency, judgment, responsibility, review, Approval, and Override remain constitutionally available. Awareness quality, signal strength, or assessed urgency cannot replace them.

## Director Authority

The Director retains final constitutional Authority for reserved matters, constitutional interpretation, amendments, and roadmap governance. No awareness output becomes Director authority.

## Awareness Is Not Governance

Strategic Awareness makes the enterprise's strategic condition visible; it never sets, enforces, waives, approves, or interprets Policy or Governance.

## Awareness Is Not Decision or Authority

Signals and assessments inform judgment; they do not become Decision, Decision authority, Approval, Recommendation-as-command, or Permission.

## Awareness Is Not Action

Perceiving a risk, opportunity, or drift creates no authority or obligation to mitigate, pursue, correct, execute, or automate.

## Evidence and Provenance

Every signal and assessment must trace to eligible inputs with preserved source, attribution, version, and Scope.

## Explainability and Uncertainty

Every material signal and assessment must preserve sufficient explanation of its basis, assumptions, limitations, and uncertainty for constitutional review, without exposing protected information.

## Attribution and Accountability

Every context, signal, assessment, and validation act must remain attributable to an eligible authority, actor, purpose, and Scope.

## Least Authority

Awareness capability, evidence access, signal strength, assessed urgency, or usefulness creates no undeclared Authority or Permission.

## Fail-Closed Awareness

Missing or incompatible Identity, Authority, Scope, provenance, classification, Permission, evidence eligibility, or required review prevents a valid awareness output.

## Independence

Strategic Awareness remains technology, model, method, vendor, Runtime, infrastructure, product, and implementation independent.

## No False Precision

Scores, indices, thresholds, or confidence must not conceal uncertainty or imply truth, decision, governance, or obligation.

## Principle Rules

- **P46-SA-014 — Awareness Non-action:** Awareness must not mitigate, pursue, correct, reorganize, execute, or automate.
- **P46-SA-015 — Provenance Required:** Every signal and assessment must preserve source inputs, attribution, version, and Scope.
- **P46-SA-016 — Explainability Required:** Every material signal and assessment must expose sufficient constitutional explanation and uncertainty.

# 4. Architecture

The constitutional Strategic Awareness model is:

```text
Declared Strategic Awareness Context (purpose · Scope · authority basis · organization · Tenant)
        +
Eligible Phase 44 Learnings + Phase 45 Optimization Outputs + Governed Evidence (read-only) + eligible references
        +
Applicable Governance, Security, privacy, and classification constraints
        ↓
Strategic Signal Detection (attributable observations of position, change, risk, opportunity, drift, dependency)
        ↓
Strategic Assessment (attributable evaluation of state, health, readiness, alignment — with uncertainty)
        ↓
Strategic Awareness Validation (eligibility, provenance, Scope, explanation, boundary checks)
        ↓
Qualified Strategic Awareness Output (attributable, non-authoritative, reviewable)
        ↓
Eligible Human review, Director consideration, or downstream Organizational Intelligence consumption
```

This model is an architectural relationship, not a Workflow, monitoring engine, alerting pipeline, Governance process, Runtime sequence, or implementation.

No downstream consumer is compelled to accept a signal or assessment. Consumption remains separately governed by that consumer's eligibility, Authority, purpose, Scope, and lifecycle. A qualified awareness output is attributable perception and assessment — not Governance, not a Decision, not a command.

## Strategic Awareness Ontology

The Strategic Awareness ontology is constitutional, not a data schema. It distinguishes, at minimum:

- **Strategic Context** — the bounded strategic matter and boundary of one awareness context;
- **Strategic State** — the attributable current condition of the Enterprise within Scope;
- **Strategic Health** — an attributable assessment of soundness and sustainability;
- **Strategic Readiness** — an attributable assessment of preparedness relative to declared position;
- **Strategic Opportunity** — an attributable perceived possibility, without authority to pursue;
- **Strategic Risk** — an attributable perceived exposure, without authority to mitigate;
- **Strategic Alignment** — an attributable assessment of coherence with declared intent;
- **Strategic Drift** — an attributable perceived divergence from declared intent or position;
- **Strategic Dependency** — an attributable relationship on which strategic condition depends;
- **Strategic Signal** — an attributable observation contributing to awareness;
- **Strategic Assessment** — an attributable, uncertainty-preserving evaluation over signals.

The ontology defines constitutional meaning and boundaries only; it prescribes no representation, storage, schema, metric, index, or threshold.

## Strategic Awareness Eligibility and Governance

An awareness output is eligible only when its context, basis, provenance, classification, Permission, and Scope are valid. Governance constraints remain active and immutable; awareness consumes them and never sets, approves, waives, enforces, or interprets them.

## Architecture Rules

- **P46-SA-022 — Declared Context Required:** Awareness requires one complete declared context before signal detection.
- **P46-SA-023 — Eligible Basis Required:** Only eligible learnings, optimization outputs, and governed evidence may ground awareness.
- **P46-SA-024 — Constraint Applicability:** Applicable Governance, Security, privacy, classification, and Tenant constraints must remain active and immutable.
- **P46-SA-025 — Signal Before Assessment:** Strategic signal detection must precede strategic assessment.
- **P46-SA-026 — Validation Before Qualification:** No awareness output is qualified before eligibility, provenance, Scope, explanation, and boundary validation complete.
- **P46-SA-027 — Ontology Non-schema:** The Strategic Awareness ontology defines constitutional meaning only and prescribes no representation, storage, schema, metric, index, or threshold.
- **P46-SA-028 — Consumer Independence:** A qualified awareness output must not compel Human, Director, Governance, Organizational Intelligence, or any consumer.
- **P46-SA-029 — Governance Immutability:** Awareness must not set, approve, waive, enforce, interpret, or rewrite Governance constraints.
- **P46-SA-030 — Read-only Basis:** Awareness must consume inputs read-only, preserving their identity, ownership, and provenance.
- **P46-SA-031 — Risk and Opportunity Non-authority:** Perceiving a risk or opportunity creates no authority or obligation to mitigate or pursue it.

# 8. Strategic Awareness Lifecycle

The constitutional lifecycle is:

```text
Context Declared
        ↓
Basis Qualified (Phase 44 learnings + Phase 45 optimization + governed evidence, read-only)
        ↓
Constraints Bound
        ↓
Strategic Signals Detected
        ↓
Strategic Assessment Produced (state · health · readiness · opportunity · risk · alignment · drift · dependencies)
        ↓
Output Validated
        ↓
Output Qualified
        ↓
Review Eligible
        ↓
Closed or Escalated
        ↓
Audited Record Preserved
```

Lifecycle meanings are architectural:

- **Context Declared:** purpose, Scope, authority, organization, Tenant, and accountability are explicit.
- **Basis Qualified:** eligible learnings, optimization outputs, and governed evidence are confirmed read-only.
- **Constraints Bound:** applicable Governance, Security, privacy, and classification limits are attached.
- **Signals Detected / Assessment Produced:** attributable signals and assessments are produced with uncertainty.
- **Validated / Qualified:** eligibility, provenance, Scope, explanation, and boundaries are assessed; output becomes attributable, non-authoritative, reviewable.
- **Closed or Escalated:** lifecycle evidence is preserved without triggering governance or action.

## Lifecycle Rules

- **P46-SA-054 — No Automatic Progression:** No lifecycle state advances automatically through signal strength, assessed urgency, availability, or completion.
- **P46-SA-055 — Context Before Basis:** Basis qualification must not precede a complete declared context.
- **P46-SA-056 — Constraints Before Signals:** Applicable constraints must be bound before signal detection.
- **P46-SA-057 — Basis Before Signals:** Signal detection must not precede eligible basis qualification.
- **P46-SA-058 — Signals Before Assessment:** Assessment must not precede signal detection.
- **P46-SA-059 — Validation Before Qualification:** No output is qualified before required validation completes.
- **P46-SA-060 — Review Non-approval:** Review eligibility must not become Human Approval, Governance approval, Decision, or Authority.
- **P46-SA-061 — Closure Non-action:** Lifecycle closure or escalation must not trigger Governance, Work change, Runtime, automation, or execution.
