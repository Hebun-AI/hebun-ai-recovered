# Purpose

Program VI exists to govern the constitutional architecture of Enterprise Reasoning, Enterprise Evidence, and Decision Intelligence across the Enterprise.

Without Program-level governance, future reasoning architecture could silently:

- treat model output, inference, confidence, consensus, or computation as constitutional truth;
- merge evidence with knowledge, context, observation, reasoning, or decision;
- convert reasoning into Decision, Approval, Authority, Recommendation, or Execution;
- redefine the published Reasoning Engine, Query Intelligence, Governance Intelligence, or Decision Support boundaries;
- weaken Human judgment, Director authority, provenance, uncertainty, contradiction, or dissent;
- absorb Knowledge, Governance, Security, Runtime, Computer Use, Organizational Intelligence, or Intellectual Evolution responsibilities;
- embed models, prompts, retrieval, tools, vendors, or infrastructure into constitutional meaning.

This Constitution establishes a durable governance envelope under which later phases may define their assigned architecture without transferring ownership, authority, or implementation responsibility.

# Architectural Principles

## Enterprise Constitution Supremacy

Every Program VI artifact remains subordinate to the Enterprise Constitution and Director authority.

## Human Primacy

Reasoning exists to support eligible Human and organizational judgment. It must not erase agency, responsibility, dissent, review, Approval, or Override.

## Director Authority Preservation

No reasoning result, evidence finding, Decision Intelligence output, consensus, confidence, model output, Governance outcome, or technical capability may become Director authority.

## Reasoning–Decision Separation

Reasoning evaluates and explains. It does not become Decision, Decision Support authority, Approval, Recommendation, command, or Execution.

## Evidence–Judgment Separation

Evidence informs reasoning and judgment. It does not become truth, conclusion, Decision, Approval, or Authority automatically.

## Knowledge–Evidence Separation

Knowledge and Evidence retain distinct identities, eligibility, provenance, Scope, and lifecycle. Neither becomes the other by use in reasoning.

## Governance Separation

Governance evaluates constitutional eligibility and constraints. Reasoning cannot approve, waive, enforce, or replace Governance.

## Program Isolation

Program VI must not absorb responsibilities assigned to another Program. Dependencies are directional and non-owning.

## Enterprise-First Design

Enterprise meaning, responsibility, evidence, authority, and constitutional boundaries precede reasoning technique or realization.

## Least Authority

Reasoning capability, information access, confidence, consensus, urgency, role, or technical feasibility creates no undeclared Authority or Permission.

## Evidence and Provenance

Every material input, transformation, inference, claim, conflict, uncertainty, and output must remain attributable, Scope-bound, versioned, and traceable to eligible sources.

## Explainability and Reproducibility

Reasoning architecture must preserve sufficient declared context, evidence relationships, assumptions, transformations, limitations, and uncertainty for constitutional explanation and reproducibility without exposing protected information or prescribing implementation.

## Ambiguity and Dissent Preservation

Missing context, uncertainty, contradiction, alternative interpretation, and dissent must remain explicit. Ambiguity never defaults to truth, Permission, or acceptance.

## Fail-Closed Governance

Missing Identity, Authority, Scope, provenance, classification, Permission, Policy, evidence eligibility, or required review blocks constitutional progression and requires restriction, deferral, or escalation.

## Technology and Model Independence

No Program VI meaning may depend on a model, method, vendor, framework, protocol, platform, database, interface, processor, or computing paradigm.

## Runtime and Implementation Independence

Program VI may define constitutional architecture consumable by Runtime, but it must not define Runtime behavior, algorithms, services, infrastructure, deployment, or implementation.

## Evolution Without Rewrite

Models, methods, technologies, organizations, evidence sources, and implementations may evolve without silently rewriting Program VI constitutional meaning.
