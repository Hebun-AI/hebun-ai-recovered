# 1. Executive Summary

Enterprise Evidence is a constitutionally governed evidentiary role assumed by an eligible Enterprise asset within a declared reasoning context. The asset retains its original identity, ownership, provenance, classification, version, and lifecycle. Evidentiary participation is contextual; it is not an identity transformation and does not convert Data, Information, Knowledge, Memory, Context, observation, assertion, or reasoning output into a new information type.

Phase 38 establishes the constitutional architecture for Evidence identity, ontology, sources, eligibility, provenance, authenticity, integrity, Trust, Context, relationships, lifecycle, Governance, consumption, and traceability. It enables Enterprise Reasoning to consume eligible Evidence while preserving the published ownership of Enterprise Knowledge, Enterprise Memory, Enterprise Context, Governance, Security, Director Decision, Runtime, and Phase 37 Enterprise Reasoning.

Enterprise Evidence:

- supports Reasoning but never replaces Reasoning;
- informs Judgment but never replaces Judgment;
- may support future Decision Intelligence but never defines it;
- preserves Human constitutional supremacy and final Director Authority;
- remains purpose-, Scope-, Tenant-, organization-, time-, and reasoning-context-bound;
- remains technology, vendor, model, Runtime, infrastructure, product, and implementation independent.

## Executive Rules

- **P38-DC-001 — Constitutional Enterprise Evidence:** Enterprise Evidence must remain subordinate to the Enterprise Constitution, Human constitutional supremacy, and Director Authority.
- **P38-DC-002 — Evidentiary Role:** Enterprise Evidence must remain a contextual constitutional role of an eligible Enterprise asset and must not become a new information identity or type.
- **P38-DC-003 — Evidence Non-authority:** Evidence, eligibility, authenticity, integrity, Trust, quantity, agreement, or presentation must not create Truth, Judgment, Decision, Approval, Permission, Authority, or Execution eligibility.
- **P38-DC-004 — Non-implementation:** Phase 38 must not define storage, retrieval, databases, indexes, models, prompts, tools, Runtime behavior, infrastructure, products, or implementation.

# 2. Architectural Purpose

Enterprise Evidence exists to provide constitutionally governed evidentiary support for Enterprise Reasoning without allowing availability, relevance, confidence, consensus, or technical accessibility to become Evidence automatically.

Its purpose is to:

- establish one stable constitutional meaning for Enterprise Evidence;
- qualify evidentiary participation within a declared Enterprise Reasoning Context;
- preserve original asset identity, ownership, provenance, lifecycle, classification, and version;
- distinguish eligibility from admission, use, weight, conclusion, Truth, and Authority;
- preserve supporting, conflicting, limiting, excluded, and insufficient Evidence;
- make Evidence use attributable, traceable, reviewable, and explainable;
- protect Human Judgment and Director Decision boundaries;
- reserve Decision Intelligence for Phase 39;
- prevent Evidence architecture from becoming Reasoning, Governance, Runtime, storage, retrieval, or implementation.

Enterprise Evidence answers: **which eligible Enterprise assets may constitutionally participate in a declared evidentiary relationship, for what purpose and Scope, under which provenance, integrity, Trust, Context, Governance, and lifecycle constraints?**

It does not answer what conclusion must be reached, what a Human must judge, what the Director must decide, or what Runtime must execute.

## Purpose Rules

- **P38-DC-005 — Declared Evidentiary Purpose:** Every Evidence relationship must have a declared and eligible reasoning purpose.
- **P38-DC-006 — Contextual Participation:** Evidentiary participation must remain bounded to the exact reasoning Context for which eligibility was established.
- **P38-DC-007 — Original Identity Preservation:** An Enterprise asset must retain its canonical identity while participating as Evidence.
- **P38-DC-008 — Human Judgment Preservation:** Enterprise Evidence must support but never substitute for eligible Human Judgment.
- **P38-DC-009 — Future-phase Separation:** Phase 38 must not define Decision Intelligence, Decision Confidence, Decision Recommendation, Decision Support, or Decision Explanation.

# 4. Architectural Principles

The following constitutional relationships are explicit and non-substitutable:

- Evidence supports Reasoning.
- Reasoning consumes Evidence.
- Decision Intelligence consumes Reasoning.
- Human Judgment remains supreme.
- Director Authority remains final.

## Human Constitutional Supremacy

Human agency, Judgment, responsibility, disagreement, review, Approval, and Override remain constitutionally available wherever required. Evidence quality or quantity cannot replace them.

## Director Authority

The Director retains final constitutional Authority for committing Director Decisions, reserved interpretation, amendments, and roadmap governance.

## Role Without Identity Transformation

Evidence is a contextual role, not an information type. Eligibility and participation never transform the identity of the participating asset.

## Provenance Preservation

Every Evidence relationship preserves source, origin, attribution, custody where constitutionally relevant, version, time, Scope, ownership, transformations, and lifecycle.

## Authenticity and Integrity Separation

Authenticity concerns attributable origin and identity correspondence. Integrity concerns preserved correspondence and freedom from unauthorized alteration. Neither establishes Truth, relevance, eligibility, or sufficient support.

## Contextual Eligibility

Evidence eligibility is established only for a declared reasoning Context, purpose, Scope, organization, Tenant, time, classification, authority boundary, and evidentiary role.

## Trust Non-intrinsic

Evidence Trust qualifies a declared evidentiary relationship. Trust is not intrinsic to the asset and cannot replace provenance, authenticity, integrity, eligibility, or Human Judgment.

## Contradiction and Limitation Preservation

Supporting, conflicting, limiting, excluded, unavailable, and insufficient Evidence remain independently visible and traceable.

## Least Authority and Fail-Closed Evidence

Evidence receives no Authority beyond its bounded evidentiary role. Missing or incompatible Identity, provenance, Permission, classification, Context, integrity, or Governance prevents eligible participation.

## Independence

Enterprise Evidence remains technology, vendor, model, Runtime, infrastructure, storage, product, and implementation independent.

## Principle Rules

- **P38-DC-014 — Human Supremacy:** Evidence must not erase or automate Human Judgment, dissent, Approval, responsibility, or Override.
- **P38-DC-015 — Director Reservation:** Evidence must not decide, pre-approve, constrain, or replace a Director Decision.
- **P38-DC-016 — Role Preservation:** Evidence participation must not transform the identity, ownership, lifecycle, or constitutional meaning of the source asset.
- **P38-DC-017 — Provenance Required:** Evidence without sufficient source, attribution, version, Scope, and lifecycle provenance is ineligible.
- **P38-DC-018 — Authenticity Non-truth:** Authenticity must not be interpreted as Truth, correctness, sufficiency, or Decision fitness.
- **P38-DC-019 — Integrity Non-truth:** Integrity must not be interpreted as Truth, relevance, Trust, or sufficient evidentiary support.
- **P38-DC-020 — Contextual Eligibility Required:** Eligibility must be established independently for each declared reasoning Context.
- **P38-DC-021 — Trust Relationship:** Evidence Trust must qualify a declared relationship and must not become an intrinsic property of an Enterprise asset.
- **P38-DC-022 — Independence Required:** No technology, vendor, model, Runtime, infrastructure, storage system, product, or implementation may define Enterprise Evidence meaning.

```text
Eligible Enterprise Asset
        ↓
Declared Enterprise Evidence Context
        ↓
Evidence Eligibility Evaluation
        ↓
Contextual Evidence Relationship
        ↓
Enterprise Reasoning Consumption
```

Every participating asset retains its canonical identity throughout this relationship.

The model distinguishes:

| Constitutional meaning | Meaning preserved |
|---|---|
| Candidate asset | An asset considered for a declared evidentiary purpose |
| Eligible asset | A candidate satisfying applicable contextual conditions |
| Evidence relationship | The exact role linking an eligible asset to a premise or claim |
| Evidence use | Read-only Reasoning consumption of that relationship |
| Evidence finding | A bounded observation about eligibility, integrity, conflict, or limitation |

No step creates Truth, Knowledge, Judgment, Reasoning, Decision Intelligence, Approval, Authority, Runtime admission, or Execution.

## Model Rules

- **P38-DC-034 — Eligibility Before Participation:** An asset must satisfy applicable Evidence eligibility before contextual evidentiary participation.

- **P38-DC-036 — No Automatic Evidentiary Weight:** Eligibility, availability, repetition, confidence, consensus, or source status must not automatically establish weight or sufficiency.
- **P38-DC-037 — Consumer Independence:** Evidence relationships must not compel Enterprise Reasoning, Human Judgment, Governance, Decision Intelligence, Decision Support, Director Decision, Runtime, or any other consumer.

# 7. Enterprise Evidence Lifecycle

The Enterprise Evidence Lifecycle is constitutional meaning, not Workflow or Runtime progression.

1. **Candidacy**  
   An attributable Enterprise asset is identified for a declared evidentiary purpose while retaining its original identity.

2. **Context Declaration**  
   Purpose, reasoning relationship, Scope, subject, organization, Tenant, time, classification, Authority basis, constraints, and accountability are declared.

3. **Qualification**  
   Identity, ownership, provenance, version, lifecycle, accessibility, and applicable Governance and Security constraints are evaluated.

4. **Eligibility Determination**  
   Authenticity, integrity, Permission, classification, Context fit, limitations, and required review determine whether participation is constitutionally eligible.

5. **Contextual Admission**  
   An eligible asset is admitted only into the declared Evidence Context and exact evidentiary role.

6. **Evidentiary Participation**  
   Enterprise Reasoning may consume the admitted Evidence relationship read-only while preserving all boundaries and limitations.

7. **Review and Challenge**  
   Eligibility, provenance, authenticity, integrity, Trust, role, conflict, insufficiency, and use may be reviewed or challenged without rewriting history.

8. **Restriction or Exclusion**  
   Invalid, expired, unauthorized, compromised, mis-scoped, or insufficient participation is restricted or excluded with attributable rationale.

9. **Contextual Closure**  
   The evidentiary relationship closes for the declared Context while its trace, findings, challenges, and limitations remain constitutionally preservable.

Eligibility may be reassessed when Context, source version, provenance, classification, Permission, Policy, lifecycle, authenticity, integrity, or material limitations change. Reassessment does not rewrite prior constitutional history.

## Lifecycle Rules

- **P38-DC-038 — No Automatic Progression:** No lifecycle meaning advances automatically through availability, confidence, technical state, elapsed time, or prior use.
- **P38-DC-039 — Context Before Qualification:** Qualification must not precede a complete Evidence Context.
- **P38-DC-040 — Qualification Before Eligibility:** Evidentiary eligibility must not be established before source and constitutional qualification.
- **P38-DC-041 — Eligibility Before Admission:** Contextual admission must not precede a valid eligibility determination.
- **P38-DC-042 — Admission Non-universality:** Admission in one Context must not imply eligibility or admission in another Context.
- **P38-DC-043 — Review Non-approval:** Evidence review or challenge must not become Human Approval, Governance Approval, Decision, or Authority.
- **P38-DC-044 — Closure Non-execution:** Restriction, exclusion, closure, or escalation must not trigger Workflow, Runtime behavior, communication protocol, or Execution.

# 9. Provenance, Authenticity, Integrity, and Trust

## Provenance

Evidence Provenance must preserve the attributable relationship from the source asset through every constitutionally relevant version, transformation, custody boundary, classification, Context, eligibility finding, evidentiary use, challenge, restriction, and closure.

Missing lineage must remain visible. Provenance must not be fabricated, inferred as fact, or silently repaired.

## Authenticity

Authenticity evaluates whether an asset constitutionally corresponds to its represented source, identity, and attribution. Authenticity may be supported, challenged, unresolved, or invalid. It does not establish Truth, correctness, relevance, integrity, or sufficiency.

## Integrity

Integrity preserves correspondence across identity, content, provenance, version, authorized transformation, Context, classification, constraints, and evidentiary role. An intact asset may still be false, irrelevant, insufficient, or ineligible.

## Trust

Evidence Trust qualifies a declared evidentiary relationship using applicable provenance, authenticity, integrity, Governance, Context, known limitations, and challenges. It is bounded, attributable, revisable, and non-intrinsic. Trust never replaces validation or Human Judgment.

## Provenance, Authenticity, Integrity, and Trust Rules

- **P38-DC-052 — End-to-end Provenance:** Every evidentiary use must trace to source identity, ownership, attribution, version, time, Context, transformations, eligibility basis, and lifecycle.
- **P38-DC-053 — Transformation Visibility:** Every material transformation affecting evidentiary interpretation must be attributable and independently visible.
- **P38-DC-054 — Authenticity Qualification:** Authenticity findings must expose their basis, Scope, limitations, challenges, and uncertainty.
- **P38-DC-055 — Integrity Preservation:** Unauthorized alteration, broken correspondence, unexplained transformation, or lost version lineage makes the affected relationship ineligible.
- **P38-DC-056 — Trust Reassessment:** Material changes to provenance, authenticity, integrity, Context, Governance, or limitations require Evidence Trust reassessment.
- **P38-DC-057 — No Trust Inheritance:** Trust from a source, owner, prior Context, prior use, or related asset must not transfer automatically to a new Evidence relationship.
- **P38-DC-058 — Finding Non-truth:** Provenance, authenticity, integrity, or Trust findings must not certify Truth, correctness, conclusion, Judgment, or Decision.

# 10. Evidence Relationships and Consumption

An Evidence relationship is directional and must identify its exact role relative to a declared premise or claim.

Permissible constitutional roles include:

- **supporting** — provides bounded support;
- **conflicting** — presents material inconsistency or opposition;
- **limiting** — constrains interpretation, Scope, applicability, or confidence;
- **contextualizing** — provides eligible context without becoming the claim;
- **corroborating** — independently supports an already expressed relationship;
- **insufficient** — is relevant but inadequate for the declared evidentiary purpose;
- **excluded** — was considered but is constitutionally ineligible, with rationale preserved.

These roles do not prescribe reasoning methods, weights, scoring, aggregation, or conclusions.

Enterprise Reasoning consumes eligible Evidence relationships read-only. Consumption must preserve:

- original asset identity and ownership;
- exact Context and evidentiary role;
- provenance, authenticity, integrity, Trust, version, and lifecycle;
- supporting and conflicting relationships;
- limitations, uncertainty, exclusion, and insufficiency;
- Governance, Security, privacy, Tenant, classification, and disclosure boundaries.

## Relationship and Consumption Rules

- **P38-DC-059 — Directional Relationship:** Every Evidence relationship must identify the eligible asset, exact premise or claim, contextual role, and direction.
- **P38-DC-060 — Role Non-conclusion:** Supporting, conflicting, limiting, contextualizing, corroborating, insufficient, or excluded status must not itself establish a conclusion.
- **P38-DC-061 — Conflict Preservation:** Conflicting Evidence must remain independently attributable and must not be silently removed, merged, or resolved.
- **P38-DC-062 — Exclusion Traceability:** Excluded Evidence must preserve its identity, exclusion reason, authority basis, and affected Context without prohibited disclosure.
- **P38-DC-063 — Consumption Non-mutation:** Enterprise Reasoning must not rewrite, fabricate, delete, reclassify, or alter Evidence or its provenance through consumption.
- **P38-DC-064 — Consumption Non-compulsion:** Evidence availability, quantity, agreement, ranking, or Trust must not compel a reasoning conclusion, Human Judgment, Decision, or action.

# 12. Traceability, Context, and Validation

## Traceability

Every Evidence relationship must remain traceable from the source asset to:

- canonical identity and owner;
- provenance, attribution, version, and time;
- declared Evidence Context and purpose;
- eligibility basis and applicable constraints;
- authenticity, integrity, and Trust findings;
- exact supporting, conflicting, limiting, contextualizing, corroborating, insufficient, or excluded role;
- Reasoning consumption;
- review, challenge, restriction, exclusion, and closure.

## Context

Evidence eligibility and meaning are contextual. Context must preserve organization, Tenant, subject, purpose, Scope, time, classification, Authority basis, constraints, reasoning relationship, and accountability. Relevance in one Context does not establish eligibility in another.

## Validation

Evidence Validation evaluates constitutional correspondence and boundary compliance. A valid result means only that the examined Evidence relationship satisfies the declared validation criteria within its Scope. It does not certify Truth, correctness, sufficiency, Reasoning validity, Judgment, Decision, Authority, or Execution eligibility.

Validation must fail closed when required identity, provenance, authenticity, integrity, Permission, classification, Context, Governance, traceability, or lifecycle evidence is missing, invalid, expired, contradictory, or ambiguous.

## Traceability, Context, and Validation Rules

- **P38-DC-071 — Trace Completeness:** Every evidentiary use must preserve sufficient traceability for constitutional review without bypassing protected disclosure boundaries.
- **P38-DC-072 — Context Non-transferability:** Evidence eligibility, role, Trust, or validation in one Context must not transfer automatically to another.
- **P38-DC-073 — Version Specificity:** Evidentiary participation must identify the exact eligible source version and must not silently follow later revisions.
- **P38-DC-074 — Limitation Visibility:** Material limitations, missing lineage, uncertainty, contradiction, challenge, and insufficiency must remain explicit.
- **P38-DC-075 — Validation Scope:** Evidence Validation must state its exact criteria, Scope, Context, time, accountable boundary, findings, and limitations.
- **P38-DC-076 — Validation Non-sufficiency:** Successful validation must not establish evidentiary sufficiency, reasoning correctness, Truth, Judgment, or Decision.
- **P38-DC-077 — Protected Traceability:** Traceability and explanation must preserve privacy, consent, classification, Tenant, Permission, retention, and disclosure constraints.
