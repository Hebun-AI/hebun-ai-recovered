# 1. Executive Summary

Enterprise Decision Intelligence is the constitutionally governed decision-support capability that consumes qualified Enterprise Reasoning and eligible Enterprise Evidence relationships to prepare attributable, explainable, uncertainty-preserving decision material without becoming a Decision, Decision Authority, Human Judgment, Governance, Approval, Workflow, Runtime behavior, Automation, or Execution.

Phase 39 establishes Decision Intelligence identity, ontology, recommendations, alternatives, constraints, confidence representation, uncertainty communication, explanation, rationale, provenance, lifecycle, Governance participation, Human interaction boundaries, and constitutional decision constraints.

Decision Intelligence:

- consumes Enterprise Reasoning without redefining it;
- preserves Enterprise Evidence provenance without owning Evidence;
- communicates uncertainty without concealing ambiguity, conflict, or insufficiency;
- explains recommendations without compelling acceptance;
- supports Human Judgment without performing or replacing it;
- remains subordinate to final Director Authority and applicable Enterprise Governance;
- remains technology, vendor, model, Runtime, infrastructure, product, and implementation independent.

## Executive Rules

- **P39-DC-001 — Constitutional Decision Intelligence:** Decision Intelligence must remain subordinate to the Enterprise Constitution, Human constitutional supremacy, and Director Authority.
- **P39-DC-002 — Decision Intelligence Non-decision:** No Decision Intelligence input, analysis, recommendation, confidence representation, explanation, rationale, or output may become a Decision or Decision Authority.
- **P39-DC-003 — Upstream Preservation:** Decision Intelligence must consume qualified Reasoning and eligible Evidence without changing their identity, ownership, provenance, integrity, or lifecycle.
- **P39-DC-004 — Non-implementation:** Phase 39 must not define models, prompts, algorithms, tools, Runtime behavior, infrastructure, products, or implementation.

# 2. Architectural Purpose

Decision Intelligence exists to prepare constitutionally bounded decision material so eligible Humans and established decision authorities can evaluate alternatives without allowing analysis, confidence, recommendation, consensus, or technical capability to become Authority.

Its purpose is to:

- establish one stable constitutional meaning for Enterprise Decision Intelligence;
- transform qualified Reasoning outputs into bounded decision-support material without re-performing Reasoning;
- preserve eligible Evidence relationships, provenance, conflicts, limitations, and exclusions;
- make alternatives, constraints, recommendations, uncertainty, confidence, explanation, and rationale explicit;
- preserve applicable Governance and Security boundaries;
- support Human Judgment and Director Decision without replacing either;
- preserve the published Decision Support architecture without absorbing its authority or package ownership;
- prevent Decision Intelligence from becoming Planning, Workflow, Runtime, Automation, or Execution.

Decision Intelligence answers: **what constitutionally supportable decision material can be presented from qualified Reasoning and eligible Evidence within a declared decision-support context?**

It does not answer what a Human must judge, what the Director must decide, what Governance must approve, or what Runtime must execute.

## Purpose Rules

- **P39-DC-005 — Declared Purpose:** Every Decision Intelligence context must declare an eligible decision-support purpose before material preparation begins.
- **P39-DC-006 — Scope Preservation:** Decision Intelligence must preserve the authorized objective and Scope and must not silently broaden, replace, or optimize them.
- **P39-DC-007 — Human Judgment Preservation:** Decision Intelligence must support but never perform, automate, constrain, or substitute for Human Judgment.
- **P39-DC-008 — Director Authority Preservation:** Decision Intelligence must not make, infer, pre-approve, compel, or constrain a Director Decision.
- **P39-DC-009 — Governance Preservation:** Decision Intelligence must not create, amend, waive, approve, enforce, or replace Governance decisions or outcomes.

# 4. Architectural Principles

The following constitutional relationships are explicit and non-substitutable:

- Decision Intelligence ≠ Decision.
- Decision Intelligence ≠ Recommendation.
- Decision Intelligence ≠ Decision Authority.
- Decision Intelligence ≠ Human Judgment.
- Decision Intelligence ≠ Governance.
- Decision Intelligence ≠ Workflow.
- Decision Intelligence ≠ Execution.
- Decision Intelligence ≠ Automation.
- Decision Intelligence consumes Reasoning.
- Reasoning consumes Evidence.
- Evidence preserves provenance.
- Human Judgment remains supreme.
- Director Authority remains final.

## Human Constitutional Supremacy

Human agency, Judgment, responsibility, disagreement, review, Approval, and Override remain constitutionally available wherever required. Recommendation quality or confidence cannot replace them.

## Director Authority

The Director retains final constitutional Authority for committing Director Decisions, reserved interpretation, amendments, and roadmap governance.

## Reasoning and Evidence Preservation

Decision Intelligence consumes qualified Reasoning and eligible Evidence relationships read-only. It cannot regenerate Reasoning, create Evidence, or alter their provenance, integrity, conflicts, uncertainty, or lifecycle.

## Explainable Recommendation

Every material recommendation must expose its qualified Reasoning basis, Evidence relationships, alternatives, constraints, assumptions, uncertainty, confidence basis, limitations, dissent, and rationale.

## Confidence Non-authority

Confidence is a bounded representation of support and uncertainty. It is not Truth, probability by default, Judgment, Approval, Decision, Authority, or execution eligibility.

## Uncertainty Preservation

Insufficiency, ambiguity, contradiction, variability, temporal instability, and unresolved interpretation remain explicit and independently traceable.

## Alternative Preservation

Material alternatives and their constraints, evidence, uncertainty, and tradeoffs remain visible. Recommendation does not erase non-selected alternatives.

## Least Authority and Fail-Closed Support

Decision Intelligence receives no Authority beyond preparation of bounded decision material. Missing or incompatible Identity, Scope, Reasoning qualification, Evidence eligibility, Governance, Security, provenance, or required review prevents a valid output.

## Independence

Decision Intelligence remains technology, vendor, model, Runtime, infrastructure, product, and implementation independent.

## Principle Rules

- **P39-DC-014 — Human Supremacy:** Decision Intelligence must not erase or automate Human Judgment, dissent, Approval, responsibility, or Override.
- **P39-DC-015 — Director Reservation:** Reserved Decisions, constitutional interpretation, exceptions, and amendments remain under Director Authority.
- **P39-DC-016 — Reasoning Consumption:** Only qualified Enterprise Reasoning outputs may participate as Reasoning inputs to Decision Intelligence.
- **P39-DC-017 — Evidence Provenance:** Every Evidence relationship used by Decision Intelligence must preserve Phase 38 identity, provenance, eligibility, Context, limitations, and lifecycle.
- **P39-DC-018 — Explainability Required:** A material recommendation without sufficient constitutional explanation is ineligible.
- **P39-DC-019 — Uncertainty Visibility:** Uncertainty, contradiction, insufficiency, and dissent must not be collapsed into certainty or hidden by presentation.
- **P39-DC-020 — Confidence Non-authority:** Confidence must not create Truth, Judgment, Decision, Approval, Permission, Authority, or Execution eligibility.
- **P39-DC-021 — Least Decision Intelligence Authority:** Capability, access, confidence, consensus, urgency, or usefulness must not expand Authority or Scope.

# 7. Decision Support Lifecycle

The Decision Support Lifecycle is constitutional meaning within Phase 39. It does not redefine the published Decision Support architecture and is not Workflow or Runtime progression.

1. **Context Declaration**  
   Purpose, objective, Scope, subject, organization, Tenant, time, Authority basis, accountable boundary, constraints, and eligible consumers are declared.

2. **Input Qualification**  
   Qualified Reasoning outputs, eligible Evidence relationships, applicable Governance and Security constraints, versions, provenance, and lifecycle validity are confirmed.

3. **Alternative Formation**  
   Materially supportable alternatives are expressed from qualified upstream material without re-performing Reasoning.

4. **Constraint Evaluation**  
   Applicable constitutional, Governance, Security, organizational, evidentiary, temporal, and Scope constraints are preserved for every alternative.

5. **Recommendation Qualification**  
   A non-binding recommendation may be qualified only relative to preserved alternatives, constraints, Evidence, Reasoning, uncertainty, and limitations.

6. **Confidence and Uncertainty Representation**  
   Support, consistency, sensitivity, insufficiency, ambiguity, conflict, variability, and limitations are communicated without false precision.

7. **Explanation and Rationale Qualification**  
   Material relationships among inputs, alternatives, constraints, recommendation, uncertainty, confidence, and limitations remain attributable and explainable.

8. **Governance and Boundary Validation**  
   Applicable Governance, Security, Authority, Human, Director, Decision Support, Runtime, and implementation boundaries are validated.

9. **Support Eligibility**  
   Qualified material becomes eligible for established downstream decision-support or Human review without becoming a Decision or Approval.

10. **Closure or Escalation**  
    The Context closes or communicates attributable constitutional insufficiency or conflict to an eligible review boundary without execution.

## Lifecycle Rules

- **P39-DC-038 — No Automatic Progression:** No lifecycle meaning advances automatically through confidence, completion, availability, consensus, technical state, or elapsed time.
- **P39-DC-039 — Context Before Inputs:** Input qualification must not precede a complete Decision Intelligence Context.
- **P39-DC-040 — Inputs Before Alternatives:** Alternative formation must not precede valid qualified Reasoning and eligible Evidence relationships.
- **P39-DC-041 — Alternatives Before Recommendation:** Recommendation qualification must not erase or precede materially supportable alternative formation.
- **P39-DC-042 — Uncertainty Before Eligibility:** Decision material must not become support-eligible before uncertainty and confidence bases are explicit.
- **P39-DC-043 — Review Non-approval:** Review or support eligibility must not become Human Approval, Governance Approval, Decision, or Authority.
- **P39-DC-044 — Closure Non-execution:** Closure or escalation must not trigger Workflow, Runtime behavior, Automation, communication protocol, or Execution.

# 9. Recommendations, Alternatives, and Constraints

A Decision Recommendation is non-binding decision material. It may express a constitutionally supportable course of consideration only when:

- its Context and purpose are explicit;
- qualified Reasoning and eligible Evidence relationships are traceable;
- materially supportable alternatives remain visible;
- applicable constraints are preserved per alternative;
- uncertainty, confidence basis, limitations, and dissent are explicit;
- explanation and rationale are constitutionally sufficient;
- Human Judgment and Decision Authority remain unmodified.

Alternatives must not be silently discarded, merged, ranked as Truth, or described as rejected Decisions. Recommendation selection within Decision Intelligence does not constitute organizational selection, Approval, commitment, or execution eligibility.

Constraints may restrict recommendation eligibility or interpretation. They do not themselves select an alternative, create Authority, or authorize action.

## Recommendation Rules

- **P39-DC-052 — Attributable Recommendation:** Every recommendation must identify its Context, purpose, Scope, accountable boundary, Reasoning basis, Evidence relationships, and constraints.
- **P39-DC-053 — Non-binding Recommendation:** A recommendation must remain optional decision material and must not compel acceptance, rejection, deferral, constraint, revocation, or action.
- **P39-DC-054 — Alternative Preservation:** Material alternatives and dissent must remain independently visible and traceable.
- **P39-DC-055 — Constraint Applicability:** Applicable constraints must remain attached to every affected alternative and recommendation.
- **P39-DC-056 — No Recommendation Authority:** Recommendation strength, rank, confidence, consensus, urgency, or presentation must not create Decision Authority.
- **P39-DC-057 — No Recommendation Execution:** A recommendation must not become a command, Workflow trigger, Runtime admission artifact, tool invocation, Automation instruction, or Execution request.
- **P39-DC-058 — No Hidden Optimization:** Phase 39 must not silently optimize the objective, values, risk posture, tradeoffs, or success criteria entrusted to eligible Judgment and Authority.

# 10. Decision Confidence and Uncertainty

Decision Confidence communicates bounded support and limitations associated with decision material. It must expose:

- the qualified Reasoning and eligible Evidence basis;
- Scope and Context;
- supporting and conflicting relationships;
- assumptions and constraints;
- sensitivity to material changes;
- insufficiency, ambiguity, temporal instability, and unresolved interpretation;
- the method-independent meaning and limitations of the representation.

Confidence may be qualitative, comparative, bounded, or otherwise constitutionally expressible by future implementations, but Phase 39 prescribes no scale, score, probability, aggregation, algorithm, threshold, or interface.

Decision Uncertainty remains independently visible. It is not failure by default, but concealed or materially unbounded uncertainty makes affected decision material ineligible.

## Confidence and Uncertainty Rules

- **P39-DC-059 — Confidence Basis:** Every confidence representation must trace to qualified Reasoning, eligible Evidence, assumptions, constraints, conflicts, uncertainty, and limitations.
- **P39-DC-060 — No False Precision:** Numeric or categorical presentation must not imply precision, probability, Truth, or certainty beyond its constitutional basis.
- **P39-DC-061 — Uncertainty Typing:** Material uncertainty must identify its source, Scope, affected alternatives or claims, and impact.
- **P39-DC-062 — Conflict Visibility:** Conflicting Evidence, Reasoning, constraints, and interpretations must remain independently visible.
- **P39-DC-063 — Confidence Revisability:** Material changes in inputs, Context, provenance, constraints, or uncertainty require confidence reassessment without rewriting history.
- **P39-DC-064 — Confidence Non-compulsion:** Confidence must not compel recommendation acceptance, Human Judgment, Governance outcome, Decision, Approval, Runtime admission, or Execution.
