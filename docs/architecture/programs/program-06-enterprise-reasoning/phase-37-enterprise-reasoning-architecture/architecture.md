# 1. Executive Summary

Enterprise Reasoning is the constitutionally bounded Enterprise capability that evaluates eligible Knowledge, Context, Evidence, Governance constraints, and declared objectives to produce attributable reasoning outputs without creating Decision Authority, Human Judgment, Director Decision, Approval, Recommendation, Runtime behavior, or Execution.

Phase 37 establishes Enterprise-wide reasoning responsibilities beyond architecture-specific reasoning while preserving the published Reasoning Engine, Query Intelligence, Governance Intelligence, Decision Support, Human Architecture, Director Authority, Program IV orchestration, Program V Security, and Runtime ownership.

Enterprise Reasoning is:

- evidence-grounded;
- purpose- and Scope-bound;
- explainable and traceable;
- uncertainty-preserving;
- constitutionally consistent;
- least-authority;
- fail-closed;
- technology, vendor, model, Runtime, infrastructure, product, and implementation independent.

Phase 37 does not define Enterprise Evidence identity assigned to Phase 38 or Decision Intelligence assigned to Phase 39. It produces bounded reasoning outputs that may become eligible inputs to those future architectures after their separate Director gates.

## Executive Rules

- **P37-DC-001 — Constitutional Enterprise Reasoning:** Enterprise Reasoning must remain subordinate to the Enterprise Constitution, Human supremacy, and Director Authority.
- **P37-DC-002 — Reasoning Non-authority:** No reasoning input, transformation, explanation, conclusion, confidence, ranking, or output may create Decision Authority, Approval, Permission, or Execution eligibility.
- **P37-DC-003 — Evidence Before Conclusion:** Every material conclusion must trace to eligible evidence, declared assumptions, applicable constraints, and visible uncertainty.
- **P37-DC-004 — Non-implementation:** Phase 37 must not define models, prompts, methods, retrieval, tools, Runtime behavior, infrastructure, products, or implementation.

# 2. Architectural Purpose

Enterprise Reasoning exists to provide a stable constitutional layer through which the Enterprise can evaluate eligible meaning without allowing computation, inference, confidence, or technical capability to become authority.

Its purpose is to:

- establish Enterprise Reasoning as a constitutional capability rather than a model or service;
- preserve exact purpose, objective, Scope, authority, responsibility, and accountable context;
- consume eligible Knowledge, Context, Evidence, and Governance constraints read-only;
- preserve evidence provenance, attribution, versions, classifications, uncertainty, contradiction, and dissent;
- produce bounded, explainable, reviewable reasoning outputs;
- preserve Human Judgment and Director Decision boundaries;
- support future Decision Intelligence without defining it;
- prevent reasoning from becoming Workflow, Planning, Orchestration, Runtime, or Execution.

Enterprise Reasoning answers: **what constitutionally supportable reasoning can be expressed from the declared inputs, constraints, and evidence within one bounded reasoning context?**

It does not answer what the Director must decide, what a Human must judge, what Governance must approve, or what Runtime must execute.

## Purpose Rules

- **P37-DC-005 — Declared Purpose:** Every Enterprise Reasoning context must declare an eligible purpose before reasoning begins.
- **P37-DC-006 — Objective Preservation:** Reasoning must preserve the exact authorized objective and must not silently broaden, replace, or optimize it.
- **P37-DC-007 — Human Judgment Preservation:** Enterprise Reasoning must support but never substitute for eligible Human Judgment.
- **P37-DC-008 — Director Decision Preservation:** Enterprise Reasoning must not make, infer, pre-approve, or constrain a Director Decision.
- **P37-DC-009 — Future-phase Separation:** Phase 37 must not define Enterprise Evidence Architecture or Decision Intelligence.

# 4. Architectural Principles

## Human Constitutional Supremacy

Human agency, judgment, responsibility, disagreement, review, Approval, and Override remain constitutionally available wherever required. Reasoning quality or confidence cannot replace them.

## Director Authority

The Director retains final constitutional Authority for committing Decisions, reserved interpretation, amendments, and roadmap governance.

## Evidence Before Conclusion

Material conclusions require eligible evidence and explicit relationships among evidence, premises, assumptions, constraints, uncertainty, and conclusions.

## Explainability

Every material reasoning output must preserve a constitutionally sufficient explanation of its basis, transformations, alternatives, conflicts, limitations, and uncertainty.

## Provenance and Traceability

Inputs, evidence use, assumptions, transformations, exclusions, conflicts, outputs, and review relationships must remain attributable and traceable.

## Uncertainty Preservation

Uncertainty, insufficiency, ambiguity, contradiction, and dissent remain explicit. They must not be erased by confidence, aggregation, ranking, or presentation.

## Constitutional Consistency

Reasoning must remain compatible with applicable constitutional identity, Authority, Policy, Governance, Security, privacy, Tenant, classification, and ownership boundaries.

## Least Authority

Reasoning receives no Authority beyond its declared constitutional responsibility. Capability, access, intelligence, confidence, consensus, urgency, or usefulness cannot create Authority.

## Fail-Closed Reasoning

Missing or incompatible Identity, Authority, Scope, provenance, Policy, classification, Permission, evidence eligibility, or required review prevents a valid reasoning output.

## Independence

Enterprise Reasoning remains Runtime, technology, vendor, model, infrastructure, product, and implementation independent.

## Principle Rules

- **P37-DC-014 — Human Supremacy:** Reasoning must not erase or automate Human Judgment, dissent, Approval, responsibility, or Override.
- **P37-DC-015 — Director Reservation:** Reserved Decisions, constitutional interpretation, exceptions, and amendments remain under Director Authority.
- **P37-DC-016 — Evidence Grounding:** Material reasoning claims must identify eligible supporting and conflicting evidence.
- **P37-DC-017 — Explainability Required:** Material conclusions without sufficient explanation are constitutionally ineligible.
- **P37-DC-018 — Provenance Required:** Every admitted input and evidence relationship must preserve source, attribution, version, Scope, and lifecycle.
- **P37-DC-019 — Uncertainty Visibility:** Uncertainty and contradiction must remain independently visible and must not be collapsed into certainty.
- **P37-DC-020 — Consistency Required:** Constitutional conflict must produce restriction, deferral, or escalation rather than silent reconciliation.
- **P37-DC-021 — Least Reasoning Authority:** Reasoning capability and access must not expand Permission, Authority, or Scope.
- **P37-DC-022 — Independence Required:** No model, method, vendor, Runtime, infrastructure, product, or implementation may define Enterprise Reasoning meaning.

# 6. Constitutional Reasoning Model

The constitutional Enterprise Reasoning model is:

```text
Declared Enterprise Reasoning Context
        +
Eligible Knowledge and Context
        +
Eligible Evidence References
        +
Applicable Governance and Security Constraints
        +
Declared Objective, Assumptions, and Uncertainty
        ↓
Evidence-grounded Enterprise Reasoning
        ↓
Integrity, Consistency, Explanation, and Boundary Validation
        ↓
Qualified Enterprise Reasoning Output
        ↓
Eligible Human, Governance, Decision Support, or future Decision Intelligence consumption
```

This model is an architectural relationship, not a Workflow, algorithm, prompt, model trace, Runtime sequence, Agent collaboration, Decision process, or implementation.

No downstream consumer is compelled to accept a reasoning output. Consumption remains separately governed by that consumer’s eligibility, Authority, purpose, Scope, and lifecycle.

## Model Rules

- **P37-DC-032 — Declared Context Required:** Enterprise Reasoning requires one complete declared context before input qualification.
- **P37-DC-033 — Eligible Inputs Required:** Only constitutionally eligible inputs may participate in reasoning.
- **P37-DC-034 — Constraint Applicability:** Applicable Governance, Security, privacy, classification, Tenant, and Authority constraints must remain active.
- **P37-DC-035 — Transformation Trace:** Every material transformation from premise to conclusion must remain traceable.
- **P37-DC-036 — Qualified Output Only:** An output is valid only after integrity, consistency, explanation, uncertainty, and boundary validation.
- **P37-DC-037 — Consumer Independence:** Reasoning output must not compel Human, Governance, Decision Support, Decision Intelligence, Runtime, or any other consumer.

# 7. Enterprise Reasoning Lifecycle

The constitutional lifecycle is:

```text
Context Declared
        ↓
Inputs Qualified
        ↓
Constraints Bound
        ↓
Evidence Consumed
        ↓
Reasoning Evaluated
        ↓
Integrity and Consistency Validated
        ↓
Explanation and Uncertainty Preserved
        ↓
Output Qualified
        ↓
Review Eligible
        ↓
Closed or Escalated
```

Lifecycle meanings are architectural:

- **Context Declared:** purpose, objective, Scope, Authority, ownership, accountability, and boundaries are explicit.
- **Inputs Qualified:** source identity, provenance, classification, eligibility, versions, and limitations are preserved.
- **Constraints Bound:** applicable constitutional, Governance, Security, and organizational limits are attached.
- **Evidence Consumed:** eligible evidence references are used read-only.
- **Reasoning Evaluated:** relationships, assumptions, alternatives, conflicts, and conclusions are evaluated.
- **Validated:** integrity, consistency, explanation, uncertainty, and boundary compliance are assessed.
- **Output Qualified:** the output is attributable, bounded, non-authoritative, and reviewable.
- **Closed or Escalated:** lifecycle evidence is preserved without triggering action.

## Lifecycle Rules

- **P37-DC-038 — No Automatic Progression:** No lifecycle meaning advances automatically through confidence, availability, completion, or technical state.
- **P37-DC-039 — Context Before Input:** Input qualification must not precede a complete reasoning context.
- **P37-DC-040 — Constraints Before Evaluation:** Applicable constitutional constraints must be bound before material evaluation.
- **P37-DC-041 — Evidence Before Conclusion:** Material conclusions must not precede eligible evidence consumption and declared assumptions.
- **P37-DC-042 — Validation Before Qualification:** No output may be qualified before required validation completes.
- **P37-DC-043 — Review Non-approval:** Review eligibility must not become Human Approval, Governance Approval, Decision, or Authority.
- **P37-DC-044 — Closure Non-execution:** Lifecycle closure or escalation must not trigger Workflow, Runtime, communication protocol, or Execution.

# 11. Reasoning Integrity and Consistency

Reasoning Integrity requires preserved correspondence among:

- purpose, objective, Scope, Authority, and accountable context;
- input identity, provenance, versions, eligibility, and limitations;
- evidence roles, premises, assumptions, transformations, conflicts, and conclusions;
- applicable Governance, Security, privacy, Tenant, classification, and organizational constraints;
- uncertainty, explanation, output qualification, review, and closure.

Reasoning Consistency evaluates compatibility without forcing agreement. It preserves:

- distinct meanings;
- temporal and Scope differences;
- alternative interpretations;
- unresolved contradictions;
- minority and dissenting positions;
- explicit incommensurability.

Consistency is not uniformity, consensus, truth, or correctness. Integrity is not confidence, completeness, popularity, or technical success.

## Integrity and Consistency Rules

- **P37-DC-065 — End-to-end Correspondence:** Every output claim must trace through transformations to eligible inputs and declared assumptions.
- **P37-DC-066 — Evidence Non-mutation:** Reasoning must not rewrite, fabricate, delete, reclassify, or alter evidence provenance.
- **P37-DC-067 — Assumption Separation:** Assumptions must remain distinct from Evidence, Knowledge, Context, and conclusions.
- **P37-DC-068 — Contradiction Preservation:** Unresolved contradictions must remain visible and independently traceable.
- **P37-DC-069 — Consistency Non-consensus:** Agreement, majority, ranking, or confidence must not substitute for constitutional consistency.
- **P37-DC-070 — Integrity Fail-closed:** Broken lineage, missing constraints, invalid inputs, or unexplained transformations make affected output ineligible.

# 12. Explainability, Uncertainty, and Evidence Consumption

## Reasoning Explanation Requirements

Reasoning Explanation must preserve:

- declared purpose, objective, and Scope;
- eligible inputs and evidence roles;
- premises, assumptions, and transformations;
- alternatives, exclusions, conflicts, and dissent;
- applicable constraints;
- uncertainty and limitations;
- output qualification and review boundary.

Explainability is not disclosure permission. Protected information remains protected.

## Uncertainty

Reasoning Uncertainty may characterize:

- missing or insufficient evidence;
- ambiguous meaning;
- conflicting evidence or constraints;
- temporal instability;
- uncertain authority or applicability;
- inference limitations;
- incomplete Scope coverage;
- alternative viable interpretations.

Uncertainty is not failure by default, but concealed or materially unbounded uncertainty invalidates affected output.

## Evidence Consumption Requirements

Evidence is consumed as immutable, eligible references. Reasoning may identify support, conflict, insufficiency, exclusion, or limitation but cannot change evidence identity, content, provenance, authority, classification, or lifecycle.

Phase 38 retains future ownership of Enterprise Evidence Architecture. Phase 37 defines only reasoning’s read-only relationship to already eligible evidence.

## Explainability, Uncertainty, and Evidence Rules

- **P37-DC-071 — Explanation Completeness:** Every material conclusion must expose sufficient constitutional explanation for eligible review.
- **P37-DC-072 — Protected Explanation:** Explanation must not bypass privacy, classification, Tenant, Permission, or disclosure boundaries.
- **P37-DC-073 — Uncertainty Typing:** Material uncertainty must identify its source, Scope, affected claims, and impact.
- **P37-DC-074 — No False Precision:** Scores, confidence, ranking, or aggregation must not conceal uncertainty or imply truth.
- **P37-DC-075 — Evidence Role Traceability:** Every evidence use must identify its exact role in the reasoning relationship.
- **P37-DC-076 — Evidence Conflict Visibility:** Supporting, conflicting, excluded, and insufficient evidence must remain independently visible.
- **P37-DC-077 — Phase 38 Preservation:** Phase 37 must not define Enterprise Evidence identity, admission, lifecycle, evaluation authority, storage, or cross-domain architecture.
