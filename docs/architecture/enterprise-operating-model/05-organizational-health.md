# 05 — Organizational Health

## Purpose

Organizational Health defines **what a healthy organization preserves** — the structural properties whose continuous integrity means the enterprise is operating well as a governed system. Health here is *organizational*, not operational: it is not about whether work succeeds, but whether the organization's structure, authority, ownership, accountability, and governance stay sound over time.

## Architectural role

Where the governance cycle ([04](04-governance-cycle.md)) runs on the organizational rhythm ([03](03-organizational-rhythm.md)), organizational health is *what each pass checks for*. It is the set of structural health properties the operating lifecycle ([02](02-operating-lifecycle.md)) sustains. Health is the standard of a well-operating organization the rest of the model maintains.

## The model — the health properties

A healthy enterprise continuously preserves:

### Structural integrity
The hierarchy, seats, and authority lines are coherent and stable — no orphan seats, no broken authority lines, no silent forking of the organization ([enterprise organizational model](../enterprise-organization/02-organizational-model.md), [operating principles](01-operating-principles.md)).

### Authority integrity
All authority is delegated, bounded, revocable, and traceable to the Director — no originated authority, no grant that escaped its bound, no cycle running above the Director ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)).

### Ownership integrity
Every domain and capability has exactly one owner; ownership is unbroken and moves only by governed transfer ([department responsibilities](../department-architecture/03-department-responsibilities.md), [specialist responsibilities](../specialist-architecture/03-specialist-responsibilities.md), [ownership transfer](../cross-organization-collaboration/03-ownership-transfer.md)).

### Accountability integrity
Every seat answers upward on an intact line; no accountability is orphaned, shed, or duplicated ([manager principles](../manager-architecture/01-manager-principles.md), [specialist principles](../specialist-architecture/01-specialist-principles.md)).

### Governance integrity
The one governance regime is in force everywhere, continuously, with no exemption and no lapse ([governance cycle](04-governance-cycle.md)).

### Parity integrity
Human and AI occupants operate under equal rules, cadence, and governance — no privileged or exempt occupant type ([9F](../human-organization/README.md)).

### Traceability integrity
The organization's structure, authority, ownership, accountability, and operation can be reconstructed and audited at any point ([operating principles](01-operating-principles.md)).

## Health is preserved, not performed

Organizational health is a set of *properties to preserve*, checked on the rhythm by the governance cycle — not work to perform or a metric to compute at runtime ([operating boundaries](06-operating-boundaries.md)). This document defines *what health means structurally*; how it is measured or instrumented is runtime, out of scope.

## Inputs

- The **operating lifecycle and governance cycle** ([02](02-operating-lifecycle.md), [04](04-governance-cycle.md)) — what sustains and checks health.
- The **whole organizational structure** ([9A](../enterprise-organization/README.md)–[9F](../human-organization/README.md)) — the properties health preserves.

## Outputs

- A **defined set of organizational health properties** — structural, authority, ownership, accountability, governance, parity, and traceability integrity — that the governance cycle preserves on rhythm.

## Boundaries

- Defines **health of the organization, not of any work** — it is not about execution success.
- Defines **no metric, measurement, or instrumentation** — those are runtime, out of scope.
- Performs **no reasoning or work** — it is a standard, not an actor.

## Future direction

As the enterprise grows, these health properties hold unchanged — more units and occupants to keep sound, same integrities. Concrete health measurement may be built later behind the Director gate ([future evolution](07-future-evolution.md)), but organizational health stays the structural standard of a well-operating enterprise. The enterprise scales; a healthy organization stays sound.
