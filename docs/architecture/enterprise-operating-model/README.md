# Enterprise Operating Model — Architecture (Phase 9G)

## Purpose

**Enterprise Operating Model** defines *how the enterprise continuously operates as one governed organizational system*. Phases 9A–9F built the structure: the enterprise ([9A](../enterprise-organization/README.md)), departments ([9B](../department-architecture/README.md)), managers ([9C](../manager-architecture/README.md)), specialists ([9D](../specialist-architecture/README.md)), cross-organization collaboration ([9E](../cross-organization-collaboration/README.md)), and human participation ([9F](../human-organization/README.md)). Those defined *what the organization is*. Phase 9G defines *how it keeps being that* — its operating principles, lifecycle, rhythm, governance cycle, health, and structural continuity over time.

This is the **organizational operating model** — organizational operation, governance rhythm, and structural continuity. It is **not** runtime execution, workflows, business procedures, implementation, or software architecture. It describes how the *organization* stays coherent and governed as it runs, not how any *work* runs.

It is **architecture only** — no implementation, no runtime, no prompts, no algorithms, no concrete departments/managers/specialists, no workflows, no business procedures.

## Relationship with Enterprise Organization (Phase 9A)

Enterprise Organization defined the structure at rest — the hierarchy, authority, and governance ([9A](../enterprise-organization/README.md)). The Operating Model defines the structure *in continuous operation* — how that hierarchy, authority, and governance persist, stay coherent, and remain accountable as the enterprise operates over time. It adds no new structure; it defines the continuity of the existing one.

## Relationship with Director Intelligence (Phase 7)

Director Intelligence is *how the enterprise thinks* ([Phase 7](../director-reasoning/README.md)). The Operating Model does not think — it defines the organizational rhythm and governance cycle *within which* thinking recurs, and it keeps the Director at the apex of every operating cycle. Reasoning stays entirely in Phase 7; the Operating Model frames how the organization operates around it.

## Relationship with Execution (Phase 8)

Execution is *how the enterprise acts* ([Phase 8](../director-execution/README.md)). The Operating Model does not act, perform, or orchestrate work — it defines how the *organization* continuously operates as a governed system while executions come and go. Execution is transient work; the Operating Model is the standing organizational operation that outlasts any execution.

## Relationship with Human Organization (Phase 9F)

Human participation is first-class and occupant-agnostic ([9F](../human-organization/README.md)). The Operating Model preserves human–AI parity in operation: the rhythm, governance cycle, and health principles apply to every occupant equally, human or AI. The enterprise operates as one system regardless of who fills its seats.

## Why the Operating Model is separate from execution

```
Enterprise Operating Model (Phase 9G)   ← this phase
   organizational operation · governance rhythm · structural continuity
                        │  frames (does not perform)
                        ▼
Execution (Phase 8)          Director Intelligence (Phase 7)
   performs approved work        reasons, plans, decides
```

- **Operating the organization vs performing work.** The Operating Model keeps the *organization* coherent and governed over time — continuity of authority, ownership, accountability, governance. Execution performs *work*. A model that also executed would confuse "is the organization healthy and governed?" with "did the work run?".
- **Standing vs transient.** The Operating Model is the enterprise's standing operation; executions are transient. Separating them lets the organization operate continuously across countless executions without being reshaped by any of them.
- **Rhythm, not scheduling.** The Operating Model defines *organizational rhythm* — the governance cadence of a living organization — not task scheduling ([execution orchestration](../execution-orchestration/README.md)). One is organizational; the other is operational.
- **Director Authority stays intact.** Every operating cycle keeps the Director at the apex ([authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). Because the Operating Model operates the organization rather than executing work, it can hold the whole system to a governed, traceable standard without ever escaping the Director.

## What the Enterprise Operating Model must always do

- **Define how the enterprise continuously operates** as one governed system.
- **Define organizational, governance, accountability, and ownership continuity.**
- **Define organizational health principles**, **organizational rhythm**, and **structural stability**.
- **Preserve Director Authority, enterprise governance, Department ownership, Manager accountability, Specialist responsibilities, human–AI parity, and traceability.**
- **Support future enterprise growth.**
- **Never** perform reasoning, execute work, orchestrate execution, redefine governance, or bypass organizational authority.

## Status
