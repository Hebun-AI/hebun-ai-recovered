# 04 — Governance Cycle

## Purpose

The Governance Cycle defines **how governance recurs and stays continuous** as the enterprise operates. Governance is not a one-time act performed at setup; it is an ongoing cycle that runs on the organization's rhythm, holding every unit and occupant to the Director's standards at every point in operation. This document defines that continuity.

## Architectural role

Where the enterprise governance regime ([9A](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)) defined governance at rest, the governance cycle defines governance *in continuous operation*. It runs on the organizational rhythm ([03](03-organizational-rhythm.md)), within the operating lifecycle ([02](02-operating-lifecycle.md)), and is what keeps organizational health ([05](05-organizational-health.md)) maintained over time. It redefines no governance; it keeps the existing regime running.

## The model

### Governance recurs; it never lapses
Governance runs as an ongoing cycle on the organizational rhythm ([organizational rhythm](03-organizational-rhythm.md)). The enterprise is governed at every point in its operation — there is no ungoverned interval. Governance continuity means the regime is always in force, not periodically remembered.

### The cycle is the existing regime, running continuously
The governance cycle applies the one enterprise-wide regime ([enterprise governance](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)) — the same standards that govern departments ([9B](../department-architecture/06-department-governance.md)), managers ([9C](../manager-architecture/06-escalation-governance.md)), specialists ([9D](../specialist-architecture/06-reporting-and-governance.md)), collaboration ([9E](../cross-organization-collaboration/06-collaboration-governance.md)), and human occupants ([9F](../human-organization/06-human-governance.md)). The cycle adds no new regime; it keeps the existing one continuous.

### The cycle preserves the continuities
Each pass of the governance cycle upholds the operating continuities ([operating lifecycle](02-operating-lifecycle.md)): structure stable, ownership unbroken, accountability intact, authority Director-topped. The cycle is how continuity is actively verified, not merely assumed.

### The cycle aligns with Director Authority
Governance expresses the Director's standards and never sits above the Director ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). Where operation would exceed governance or an occupant's authority, the cycle blocks it and forces escalation ([escalation model](../cross-organization-collaboration/05-escalation-model.md)). Committing and beyond-authority matters rise to the Director.

### The cycle applies to every occupant equally
The governance cycle reaches human and AI occupants alike, on the same rhythm, with no exemption ([9F](../human-organization/06-human-governance.md)). One cycle, one regime, one organization.

### The cycle is fully traceable
Each governance pass — what was checked, what held, what escalated — can be reconstructed and audited. The continuity of governance is provable over time, not merely asserted ([operating principles](01-operating-principles.md)).

## Inputs

- The **enterprise governance regime** ([9A](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)) — the standards the cycle runs.
- The **organizational rhythm** ([03](03-organizational-rhythm.md)) — the cadence the cycle recurs on.

## Outputs

- A **continuous governance cycle** — the existing regime, running on rhythm, preserving the continuities, Director-topped, traceable — that keeps the enterprise governed at every point in operation.

## Boundaries

- Governs **the operating organization, not concrete units or occupants** — it names none.
- Redefines **no governance** — it runs the existing regime continuously.
- Performs **no reasoning, work, or orchestration** — it checks, records, and escalates.
- Describes **no runtime, schedule, or mechanism** — only the structure of continuous governance.

## Future direction

As the enterprise grows, the governance cycle reaches more units and occupants on the same rhythm, under the same regime, no exemptions. Its checks may deepen over time ([future evolution](07-future-evolution.md)), but governance stays continuous, enterprise-anchored, Director-topped, and traceable. The enterprise scales; governance never lapses.
