# 01 — Operating Principles

## Purpose

The Operating Principles are the constitution of the Enterprise Operating Model — the commitments the enterprise's continuous operation must obey. The Operating Model keeps the organization coherent and governed over time; these principles are what keep that operation from drifting into execution, forking governance, or eroding the structure. Any operation that violates one of these is not doing the Enterprise Operating Model.

## Architectural role

These principles constrain all the operating topics that follow (lifecycle, rhythm, governance cycle, health, boundaries). Every subsequent document inherits them first. They keep operation organizational, continuous, governed, structure-preserving, and traceable.

## The principles

### 1. The operating model operates the organization; it does not perform work
It keeps authority, ownership, accountability, and governance coherent over time. It performs no reasoning ([Director Intelligence](../director-reasoning/README.md)), no work ([Execution](../director-execution/README.md)), and no orchestration of running work ([execution orchestration](../execution-orchestration/README.md)). Operating the organization and performing work are different concerns; the model holds only the first.

### 2. Continuity is the point
The enterprise operates *continuously*: its structure, authority, ownership, accountability, and governance persist across time and across countless executions ([operating lifecycle](02-operating-lifecycle.md)). The Operating Model exists to keep the organization the same governed system tomorrow that it is today.

### 3. The Director stays the apex of every cycle
Every operating cycle, rhythm beat, and governance pass keeps the Director at the top ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). Operation never drifts authority away from the Director or lets a cycle run above the Director.

### 4. Governance is continuous, not occasional
Governance recurs as an ongoing cycle, not a one-time act ([governance cycle](04-governance-cycle.md)). The enterprise is governed at every point in its operation, uniformly across every unit and occupant ([enterprise governance](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)). The Operating Model redefines no governance; it keeps the existing regime running.

### 5. Ownership and accountability are preserved through operation
As the enterprise operates, departments keep domain ownership ([9B](../department-architecture/03-department-responsibilities.md)), managers keep accountability ([9C](../manager-architecture/01-manager-principles.md)), specialists keep responsibility ([9D](../specialist-architecture/03-specialist-responsibilities.md)). Operation never quietly erodes ownership or blurs accountability; continuity means these hold over time.

### 6. Human and AI parity holds in operation
The operating rhythm, governance cycle, and health principles apply to every occupant equally ([9F](../human-organization/README.md)). Operation grants no occupant type an exemption or a privileged cadence. One system, one operation, for humans and AI alike.

### 7. Structural stability is preserved
The organization's structure stays stable as it operates ([organizational health](05-organizational-health.md)). Operation does not silently reshape the hierarchy, reassign authority, or fork the organization. Structural change is a Director-gated act, not a side effect of operating.

### 8. Operation is traceable
The enterprise's operation — its cycles, governance passes, and health over time — can be reconstructed and audited ([governance cycle](04-governance-cycle.md)). Continuity is provable, not merely asserted.

### 9. Operation flows through organizational authority
The Operating Model never bypasses the hierarchy's authority ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). It operates *through* the structure — its rhythm and governance run along the existing authority lines, never around them.

## Inputs

- The **whole organizational structure** ([9A](../enterprise-organization/README.md)–[9F](../human-organization/README.md)) — the system the model keeps operating.

## Outputs

- A **principled frame** every operating topic operates within — the standard the enterprise's continuous operation is held to.

## Boundaries

- These principles **define no runtime, workflow, or business procedure** — they state what operation must obey, not how work runs.
- They **describe no mechanism** — operating machinery is a later phase behind the Director gate.

## Future direction

As the enterprise grows, its operation still obeys these principles: operate not perform, continuity, Director-apex, continuous governance, ownership and accountability preserved, human–AI parity, structural stability, traceable, through authority. The enterprise scales; the constitution holds.
