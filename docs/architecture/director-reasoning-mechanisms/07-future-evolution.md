# 07 — Future Evolution

How the Director Reasoning Mechanisms are expected to evolve — **at the architecture level only**. No algorithms, no prompts, no implementation, no runtime. The mechanisms defined here are the stable *vocabulary of how reasoning works*; evolution deepens each mechanism, never removes the discipline it carries.

Each direction below is future work, behind its own Director gate, following the [Capability Lifecycle](../../architecture-backlog/00-capability-lifecycle.md).

## Deeper mechanisms, same set

Future reasoning engines will perform each mechanism more capably — richer strategies, sharper decomposition, more creative hypotheses, more discerning evidence evaluation, finer confidence calibration, wiser termination. But they will still use the **same set of mechanisms** in service of the **same lifecycle** ([Phase 7B](../director-reasoning-cognition/README.md)) and the **same principles** ([Phase 7A](../director-reasoning/README.md)). An engine that dropped evidence evaluation, or treated hypotheses as conclusions, would not be more advanced — it would be broken. The mechanism set and its disciplines are the invariant every future engine inherits.

## Toward the realization phase

The natural next phase realizes these mechanisms — defines *how they are actually built*. This phase deliberately stops at the mechanism level; realization is where machinery is specified, still contracts-first and behind the Director gate. The seam is clean: mechanisms describe *what cognitive tools reasoning needs and what disciplines they carry*; realization describes *how those tools are constructed*. Realization must preserve every mechanism discipline defined here.

## Integration with richer memory and reasoning

As Organizational Memory and the semantic layer deepen ([Phase 6](../memory/README.md)), the mechanisms that draw on them grow stronger — better precedent for strategy selection, richer evidence for evaluation, more history for hypothesis generation. The mechanisms consume deeper knowledge; their roles and boundaries do not change to accommodate it.

## The invariant across all evolution

Through every future version of the mechanisms:

- The **mechanism set holds** — strategies, decomposition, hypothesis generation, evidence evaluation, uncertainty/confidence, termination.
- Each mechanism **keeps its discipline** — evidence before conclusion, hypotheses stay tentative, wholes are preserved, uncertainty stays honest, nothing is fabricated.
- The mechanisms serve the **lifecycle and the principles**; they never override them.
- Everything remains **advisory** — mechanisms realize thinking, never action. The Director always decides.

The Director Reasoning Mechanisms can become far more capable. They cannot abandon their disciplines, act on their own conclusions, or decide for the Director. That fixed foundation is what every future reasoning engine is built around.
