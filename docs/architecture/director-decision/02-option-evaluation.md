# 02 — Option Evaluation

## Purpose

Option Evaluation is where decision **assesses the validated plan alternatives objectively** — judging each candidate plan on its merits so the choice among them is grounded, not arbitrary. Where planning produced sound plans, evaluation determines *how good each is* for the situation: its value, its cost, its fit to the goal, its evidence base. It is the foundation the rest of the decision rests on.

## Architectural role

Option Evaluation is the first decision topic, feeding [Prioritization](03-prioritization.md), [Risk Balancing](04-risk-balancing.md), and [Governance Alignment](05-governance-alignment.md). It reuses reasoning's evidence-evaluation discipline ([Phase 7C](../director-reasoning-mechanisms/04-evidence-evaluation.md)), applied now to whole validated plans rather than raw hypotheses. It also carries reasoning's trade-off analysis ([Phase 7B](../director-reasoning-cognition/06-tradeoff-analysis.md)) forward to the plan level. Its job is a fair, comparable assessment of each alternative.

## Inputs

- The **validated plan alternatives** from planning — including, where relevant, the do-nothing alternative.
- The **approved goal and context** — what the plans are measured against.
- **Evidence from the knowledge layers** — model, graph, memory — grounding the assessment.

## Outputs

- **Assessed alternatives** — each plan evaluated for value, cost, fit-to-goal, and evidence strength, in comparable terms.
- Explicit **trade-offs** per alternative — what each gains and gives up, none hidden.
- **Confidence and gaps** in the assessment, stated honestly ([uncertainty & confidence](../director-reasoning-mechanisms/05-uncertainty-confidence.md)).

## Boundaries

- Evaluation is **objective** — no alternative is pre-favored; each gets an honest assessment ([decision principles](01-decision-principles.md)).
- It **assesses; it does not choose** — ranking and selection come in prioritization and, finally, the Director's decision. Evaluation supplies the fair comparison the choice rests on.
- It **produces no action** and **defines no method** — this document establishes that option evaluation exists and its role, not any scoring algorithm.

## Future direction

Future decision engines may evaluate alternatives more discerningly — weighing value, cost, and fit more precisely, detecting subtler trade-offs. The discipline is fixed: objective, evidence-grounded, honest about trade-offs and uncertainty. Discernment grows; the objectivity holds.
