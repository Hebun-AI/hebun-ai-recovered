# 04 — Risk Balancing

## Purpose

Risk Balancing is where decision **weighs opportunity against risk** for the candidate plans — ensuring the outcome reflects not just what could be gained but what could go wrong, and how those two sides net out. A plan with high opportunity and unacceptable risk is not the right choice; risk balancing is what keeps a decision from being seduced by upside alone.

## Architectural role

Risk Balancing works alongside [Prioritization](03-prioritization.md) — it can reorder a ranking once risk is properly weighed — and feeds [Decision Validation](06-decision-validation.md). It carries reasoning's risk-evaluation discipline ([decision principles](../director-reasoning/04-decision-principles.md)) to the plan level and honors the **long-term over short-term** principle ([first principles](../director-reasoning/02-first-principles.md)): a short-term gain carrying long-term risk is weighted accordingly. Its job is a fair, explicit netting of opportunity and risk.

## Inputs

- The **assessed and ranked alternatives** — each with its opportunity and its risk profile.
- The **organizational risk posture** — from governance, memory of past outcomes, and the Director's known tolerances.
- The **impact context** — how a plan's risks ripple through the organization ([impact analysis](../relationship-graph/05-impact-analysis.md)).

## Outputs

- **Risk-balanced alternatives** — each plan's opportunity and risk made explicit and netted, comparably.
- **Recoverability read** — for each plan, how reversible or containable its risks are, since irreversible risk weighs heavier.
- Explicit flags where **risk is high enough to be decision-critical**, surfaced for the Director.

## Boundaries

- Risk is **weighed honestly** — neither ignored to make a plan attractive nor inflated to avoid a decision ([decision principles](01-decision-principles.md)).
- Risk Balancing **weighs; it does not commit or mitigate** — it informs the outcome; acting on or mitigating risk is execution, under the Director's authority.
- It **produces no action** and **defines no method** — this document establishes that risk balancing exists and its role, not any risk model.

## Future direction

Future decision engines may balance risk more precisely — better estimating likelihood and impact, tracing recoverability, learning the organization's true risk posture. The discipline is fixed: opportunity and risk both explicit, honestly netted, irreversible risk weighted heavily. Precision grows; the honest balance holds.
