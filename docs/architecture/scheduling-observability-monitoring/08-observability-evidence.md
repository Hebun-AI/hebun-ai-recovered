# 08 — Observability Evidence

## Purpose

Define eligible evidence, operational explanation, correlation, and health visibility.

## Evidence Sources

Eligible evidence may reference:

- Phase 21 admission, responsibility, limitation, failure, and outcome artifacts;
- Phase 22 Workflow, State, Event, traceability, and visibility artifacts;
- Scheduling determinations and their evidence;
- Monitoring evaluations and Alerts;
- Operational Metrics and declared measurement context.

Eligibility for Observability does not make evidence canonical truth or Enterprise Memory.

## Evidence Qualification

Each evidence item preserves identity, provenance, source authority, effective and observed time, version, correlation, ordering semantics, Tenant, classification, Scope, quality, uncertainty, and known limitations.

## Operational Health Visibility

Operational health visibility is a bounded evidence-based view of declared health dimensions and conditions. It is not universal health, business success, Governance compliance, approval, control, or remediation.

## Explanation

An explanation identifies evidence, observed relationships, supported interpretations, alternatives, contradictions, missing evidence, uncertainty, affected Scope, and limitations. It cannot rewrite evidence or claim unsupported causality.

## Rules

- **P23-EVIDENCE-001:** Every explanation must cite exact evidence identities and versions.
- **P23-EVIDENCE-002:** Evidence must remain distinct from interpretation and decision.
- **P23-EVIDENCE-003:** Operational health must be qualified by declared dimensions and time.
- **P23-EVIDENCE-004:** Aggregation must preserve source boundaries and uncertainty.
- **P23-EVIDENCE-005:** Evidence must not become Enterprise Memory without separate admission.
- **P23-EVIDENCE-006:** Explanation must fail closed when evidence is insufficient for the claim.

## Enterprise Example

An operational health view may show that latency evidence exceeds a declared condition. It does not establish customer impact or authorize intervention without further evidence and authority.
