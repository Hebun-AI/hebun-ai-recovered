# 06 — Observability Constitution

## Purpose

Define Observability as evidence-grounded explanation of operational behavior.

## Constitutional Identity

Observability is the bounded ability to explain operational behavior, condition, progression, divergence, limitation, failure, and outcome from attributable Runtime, Workflow, State, Event, Metric, and Monitoring evidence.

Observability is not Visibility, monitoring, Governance, reasoning authority, decision, control, remediation, behavior modification, telemetry implementation, dashboard, or technology platform.

## Constitutional Duties

Observability must preserve evidence identity, provenance, version, effective time, correlation, ordering limits, uncertainty, Tenant, classification, Scope, and source authority. Explanations must distinguish evidence from inference and expose alternative explanations or missing evidence.

## Observability Reasoning Boundary

Observability may construct bounded operational explanations from evidence. This explanatory interpretation is not the Phase 14 Reasoning Engine, business reasoning, decision support, Governance evaluation, causal certainty, or authority.

## Observability Boundaries

Observability explains behavior but never changes behavior. It cannot execute, schedule, alter Workflow or State, create Events, enforce policy, approve, decide, or authorize remediation.

## Rules

- **P23-OBSERVABILITY-001:** Every explanation must trace to attributable operational evidence.
- **P23-OBSERVABILITY-002:** Explanation must preserve uncertainty, contradiction, and alternatives.
- **P23-OBSERVABILITY-003:** Observability must not modify observed behavior or evidence.
- **P23-OBSERVABILITY-004:** Correlation and precedence must not be presented as causality without evidence.
- **P23-OBSERVABILITY-005:** Observability must preserve Tenant, classification, and Scope boundaries.
- **P23-OBSERVABILITY-006:** Observability meaning must remain implementation-independent.

## Enterprise Example

Observability may explain that an expired eligibility basis coincided with Workflow suspension. It cannot claim causality without evidence or resume the Workflow.
