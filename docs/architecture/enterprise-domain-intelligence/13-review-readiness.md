## Boundary Validation

Phase 18 defines intelligence-only constitutional domains. It contains no Runtime behavior, execution, tool invocation, external-system access, Agent communication, shared memory, planning, workflow, task orchestration, Computer Use, automation, code, API, schema, prompt, model, or infrastructure.

## Coverage Validation

Each of the nine domains defines Purpose, Organizational Mission, Intelligence Scope, Strategic Responsibilities, Information Ownership, Information Boundaries, Capability Boundaries, Authority Limitations, Director Relationship, Governance Relationship, Reasoning Participation, Cross-Domain Dependencies, Inputs, Outputs, Escalation Responsibilities, Constitutional Invariants, and Explicit Non-Responsibilities.

## Residual Risks

- domain intelligence may be mistaken for a department or Agent;
- information responsibility may be mistaken for canonical ownership;
- cross-domain dependency may be mistaken for communication or data access;
- domain synthesis may be mistaken for reasoning or Decision Support;
- future Agents may combine domain participation with execution;
- scale may pressure Tenant, classification, or provenance boundaries.

## Rules

- **P18-REVIEW-001:** Every canonical domain and required responsibility must be traceable.
- **P18-REVIEW-002:** Missing sections, broken links, duplicate numbering, or duplicate Rule Identity blocks readiness.
- **P18-REVIEW-003:** Authority, Runtime, execution, or future-phase leakage blocks readiness.
- **P18-REVIEW-004:** Phase 17 inheritance and Tenant isolation are mandatory.
- **P18-REVIEW-005:** Director review is required before closure.
- **P18-REVIEW-006:** Phase 19 requires a separate Director gate.
