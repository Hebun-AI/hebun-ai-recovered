# 02 — Domain Intelligence Constitution

## Purpose

This constitution is the shared contract inherited by every Phase 18 domain and every future realization of domain intelligence.

## Constitutional Identity

A Domain Intelligence component is a bounded organizational intelligence responsibility. It is not a department, organizational owner, Agent identity, Capability, canonical source, Reasoning Engine, Governance authority, Decision Support authority, Runtime service, or execution unit.

## Mandatory Responsibilities

Every domain must:

- declare purpose, mission, Scope, boundaries, dependencies, inputs, outputs, and escalation;
- preserve source authority, provenance, classification, lifecycle, Tenant, and organizational context;
- distinguish source information, derived information, inference, uncertainty, conflict, and intelligence contribution;
- remain non-authoritative and independently analyzable;
- preserve immutable Processing, Reasoning, Governance, and Decision Support artifacts;
- expose cross-domain overlap without resolving it through hidden consensus;
- stop when evidence, Scope, governance, or authority is insufficient.

## Mandatory Prohibitions

No domain may execute business operations, perform Runtime actions, invoke tools or external systems, approve organizational decisions, replace the Director, override Governance, bypass Decision Support, own organizational authority, alter canonical truth, create Agents, communicate tasks, maintain shared memory, plan work, orchestrate activity, or automate outcomes.

## Information Responsibility

A domain is responsible for the fitness, classification, provenance, relevance, and boundary integrity of information it contributes. Canonical ownership remains with the authoritative source and accountable organizational seat. Information responsibility does not transfer source authority.

## Rules

- **P18-CONSTITUTION-001:** Phase 17 constitutional inheritance is mandatory.
- **P18-CONSTITUTION-002:** Intelligence must remain distinct from reasoning, governance, decision support, Runtime, and execution.
- **P18-CONSTITUTION-003:** Domain confidence, scoring, consensus, or expertise must not create authority.
- **P18-CONSTITUTION-004:** Cross-domain evidence must retain original domain, source, Tenant, and provenance.
- **P18-CONSTITUTION-005:** A domain must escalate rather than infer permission or resolve an authority conflict.
- **P18-CONSTITUTION-006:** Future scale must not weaken isolation, explainability, or Director control.
- **P18-CONSTITUTION-007:** Domain outputs must be declarative, attributable, non-executable intelligence.

## Human and Director Boundary

Material conflict, insufficient authority, governance exception, canonical change, organizational decision, approval, or execution authorization requires an explicit human or Director boundary. No lack of response constitutes consent.
