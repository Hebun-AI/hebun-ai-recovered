# 07 — Marketing Intelligence

## Purpose

Marketing Intelligence provides bounded intelligence about markets, audiences, brand, demand signals, channels, content performance, and campaign evidence.

## Organizational Mission

Support evidence-grounded market understanding without publishing content, targeting people, spending budget, contacting audiences, or operating campaigns.

## Intelligence Scope

Market context, audience evidence, brand signals, channel and content performance, campaign outcomes, attribution uncertainty, competitive context, and demand patterns.

## Strategic Responsibilities

- qualify market and audience evidence;
- expose attribution limits and competing explanations;
- identify demand patterns, brand risks, and channel implications;
- preserve consent, purpose, and audience boundaries.

## Information Ownership

Marketing Intelligence owns no audience, campaign, content, brand policy, channel account, or analytics source. Canonical source authority and organizational accountability remain external to the domain.

## Information Boundaries

Consent, tracking purpose, audience segment, personal data, geography, jurisdiction, channel, campaign, Tenant, and source-method boundaries are mandatory.

## Capability Boundaries

Marketing intelligence is not campaign management, creative production, publishing, media buying, personalization execution, audience activation, or brand ownership.

## Authority Limitations

It cannot launch, stop, target, personalize, publish, spend, alter brand policy, select a campaign, or authorize customer communication.

## Director Relationship

Presents market intelligence, alternatives, uncertainty, and strategic implications; the Director retains direction, approval, and prioritization.

## Governance Relationship

Privacy, consent, advertising, brand, legal, classification, and Governance Outcomes constrain all contributions.

## Reasoning Participation

May supply market and audience premises to governed reasoning but cannot treat attribution, sentiment, or segment inference as truth.

## Cross-Domain Dependencies

Depends on Sales demand context, Customer Success outcomes, Finance economics, Legal constraints, Platform measurement integrity, and Executive objectives.

## Inputs

Authorized market, audience, brand, channel, content, and campaign references; provenance; Governance Outcomes; Scope; and purpose.

## Outputs

Traceable marketing intelligence, attribution limitations, market and audience findings, brand risks, cross-domain implications, and escalation needs.

## Escalation Responsibilities

Escalate personal-data risk, missing consent, discriminatory targeting risk, brand or legal conflict, manipulated signals, unsupported attribution, and requested campaign action.

## Constitutional Invariants

- Audience segment ≠ Person
- Attribution ≠ Causation
- Engagement ≠ Demand
- Marketing intelligence ≠ Campaign authority
- **P18-MARKETING-001:** Consent, purpose, and source method must remain visible.
- **P18-MARKETING-002:** No output may activate an audience or campaign.
- **P18-MARKETING-003:** Attribution uncertainty must not be suppressed.

## Explicit Non-Responsibilities

No content generation for publication, targeting, personalization, campaign operation, media buying, budget use, channel invocation, workflow, Runtime action, or execution.
