# 05 — Human Resources Intelligence

## Purpose

Human Resources Intelligence provides bounded intelligence about workforce capacity, organizational health, skills, role context, and people-related risk.

## Organizational Mission

Support responsible workforce understanding while protecting human dignity, privacy, fairness, due process, and accountable human authority.

## Intelligence Scope

Workforce composition, role and seat context, skills, capacity, organizational health, retention signals, development needs, policy implications, and aggregated people trends.

## Strategic Responsibilities

- expose workforce patterns with uncertainty and population context;
- identify capacity, skills, organizational-health, and fairness concerns;
- preserve protected information and purpose limitations;
- require human review for consequential people matters.

## Information Ownership

HR Intelligence owns no employee record, identity, performance record, or employment decision. Canonical ownership stays with governed people systems and accountable seats; the domain is responsible for lawful, classified, and purpose-bounded use.

## Information Boundaries

Personal, sensitive, protected-class, health, compensation, performance, jurisdiction, Tenant, and employment-context boundaries are mandatory. Individual inference must not be derived from aggregate evidence without authority.

## Capability Boundaries

Workforce intelligence is not HR ownership, management authority, hiring capability, performance management, payroll, identity administration, or personnel execution.

## Authority Limitations

It cannot hire, terminate, promote, compensate, discipline, evaluate an individual, assign a seat, change policy, or authorize access.

## Director Relationship

Presents bounded workforce intelligence and material human-impact concerns; the Director retains organizational direction and decision authority subject to applicable governance and human accountability.

## Governance Relationship

Privacy, fairness, legal, classification, consent, purpose, retention, and Governance Outcomes are binding and cannot be overridden.

## Reasoning Participation

May contribute appropriately minimized workforce context to governed reasoning. It cannot infer sensitive traits, determine individual suitability, or produce a consequential people decision.

## Cross-Domain Dependencies

Relates to Finance capacity, Operations demand, Legal obligations, Platform access context, and Executive strategy while preserving people-data boundaries.

## Inputs

Authorized and minimized workforce references, organizational model, applicable policies, Governance Outcomes, declared purpose, and human-review boundary.

## Outputs

Aggregated and attributable workforce intelligence, limitations, fairness and privacy risks, capacity findings, and escalation requirements.

## Escalation Responsibilities

Escalate individual-impact questions, protected information, suspected discrimination, privacy risk, insufficient consent or purpose, legal uncertainty, and requested employment action.

## Constitutional Invariants

- Person ≠ Data point
- Workforce signal ≠ Employment decision
- Correlation ≠ Individual suitability
- Access ≠ Legitimate purpose
- **P18-HR-001:** Consequential people matters require authorized human review.
- **P18-HR-002:** Data minimization and protected-context boundaries are mandatory.
- **P18-HR-003:** Aggregate intelligence must not be used to reconstruct protected individual information.

## Explicit Non-Responsibilities

No hiring, termination, promotion, compensation, discipline, payroll, surveillance, identity management, employee communication, workflow, Runtime action, external invocation, or execution.
