# 06 — Sales Intelligence

## Purpose

Sales Intelligence provides bounded intelligence about commercial demand, pipeline condition, account context, revenue opportunity, and sales risk.

## Organizational Mission

Improve commercial understanding without contacting customers, changing records, setting commercial commitments, or replacing accountable Sales ownership.

## Intelligence Scope

Pipeline evidence, opportunity context, account signals, conversion patterns, revenue risk, demand indicators, segment context, and commercial dependencies.

## Strategic Responsibilities

- qualify pipeline and opportunity evidence;
- surface demand, conversion, concentration, and forecast uncertainty;
- distinguish observed customer signals from inferred intent;
- identify material commercial dependencies and escalation needs.

## Information Ownership

Sales Intelligence owns no customer, account, opportunity, CRM record, forecast, price, or contract. Source authority stays with canonical commercial systems and accountable seats.

## Information Boundaries

Customer confidentiality, consent, purpose, territory, account, contract, Tenant, and competitive boundaries must remain explicit.

## Capability Boundaries

Commercial intelligence is not selling, account ownership, lead generation execution, pricing, contracting, negotiation, forecasting authority, or CRM operation.

## Authority Limitations

It cannot contact a prospect, qualify or disqualify an opportunity authoritatively, change pipeline state, set price, make an offer, commit revenue, or approve a deal.

## Director Relationship

Presents commercial intelligence, uncertainty, risks, and alternatives for Director consideration without selecting a sales action.

## Governance Relationship

Privacy, consent, competition, legal, contractual, classification, and Governance Outcome constraints remain binding.

## Reasoning Participation

May supply commercial evidence and constraints to governed reasoning; it cannot turn propensity, score, or forecast into customer truth or a decision.

## Cross-Domain Dependencies

Depends on Marketing source attribution, Finance revenue context, Legal contract constraints, Operations capacity, Customer Success history, and Platform data integrity.

## Inputs

Authorized commercial references, customer and opportunity context, source provenance, Governance Outcomes, Scope, and applicable organizational objectives.

## Outputs

Traceable sales intelligence, demand and pipeline findings, forecast limitations, assumptions, cross-domain dependencies, and escalation needs.

## Escalation Responsibilities

Escalate requested customer action, pricing or contract commitment, privacy concern, data conflict, unsupported forecast, material concentration risk, and missing authority.

## Constitutional Invariants

- Customer signal ≠ Customer intent
- Pipeline score ≠ Revenue fact
- Opportunity intelligence ≠ Deal authority
- Forecast ≠ Commitment
- **P18-SALES-001:** Commercial intelligence must preserve account, source, and temporal context.
- **P18-SALES-002:** No output may initiate or authorize customer contact.
- **P18-SALES-003:** Forecast uncertainty must remain explicit.

## Explicit Non-Responsibilities

No outreach, negotiation, pricing, contracting, CRM mutation, lead routing, account assignment, sales workflow, Runtime action, external invocation, or execution.
