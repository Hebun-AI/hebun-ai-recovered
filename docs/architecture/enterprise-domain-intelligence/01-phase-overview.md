# 01 — Phase 18 Overview

## Purpose

Phase 18 defines the constitutional structure of enterprise intelligence across nine core business domains. It makes domain responsibility explicit without turning a domain into an Agent, department, authority, decision-maker, or executor.

## Architectural Responsibility

Each domain:

- frames intelligence questions within a bounded business subject;
- qualifies relevant information and its limitations;
- contributes attributable, non-authoritative intelligence;
- identifies cross-domain dependencies and conflicts;
- escalates authority, governance, evidence, and boundary questions;
- remains independently analyzable and replaceable at the realization layer.

## Required Outcomes

- nine canonical domain intelligence constitutions;
- one shared inheritance contract;
- explicit information and Capability boundaries;
- Director, Governance, Reasoning, and Decision Support relationships;
- cross-domain dependency semantics without communication design;
- forward compatibility with Phases 19–21+.

## Explicit Exclusions

No concrete Agent, department, organizational seat, Runtime, execution, tool, external system, communication protocol, shared memory, planning engine, workflow engine, task orchestration, Computer Use, automation, API, schema, prompt, model, infrastructure, or implementation is defined.

## Rules

- **P18-OVERVIEW-001:** Every domain must inherit the Phase 17 Agent Constitution.
- **P18-OVERVIEW-002:** Every domain must produce intelligence only.
- **P18-OVERVIEW-003:** Domain definition must not create organizational authority or ownership.
- **P18-OVERVIEW-004:** Phase 18 must not pre-empt Phases 19–24.
- **P18-OVERVIEW-005:** Domain boundaries must remain independently analyzable at enterprise scale.

## Enterprise Example

Finance Intelligence may expose an evidence-based liquidity concern. It cannot move funds, approve a budget, choose a response, or instruct another domain to act.
