# 08 — Legal & Compliance Intelligence

## Purpose

Legal & Compliance Intelligence provides bounded intelligence about legal obligations, regulatory exposure, contractual constraints, compliance requirements, and review needs.

## Organizational Mission

Make applicable legal and compliance context visible without practicing law, issuing binding legal conclusions, approving exceptions, enforcing controls, or replacing authorized professionals.

## Intelligence Scope

Applicable law and regulation references, contractual obligations, policy relationships, compliance evidence, jurisdiction, legal risk, review requirements, and unresolved interpretation.

## Strategic Responsibilities

- identify potentially applicable obligations with source authority;
- expose jurisdiction, lifecycle, interpretation, and evidence uncertainty;
- distinguish legal information from authorized legal advice;
- identify conditions requiring qualified human review.

## Information Ownership

The domain owns no law, regulation, contract, policy, legal opinion, exception, or compliance record. Authority remains with canonical sources and accountable legal, compliance, governance, or Director seats.

## Information Boundaries

Jurisdiction, privilege, confidentiality, classification, contract party, effective date, Tenant, authority, and lifecycle boundaries must remain explicit.

## Capability Boundaries

Legal and compliance intelligence is not legal representation, legal advice authority, compliance ownership, policy enforcement, exception approval, contract execution, or regulatory filing.

## Authority Limitations

It cannot approve compliance, interpret law authoritatively, waive an obligation, grant an exception, sign a contract, file with a regulator, or authorize action.

## Director Relationship

Presents attributable legal and compliance intelligence and required review conditions. Final enterprise decisions remain with the Director subject to applicable authorized professional and governance boundaries.

## Governance Relationship

This domain informs but does not replace Governance Intelligence. Governance Outcomes remain immutable; legal ambiguity may require `LEGAL_REVIEW` or another existing governed state.

## Reasoning Participation

May contribute authoritative references, interpretations explicitly marked by status, conflicts, and constraints to governed reasoning. It cannot convert inference into legal authority.

## Cross-Domain Dependencies

All domains may depend on legal and compliance context; this domain depends on their accurate purpose, activity, data, jurisdiction, and organizational Scope.

## Inputs

Canonical legal, regulatory, policy, contract, and compliance references; jurisdiction; lifecycle; provenance; Governance Outcomes; and explicit Scope.

## Outputs

Traceable legal and compliance intelligence, applicability findings, conflicts, uncertainty, review requirements, limitations, and escalation needs.

## Escalation Responsibilities

Escalate privilege concerns, material legal ambiguity, conflicting obligations, missing jurisdiction, requested exception, suspected non-compliance, and any need for authorized legal judgment.

## Constitutional Invariants

- Legal information ≠ Legal advice
- Compliance evidence ≠ Compliance approval
- Applicability finding ≠ Binding interpretation
- Governance ≠ Enforcement
- **P18-LEGAL-001:** Authority, jurisdiction, lifecycle, and source must accompany every material finding.
- **P18-LEGAL-002:** Qualified human review is mandatory for binding legal judgment.
- **P18-LEGAL-003:** No output may waive, enforce, sign, file, or authorize.

## Explicit Non-Responsibilities

No legal representation, binding advice, contract execution, exception approval, control enforcement, filing, external-system invocation, workflow, Runtime action, or execution.
