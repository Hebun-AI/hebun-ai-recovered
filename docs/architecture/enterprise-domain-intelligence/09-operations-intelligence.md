# 09 — Operations Intelligence

## Purpose

Operations Intelligence provides bounded intelligence about operational capacity, performance, quality, dependencies, continuity, constraints, and risk.

## Organizational Mission

Make operational conditions and implications visible without scheduling, dispatching, controlling, repairing, or executing operations.

## Intelligence Scope

Capacity, throughput, quality, service levels, constraints, dependencies, process evidence, continuity risk, operational incidents, and resource context.

## Strategic Responsibilities

- qualify operational performance and constraint evidence;
- expose bottlenecks, dependencies, quality, and continuity risks;
- preserve the difference between observed state and canonical truth;
- identify operational conditions requiring human or Director review.

## Information Ownership

Operations Intelligence owns no process, asset, schedule, inventory, incident, resource, or operational record. Source authority and operational ownership remain with canonical systems and accountable seats.

## Information Boundaries

Facility, process, asset, time, product, service, jurisdiction, Tenant, classification, and observation-source boundaries must remain explicit.

## Capability Boundaries

Operational intelligence is not operations ownership, process control, scheduling, dispatch, inventory management, incident response, workflow, or Runtime control.

## Authority Limitations

It cannot start, stop, schedule, prioritize, reroute, repair, allocate, change process state, or authorize operational action.

## Director Relationship

Presents operational intelligence, constraints, alternatives, uncertainty, and material risks; it does not choose or authorize an operational response.

## Governance Relationship

Safety, quality, policy, permission, compliance, privacy, and Governance Outcomes remain binding and immutable.

## Reasoning Participation

May contribute observations, constraints, dependencies, and historical patterns to governed reasoning. It cannot treat telemetry as truth or turn a finding into control.

## Cross-Domain Dependencies

Depends on Sales demand, Finance constraints, HR capacity, Legal obligations, Customer outcomes, Platform health, and Executive objectives.

## Inputs

Authorized operational references, observations, classifications, provenance, applicable Governance Outcomes, Scope, and organizational context.

## Outputs

Traceable operational intelligence, capacity and constraint findings, quality and continuity risks, uncertainty, dependencies, and escalation needs.

## Escalation Responsibilities

Escalate safety or continuity risk, material data conflict, missing authority, critical constraint, requested operational action, and conditions outside declared Scope.

## Constitutional Invariants

- Observation ≠ Truth
- Operational intelligence ≠ Operational control
- Constraint finding ≠ Scheduling decision
- Incident signal ≠ Remediation authorization
- **P18-OPERATIONS-001:** Operational context and observation provenance must remain explicit.
- **P18-OPERATIONS-002:** No output may alter operational state.
- **P18-OPERATIONS-003:** Safety and continuity concerns require governed escalation.

## Explicit Non-Responsibilities

No scheduling, dispatch, process control, inventory mutation, incident response, resource allocation, workflow, automation, Runtime action, external invocation, or execution.
