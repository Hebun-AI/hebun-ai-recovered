# 03 — Executive Intelligence

## Purpose

Executive Intelligence provides enterprise-wide strategic synthesis for Director consideration without becoming executive authority.

## Organizational Mission

Make strategic conditions, enterprise performance tensions, dependencies, risks, and alternatives visible across domains while preserving the Director's ownership of strategy and final decisions.

## Intelligence Scope

Enterprise objectives, strategic alignment, portfolio context, organizational health, cross-domain impacts, material risks, and decision-support readiness.

## Strategic Responsibilities

- synthesize attributable domain intelligence;
- expose strategic trade-offs, uncertainty, and conflicting signals;
- identify issues requiring Director attention;
- preserve domain-specific dissent rather than manufacture one enterprise view.

## Information Ownership

Executive Intelligence owns no source information. It is responsible for the integrity and provenance of its enterprise synthesis; authoritative domain information remains with its canonical source and accountable organizational seat.

## Information Boundaries

Only appropriately classified, Tenant-bounded, purpose-compatible information may enter synthesis. Domain detail must not be flattened beyond what evidence supports.

## Capability Boundaries

Executive intelligence capabilities remain separate from Executive departments, seats, Agents, strategy ownership, portfolio approval, planning, and execution.

## Authority Limitations

It cannot set strategy, priorities, budgets, policy, ownership, approval, authorization, or enterprise direction.

## Director Relationship

It presents bounded strategic intelligence, conflicts, options, and limitations. The Director accepts, rejects, requests clarification, or takes no action.

## Governance Relationship

Governance Outcomes and restrictions remain immutable. Executive synthesis cannot waive, reinterpret, or override them.

## Reasoning Participation

It may frame enterprise context for governed reasoning but cannot reason independently, rank a final choice, or convert findings into a decision.

## Cross-Domain Dependencies

Depends on all eight other domains for attributable intelligence and must preserve their identities, boundaries, and uncertainty.

## Inputs

Domain intelligence contributions, canonical strategy references, organizational context, approved objectives, Governance Outcomes, and explicit Director Scope.

## Outputs

Traceable executive intelligence synthesis, strategic tensions, material dependencies, uncertainty, limitations, and escalation needs.

## Escalation Responsibilities

Escalate strategic conflict, missing domain evidence, unresolved material risk, authority ambiguity, governance exception, and attempted decision or approval substitution to the Director.

## Constitutional Invariants

- Executive Intelligence ≠ Director
- Synthesis ≠ Strategy ownership
- Enterprise view ≠ Canonical truth
- Priority signal ≠ Decision
- **P18-EXECUTIVE-001:** Executive synthesis must preserve domain provenance and dissent.
- **P18-EXECUTIVE-002:** No executive output may imply Director approval.
- **P18-EXECUTIVE-003:** Cross-domain aggregation must not create authority.

## Explicit Non-Responsibilities

No strategy setting, prioritization decision, approval, budgeting, planning, task assignment, communication orchestration, execution, Runtime action, external invocation, or organizational ownership.
