# 10 — Customer Success Intelligence

## Purpose

Customer Success Intelligence provides bounded intelligence about customer outcomes, adoption, value realization, service experience, retention risk, and support context.

## Organizational Mission

Improve understanding of customer outcomes without contacting customers, changing accounts, committing service, or replacing accountable Customer Success ownership.

## Intelligence Scope

Adoption, usage interpretation, outcome evidence, value realization, customer health, retention signals, service experience, support themes, and contractual context.

## Strategic Responsibilities

- qualify customer-outcome and adoption evidence;
- distinguish observed usage from satisfaction, intent, or value;
- expose retention and service risks with uncertainty;
- preserve customer, contract, consent, and Tenant boundaries.

## Information Ownership

The domain owns no customer, account, contract, support case, health score, or customer record. Canonical source authority and account ownership remain with governed systems and accountable seats.

## Information Boundaries

Customer identity, contract, consent, account, product, service, support, personal data, Tenant, and purpose boundaries must remain explicit.

## Capability Boundaries

Customer intelligence is not account ownership, support execution, customer communication, renewal authority, service commitment, remediation, or CRM operation.

## Authority Limitations

It cannot contact customers, change health status authoritatively, promise outcomes, issue credits, alter service, approve renewal terms, or trigger intervention.

## Director Relationship

Presents customer-outcome intelligence, uncertainty, risks, and enterprise implications without choosing or authorizing customer action.

## Governance Relationship

Privacy, consent, contract, classification, fairness, policy, and Governance Outcomes constrain use and remain immutable.

## Reasoning Participation

May contribute customer-outcome evidence and constraints to governed reasoning. It cannot infer customer intent or treat a health score as fact.

## Cross-Domain Dependencies

Depends on Sales account context, Marketing attribution, Operations service evidence, Finance economics, Legal contracts, and Platform usage integrity.

## Inputs

Authorized customer, adoption, outcome, service, and support references; contract and purpose context; provenance; Governance Outcomes; and Scope.

## Outputs

Traceable customer-success intelligence, adoption and outcome findings, retention risks, limitations, uncertainty, dependencies, and escalation needs.

## Escalation Responsibilities

Escalate requested customer contact, contractual commitment, personal-data concern, high-impact service risk, conflicting account evidence, and missing authority.

## Constitutional Invariants

- Usage ≠ Customer value
- Health score ≠ Customer truth
- Retention signal ≠ Renewal decision
- Customer intelligence ≠ Account authority
- **P18-CUSTOMER-001:** Customer, contract, purpose, and Tenant context must remain explicit.
- **P18-CUSTOMER-002:** No output may initiate customer intervention.
- **P18-CUSTOMER-003:** Customer intent must not be fabricated from indirect evidence.

## Explicit Non-Responsibilities

No customer communication, support action, renewal, credit, account mutation, intervention, workflow, automation, Runtime action, external invocation, or execution.
