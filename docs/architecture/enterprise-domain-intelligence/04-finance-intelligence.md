# 04 — Finance Intelligence

## Purpose

Finance Intelligence provides bounded, evidence-grounded intelligence about enterprise financial condition and implications.

## Organizational Mission

Make financial performance, exposure, constraints, uncertainty, and material trade-offs visible without controlling money or replacing accountable Finance ownership.

## Intelligence Scope

Revenue, cost, cash, liquidity, budget variance, forecast evidence, unit economics, financial risk, investment context, and financial-control implications.

## Strategic Responsibilities

- qualify financial evidence and variance;
- surface material financial trends and uncertainty;
- identify financial dependencies and constraint impacts;
- present non-authoritative financial intelligence for governed review.

## Information Ownership

Finance Intelligence owns no ledger, account, budget, forecast, or financial record. Source authority remains with canonical financial systems and accountable organizational seats; the domain is responsible for provenance and fitness of its contribution.

## Information Boundaries

Financial classification, confidentiality, jurisdiction, accounting period, currency, Tenant, and entity boundaries must remain explicit.

## Capability Boundaries

Financial analysis capabilities are not Finance departments, banking authority, accounting ownership, payment capability, procurement authority, or execution attachment.

## Authority Limitations

It cannot approve budgets, allocate capital, price products, move funds, create accounting entries, authorize spend, or determine financial policy.

## Director Relationship

Presents financial facts, interpretations, conflicts, risks, and limitations to the Director; it never selects or authorizes a financial course.

## Governance Relationship

Applicable policy, permission, compliance, privacy, and Governance Outcomes constrain use and remain immutable.

## Reasoning Participation

May contribute financial premises and domain constraints to governed reasoning; it cannot perform autonomous forecasting decisions or treat a projection as truth.

## Cross-Domain Dependencies

Consumes attributable commercial, workforce, marketing, operational, customer, legal, and platform context without absorbing their ownership.

## Inputs

Canonical financial references, approved organizational context, classified domain evidence, applicable Governance Outcomes, and explicit Scope.

## Outputs

Traceable financial intelligence, variance and exposure findings, uncertainty, assumptions, cross-domain implications, and escalation needs.

## Escalation Responsibilities

Escalate material misstatement risk, missing authority, conflicting sources, policy or compliance concerns, high-impact uncertainty, and requested financial commitment.

## Constitutional Invariants

- Financial intelligence ≠ Financial authority
- Forecast ≠ Fact
- Budget signal ≠ Approval
- Access ≠ Permission
- **P18-FINANCE-001:** Financial periods, currency, entity, and provenance must remain explicit.
- **P18-FINANCE-002:** Financial intelligence must never initiate a transaction.
- **P18-FINANCE-003:** Financial uncertainty must not be hidden by aggregation.

## Explicit Non-Responsibilities

No bookkeeping, payment, pricing action, budget approval, procurement, banking operation, external-system invocation, Runtime action, workflow, planning, or execution.
