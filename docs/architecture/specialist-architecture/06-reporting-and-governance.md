# 06 — Reporting & Governance

## Purpose

Reporting & Governance defines **how a Specialist reports to its Manager and stays governed and traceable**. A Specialist governs nothing, but it is itself governed: it reports upward honestly, escalates what exceeds its grant, and falls under the enterprise-wide governance regime. This document closes the Specialist's accountability loop.

## Architectural role

Where responsibilities ([03](03-specialist-responsibilities.md)) define what a Specialist owns and authority boundaries ([04](04-specialist-authority-boundaries.md)) fence what it may do, reporting and governance define how it stays accountable for both. Reporting carries status up to the Manager; escalation raises what exceeds the grant; governance holds the Specialist to its bounds and makes it auditable.

## The model — reporting

### A Specialist reports to its Manager, honestly
A Specialist reports the status, progress, and problems of its capability to its Manager ([manager oversight & reporting](../manager-architecture/05-oversight-and-reporting.md)). Reporting is truthful — a Specialist does not hide a failure, overstate progress, or claim an outcome that did not happen ([specialist principles](01-specialist-principles.md)). Honest reporting is what lets the Manager oversee the department.

### A Specialist escalates beyond-grant matters to its Manager
When a matter exceeds a Specialist's delegated authority — a committing action needing Director approval, a cross-capability or cross-department matter, or a need for reasoning, planning, or a decision — the Specialist escalates to its Manager rather than acting beyond its grant ([authority boundaries](04-specialist-authority-boundaries.md)). Escalation, not overreach, is the correct response to a limit.

### Reporting is upward, not governing
A Specialist reports and escalates upward; it never governs, directs, or overrides its Manager ([authority boundaries](04-specialist-authority-boundaries.md)). Accountability flows up the hierarchy; authority flows down. A Specialist surfaces; the Manager governs; Director Intelligence decides.

## The model — governance

### A Specialist falls under enterprise governance — no exceptions
There is one governance regime, and every Specialist is inside it ([enterprise governance](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md), [department governance](../department-architecture/06-department-governance.md)). A Specialist invents no governance of its own and is never exempt.

### Governance aligns with Director Authority
A Specialist's governance expresses the Director's standards at the seat; it never sits above the Director ([enterprise authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md)). Where a Specialist's action would exceed governance or its grant, governance blocks it and forces escalation.

### A Specialist is fully traceable
A Specialist's capability ownership, delegated authority, reports, escalations, and collaborations can all be reconstructed and audited. Traceability is structural — a property of the seat, not of any run of work ([specialist responsibilities](03-specialist-responsibilities.md)).

## What reporting & governance preserve

- **Manager accountability** — the Specialist reports and escalates to its Manager, keeping the Manager's oversight real.
- **Department ownership** — the Specialist's capability ownership nests within the department's domain ownership.
- **Director Authority** — beyond-grant and committing matters escalate toward the Director.
- **Enterprise governance** — one regime, reaching the Specialist, no exemptions.
- **Traceability** — the Specialist's whole role is auditable.

## Inputs

- The **specialist responsibilities and authority boundaries** ([03](03-specialist-responsibilities.md), [04](04-specialist-authority-boundaries.md)) — what is reported on and what must escalate.
- The **manager reporting line and enterprise governance regime** ([9C](../manager-architecture/05-oversight-and-reporting.md), [9A](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)) — where reports go and the standards applied.

## Outputs

- **Reports and escalations** — honest status to the Manager and beyond-grant matters raised upward.
- A **governance and traceability frame** — the Specialist held to its bounds and made auditable.

## Boundaries

- Reports and governs **the specialist seat, not a concrete specialist** — it names none.
- Performs **no work, reasoning, or orchestration** — it surfaces and records; it does not resolve or execute.
- Governs **nothing** — a Specialist is governed, not a governor.
- Describes **no runtime or mechanism** — only the structure of reporting and governance.

## Future direction

As human and AI specialists fill the seat, reporting and governance apply unchanged — the moment a Specialist exists, it reports to its Manager, escalates at its limit, and falls under the enterprise regime. Governance may deepen its checks over time ([future evolution](07-future-evolution.md)), but it stays enterprise-anchored, Director-topped, and traceable. Specialists multiply; none escapes its bounds.
