# 05 — Governance Validation

## Purpose

Verify that the enterprise architecture is governed by **one** Director-anchored regime — applied continuously and uniformly across every domain, level, and occupant, with no exemption, no parallel regime, and no conflict.

## Method

Governance was traced from its origin (9A enterprise governance + authority model) through every domain's governance treatment, checking for a single regime, alignment with Director Authority, and no redefinition.

## Findings

### One regime, defined once — PASS
Enterprise governance is defined in 9A ([enterprise governance](../enterprise-organization/06-enterprise-governance.md)) as one enterprise-wide, Director-anchored regime. Every other domain's governance document explicitly **applies** this regime rather than defining a new one:
- Department governance — "under enterprise governance, no exceptions" ([9B](../department-architecture/06-department-governance.md)).
- Manager escalation & governance — "under the enterprise regime, no exemptions" ([9C](../manager-architecture/06-escalation-governance.md)).
- Specialist reporting & governance — "one regime, no exemptions" ([9D](../specialist-architecture/06-reporting-and-governance.md)).
- Collaboration governance — "the one regime applied to collaboration" ([9E](../cross-organization-collaboration/06-collaboration-governance.md)).
- Human governance — "redefines no governance; the one regime through human occupants" ([9F](../human-organization/06-human-governance.md)).
- Governance cycle — "the existing regime running continuously" ([9G](../enterprise-operating-model/04-governance-cycle.md)).

No domain forks governance. The regime is singular.

### Director Authority alignment — PASS
Every governance treatment states that governance expresses the Director's standards and never sits above the Director. Committing and beyond-authority matters escalate to the Director everywhere ([authority model](../enterprise-organization/04-authority-model.md), and each domain's governance/escalation document). No governance claims authority over the Director.

### No exemptions — PASS
No domain, level, or occupant is exempt. Human occupants explicitly fall under the same regime as AI occupants ([9F](../human-organization/06-human-governance.md)); no seat or department is outside governance.

### Continuity — PASS
The operating model makes governance continuous rather than occasional ([9G governance cycle](../enterprise-operating-model/04-governance-cycle.md)), running on the organizational rhythm. Governance is in force at every point in operation — a genuine addition by 9G, consistent with (not a redefinition of) the 9A regime.

### Traceability under governance — PASS
Governance and traceability are paired in every domain: what is governed is also reconstructable and auditable. Consistent framing throughout.

### No conflicting authority model — PASS
There is exactly one authority model (delegated, downward, bounded, revocable, Director-topped, committing-gated). No domain introduces a second or contradictory model. Confirmed in [Consistency Review](02-consistency-review.md).

## Verdict

**PASS.** One Director-anchored governance regime, applied uniformly and continuously across all seven domains, no exemptions, no conflict, no redefinition.
