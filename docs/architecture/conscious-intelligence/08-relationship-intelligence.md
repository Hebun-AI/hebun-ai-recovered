# 08 — Relationship Intelligence

## Definition

Relationship Intelligence maintains consent-bound context that helps users honor commitments and collaborate coherently. It is a context and follow-up aid, not a persuasion, profiling, surveillance, or emotional influence system.

## Supported Context

- communication preferences explicitly provided or approved;
- commitments and follow-ups;
- shared history;
- collaboration context;
- meeting summaries;
- important dates;
- relationship-scoped memories and evidence;
- corrections, consent status, and retention rules.

## Relationship Context Contract

Every item must identify:

- context owner;
- subject or parties;
- source and evidence;
- purpose of use;
- consent or lawful basis;
- sensitivity;
- visibility boundary;
- confidence;
- retention and review status;
- correction, revocation, and version history.

## Allowed Uses

- remind the user of a commitment;
- summarize an approved shared history;
- adapt to an explicit communication preference;
- identify contradictory commitments;
- prepare neutral meeting context;
- prompt the user to verify sensitive or uncertain information.

## Prohibited Uses

- psychological profiling for persuasion;
- inferred vulnerability targeting;
- emotional exploitation;
- covert sentiment scoring;
- manipulation of attachment, guilt, fear, urgency, or trust;
- disclosure across relationship boundaries;
- claiming knowledge of another person's private intent;
- impersonation or autonomous communication.

## Enterprise Example

Before a partner meeting, Heby may show the last approved commitments, unresolved follow-ups, and preferred meeting format. It must not suggest exploiting the partner's emotional state or infer hidden motivations.

## Boundaries

Relationship context ≠ psychological profile, preference ≠ permanent trait, meeting summary ≠ shared truth, and assistance ≠ persuasion.
