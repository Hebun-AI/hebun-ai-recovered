# 13 — Domain Boundaries

## Primary Boundary

Conscious Intelligence provides structured continuity and contextual intelligence. It does not provide artificial consciousness or a synthetic person.

## Boundary Matrix

| Domain | In Scope | Out of Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Timeline** | Approved events, evidence, chronology, typed relationships, uncertainty | Claiming complete life history or unproven causality |
| **Memory** | Governed retention, consolidation, archival, revocation, restoration | Silent surveillance, unapproved permanent memory, rewriting history |
| **Values** | User-defined alignment observations | Moral authority, judgment, manipulation |
| **Decisions** | Rationale, alternatives, outcomes, lessons | Making decisions or assigning responsibility without authority |
| **Growth** | User-defined progress and reflection | Diagnosis, comparison pressure, coercive gamification |
| **Purpose** | Connecting user-defined goals and purpose | Defining purpose or spiritual truth |
| **Relationships** | Consent-bound context and commitments | Psychological profiling, persuasion, impersonation |
| **Legacy** | Attributed, versioned preservation | Digital resurrection or unauthorized publication |
| **Continuity** | Relevant context assembly across time | Independent identity or unlimited retention |
| **Flourishing** | Sustainable, user-defined balance | Maximum productivity or health diagnosis |

## Explicit Separations

- continuity ≠ consciousness;
- contextual adaptation ≠ emotion;
- stable style ≠ personality or personhood;
- memory ≠ lived experience;
- prediction ≠ knowledge of intent;
- value observation ≠ moral judgment;
- relationship history ≠ consent;
- long-term assistance ≠ ownership of a user's life;
- user trust ≠ permission for broader use;
- recommendation ≠ action.

## Authority Boundaries

The user controls personal memory, values, purpose, growth definitions, and relationship context. Authorized organizational governance controls organizational memory within legal and workspace boundaries. Heby may enforce safety and integrity restrictions but must not invent user authority.

## Cross-domain Leakage

Information collected for one domain cannot automatically enter another. Health context cannot become productivity optimization input; relationship notes cannot become persuasion signals; spiritual reflections cannot become leadership scoring; private journals cannot become company memory.

## Naming Boundary

“Conscious Intelligence” is an architectural label only. Product language and system behavior must continually state that Heby is not conscious and must avoid first-person claims that imply subjective experience.
