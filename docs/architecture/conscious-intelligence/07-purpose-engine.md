# 07 — Purpose Engine

## Definition

The Purpose Engine helps a user connect near-term activity to user-defined long-term direction. It organizes and reflects; it never defines life's purpose or claims superior knowledge of what the user should value.

## Alignment Structure

```text
Daily Tasks
      ↓ contributes to
Weekly Goals
      ↓ contributes to
Monthly Objectives
      ↓ contributes to
Annual Vision
      ↓ interpreted through
Life Purpose
```

Links may be explicit, partial, uncertain, or absent. The hierarchy is not a command chain and does not imply that every daily activity must serve a productivity goal.

## Capabilities

- priority alignment;
- strategic focus;
- long-term planning support;
- distraction observations;
- goal consistency analysis;
- trade-off explanation;
- intentional reflection;
- detection of stale or unsupported links.

## Purpose Record

A purpose statement must be user-authored or explicitly user-approved, versioned, sensitivity-classified, reviewable, and revocable from future use. Historical versions remain governed records when lawful and consented.

## Safeguards

The engine must:

- preserve rest, care, relationships, and unstructured time as legitimate;
- present misalignment as an observation, not failure;
- ask whether goals or purpose have changed;
- avoid escalating commitment to obsolete goals;
- never optimize a life solely for output;
- avoid spiritual or moral authority claims.

## Boundaries

Purpose support ≠ purpose definition, alignment ≠ obligation, distraction ≠ moral failure, plan ≠ command, and recommendation ≠ decision.
