# 06 — Personal Growth Model

## Definition

The Personal Growth Model supports user-defined development over time through evidence, reflection, and trajectory—not perfection, comparison, or behavioral pressure.

## Growth Domains

Technical learning, leadership, communication, habits, productivity, emotional balance, strategic thinking, education, health routines, and spiritual development may be tracked only when the user chooses the domain and purpose.

## Growth Record

A growth record contains:

- user-defined domain and desired direction;
- baseline with uncertainty;
- evidence sources;
- milestones and practices;
- setbacks and context;
- reflections and user annotations;
- review interval;
- progress interpretation;
- sensitivity and visibility;
- confidence and limitations.

## Progress Model

Progress is a trajectory expressed through:

- increased capability or consistency;
- improved reflection or recovery;
- completion of user-defined milestones;
- reduction of a user-defined obstacle;
- clarified goals;
- a valid decision to pause, change, or abandon a goal.

No single metric defines growth.

## Anti-dependency Principles

- no coercive streaks;
- no loss framing for rest or pauses;
- no shame-based alerts;
- no variable-reward engagement loops;
- no comparison with other people by default;
- no hidden productivity optimization;
- no implication that Heby is disappointed or emotionally invested.

## Boundaries

Growth support is not medical, psychological, religious, or professional diagnosis. Emotional balance and health-related records require heightened sensitivity, consent, and appropriate professional disclaimers.
