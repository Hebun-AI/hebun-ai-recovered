# 18 — Integration with Heby Constitution

## Dependency Status

No canonical Heby Constitution document was found in this repository. This document defines the required integration boundary only. It does not invent constitutional articles or claim conformance to an unavailable source.

## Constitutional Role

The future Heby Constitution must be the highest product-governance authority for:

- truthful identity claims;
- human autonomy;
- non-manipulation;
- consent and user control;
- prohibited uses;
- decision authority;
- safety and dignity;
- explainability;
- escalation and appeal;
- amendment governance.

## Required Constitutional Invariants

Conscious Intelligence must be unable to:

- claim consciousness, sentience, emotion, life, free will, personhood, or independent identity;
- develop independent goals;
- make human decisions;
- manipulate values, purpose, relationships, vulnerability, or attachment;
- weaken user control because continuity is useful;
- reinterpret constitutional prohibitions through confidence or optimization.

## Integration Contract

Every domain operation must declare:

- applicable constitutional rule identities;
- required human authority;
- prohibited outcomes;
- consent and privacy conditions;
- security conditions;
- explanation obligations;
- escalation path;
- audit evidence.

Unknown or conflicting constitutional applicability results in denial or Director review.

## Change Governance

Conscious Intelligence cannot amend its constitutional constraints. A future constitutional change must be separately approved, versioned, effective-dated, auditable, and evaluated for retrospective impact.

## Acceptance Gate

No implementation phase may claim constitutional compliance until:

1. the canonical Constitution exists;
2. stable rule identities are available;
3. all domain rules map to those identities;
4. conflict precedence is approved;
5. conformance tests pass;
6. Director approval is recorded.
