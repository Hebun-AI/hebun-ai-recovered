# 02 — Life Timeline Model

## Definition

The Life Timeline is an evidence-linked temporal view of user-approved personal and organizational events. It organizes history without claiming that sequence proves causality or that the record is complete.

## Event Categories

Supported categories include personal milestones, company milestones, projects, learning journeys, decisions, achievements, failures, major conversations, strategic pivots, health goals, educational progress, relationship milestones, and life events.

Categories aid navigation; they do not define a person's identity or worth.

## Timeline Event Contract

Every event must carry:

- event identity and category;
- owner and visibility boundary;
- occurred-at time or explicit uncertainty interval;
- recorded-at time;
- source and supporting evidence;
- confidence and review status;
- sensitivity and retention policy;
- related events and memories;
- causal claim status;
- version and supersession history;
- user-approved annotations.

## Relationships

Permitted logical relationships include:

- precedes / follows;
- overlaps;
- part of;
- relates to;
- supports;
- contradicts;
- supersedes;
- possible cause of;
- reported consequence of.

`Possible cause of` must never be presented as proven causality without adequate evidence.

## Editable Annotations

Annotations are separate, versioned interpretive overlays. Editing an annotation creates a new version; it does not rewrite the event or its original evidence. A user may revoke an annotation from active use while audit requirements preserve a minimal, access-restricted revocation record.

## Historical Reconstruction

Reconstruction must:

1. state the requested time boundary;
2. use evidence valid for that historical point;
3. distinguish known events from inferred gaps;
4. preserve conflicting accounts;
5. explain confidence and missing evidence;
6. avoid hindsight being represented as past knowledge.

## Visualization Readiness

The model supports chronological, grouped, relationship, and evidence views through stable identities and typed relationships. It defines no user interface.

## Boundaries

Timeline ≠ Memory, chronology ≠ causality, annotation ≠ fact, reconstruction ≠ truth, and omission ≠ proof that an event did not occur.
