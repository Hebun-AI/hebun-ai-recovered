# 23 — Implementation Roadmap

## Principle

Implementation must proceed from governance to data, from reversible read-only capability to carefully approved writes, and from low-sensitivity continuity to explicitly consented high-sensitivity domains.

This roadmap selects no technology and authorizes no implementation by itself.

## Stage 0 — Canonical Dependency Closure

- author and approve the Heby Constitution;
- author and approve Security Sentinel and Security Operating System contracts;
- define Adaptive Character and Wisdom Engine boundaries;
- map all Conscious Intelligence rules to stable canonical identities;
- resolve legal and privacy obligations.

**Gate:** No unresolved constitutional or security dependency.

## Stage 1 — Core Governance and Logical Contracts

- ratify domain boundaries;
- ratify logical records and relationships;
- define consent, retention, sensitivity, review, revocation, audit, and deletion semantics;
- reconcile personal continuity with Organizational Memory contracts.

**Gate:** Director, security, privacy, and memory-governance approval.

## Stage 2 — Read-only Timeline and Continuity

- expose user-inspectable evidence-linked timelines;
- assemble temporary continuity packages;
- support gaps, conflicts, provenance, and confidence;
- prohibit permanent-memory creation through this stage.

**Gate:** Temporal correctness, isolation, and explanation tests.

## Stage 3 — Governed Memory Admission

- introduce candidate memory;
- add integrity validation and explicit permanent-memory approval;
- support annotations, supersession, archival, restoration, and revocation propagation;
- provide inspection, export, and correction controls.

**Gate:** Memory Integrity, privacy, security, and recovery validation.

## Stage 4 — Decision and Learning Reflection

- decision history and outcome review;
- evidence-bounded pattern analysis;
- personal learning trajectories;
- non-coercive recommendations.

**Gate:** Reasoning quality, autonomy, and anti-manipulation validation.

## Stage 5 — Values, Purpose, and Growth

- opt-in value alignment;
- user-authored purpose links;
- personal growth reflection;
- sensitive-domain controls.

**Gate:** heightened privacy review and human-centered evaluation.

## Stage 6 — Relationship, Legacy, and Flourishing

- consent-bound relationship context;
- legacy preservation and succession governance;
- human flourishing reflection;
- independent review of emotional and dependency risks.

**Gate:** third-party privacy, publication, non-diagnostic, and non-manipulation approval.

## Stage 7 — External Engine Integrations

- integrate only with approved Constitution, Security Sentinel, Adaptive Character, and Wisdom contracts;
- verify deny precedence, data minimization, revocation, and explanation;
- run adversarial longitudinal testing.

**Gate:** Director approval for bounded production use.

## Rollback and Reversibility

Every stage must be independently disableable. Disabling analysis must not destroy historical records. Revocation and policy changes must invalidate future use and derived continuity packages. Rollback must preserve auditability without preserving prohibited content beyond lawful necessity.

## Deferred Capabilities

Autonomous execution, independent goals, artificial personhood, emotional simulation, covert persuasion, diagnostic use, unrestricted sensitive-memory retention, and self-modification are permanently outside this roadmap unless the Heby Constitution explicitly prohibits them more strongly—not candidates for later delivery.
