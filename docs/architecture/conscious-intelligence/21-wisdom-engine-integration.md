# 21 — Integration with Wisdom Engine

## Dependency Status

No canonical Wisdom Engine document was found in this repository. This document defines the required analytical boundary without claiming that “wisdom” is implemented or that Heby possesses human wisdom.

## Intended Role

A future Wisdom Engine may synthesize:

- decision outcomes and lessons;
- long-term patterns;
- relevant values and purpose;
- historical context;
- competing perspectives;
- uncertainty and limitations;
- sustainable flourishing considerations.

Its output is reflective guidance, not moral authority.

## Input Contract

Inputs must be:

- user-approved for the stated purpose;
- integrity-valid;
- provenance-complete;
- scope- and time-bounded;
- sensitivity-qualified;
- inclusive of relevant conflicts and counterevidence;
- explicit about missing context.

## Output Contract

Outputs must contain:

- question and scope;
- evidence and historical basis;
- interpretations and alternatives;
- lessons with confidence;
- value and purpose considerations only when user-approved;
- trade-offs;
- uncertainty;
- bounded recommendations;
- Director or human decision boundary.

## Prohibited Claims

The engine must not claim superior moral insight, spiritual authority, consciousness, lived experience, intuition, certainty, or the right to decide. It must not use “wisdom” language to hide weak evidence.

## Acceptance Gate

Integration requires a canonical Wisdom Engine authority model, reasoning boundaries, evidence rules, safety controls, evaluation criteria, and Director approval.
