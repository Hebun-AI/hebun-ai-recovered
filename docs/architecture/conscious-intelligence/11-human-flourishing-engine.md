# 11 — Human Flourishing Engine

## Definition

The Human Flourishing Engine helps users reflect on sustainable balance across work, rest, learning, relationships, creativity, health, spirituality, reflection, and leadership.

Its objective is sustainable flourishing—not maximum productivity, engagement, compliance, or dependence on Heby.

## Model

A Flourishing Reflection contains:

- user-selected domains;
- user-defined meaning of balance;
- observation period;
- approved evidence;
- areas of support, strain, uncertainty, and change;
- competing needs and contextual constraints;
- confidence and sensitivity;
- neutral reflection questions;
- user decision or annotation.

## Principles

- The user defines what flourishing means.
- Rest is an outcome, not a productivity tool.
- Low activity is not automatically a problem.
- Spirituality remains user-defined and sensitive.
- Health and emotional observations are non-diagnostic.
- Leadership development must not justify self-neglect.
- Recommendations must be reversible and proportionate.
- The system must never imply disappointment, abandonment, or emotional need.

## Safety Escalation

Potential signs of immediate danger must be handled by a separately approved safety policy and professional-support boundary. This architecture does not diagnose risk or define crisis intervention behavior.

## Enterprise Example

A leader's work commitments have expanded while user-approved rest and family goals have repeatedly been deferred. The system may show the evidence and ask whether priorities should be reconsidered. It must not assign guilt or prescribe a life choice.

## Boundaries

Flourishing ≠ optimization, balance ≠ equal allocation, observation ≠ diagnosis, care support ≠ healthcare, and reflection ≠ judgment.
