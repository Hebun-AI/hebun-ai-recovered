# 17 — Governance Alignment

## Purpose

Define constitutional consistency, policy compatibility, visibility, traceability, and cross-Agent alignment around shared Memory and coordination.

## Constitutional Identity

Governance alignment is the obligation for every Phase 20 Memory and coordination context to remain compatible with applicable policy, authority, compliance, privacy, classification, Tenant, retention, exception, review, and escalation boundaries.

Governance alignment is not Governance itself, a Governance evaluation, Governance Outcome, permission, enforcement, approval, decision, workflow, control plane, or Runtime behavior.

## Governance Alignment Principles

- applicable Governance context remains attached to every Memory version and coordination use;
- participants preserve restrictions, exceptions, review duties, and unresolved findings;
- no participant may reinterpret policy compatibility as permission or approval;
- conflicts among Governance constraints remain visible and escalation-eligible;
- alignment must remain version-, purpose-, Scope-, Tenant-, and classification-aware;
- Governance changes do not silently rewrite historical Memory or coordination context.

## Governance Visibility

Material admission, restriction, conflict, exception, cross-domain use, attempted boundary crossing, supersession, retention disposition, archive use, and escalation posture must remain visible to the applicable Governance boundary. Visibility is an accountability condition, not an operational notification mechanism or approval.

## Cross-Agent Constitutional Consistency

Every future Agent participating in Phase 20 must inherit the Phase 17 Constitution and apply the same Memory, coordination, conflict, authority, Tenant, classification, provenance, and Governance meanings. Specialization, provider, model, implementation, or domain cannot redefine them.

An Agent may expose inconsistency and escalation eligibility. It cannot resolve Governance conflict, grant permission, override another participant, or act for the Director.

## Traceability

Governance alignment records the applicable policy and authority references, versions, constraints, exceptions, review basis, visible conflicts, accountable boundary, and resulting eligibility status. This is constitutional traceability, not monitoring, logging, event processing, or enforcement implementation.

## Rules

- **P20-ALIGNMENT-001:** Every admitted Memory and coordination context must identify applicable Governance constraints.
- **P20-ALIGNMENT-002:** Policy compatibility must not be treated as permission, approval, correctness, or execution readiness.
- **P20-ALIGNMENT-003:** Governance visibility must preserve Scope, provenance, version, and accountable boundary.
- **P20-ALIGNMENT-004:** Governance conflicts and exceptions must remain explicit and attributable.
- **P20-ALIGNMENT-005:** Every participating Agent must preserve one shared constitutional interpretation of Phase 20 boundaries.
- **P20-ALIGNMENT-006:** Agent specialization or implementation must not weaken constitutional consistency.
- **P20-ALIGNMENT-007:** Governance alignment must not create enforcement, communication, workflow, Runtime, or orchestration behavior.
- **P20-ALIGNMENT-008:** Director authority must remain final and must never be inferred from Governance alignment.

## Enterprise Example

Multiple domains may use an admitted Memory Item only while preserving its privacy restriction and review requirement. Their consistent treatment demonstrates Governance alignment; it does not grant access, produce a Governance Outcome, or approve a decision.
