# Hebun Academy — Hebun Product Vision

## Document Status

**DOCUMENT TYPE: PRODUCT VISION**

**STATUS: RECORDED — CONCEPTS ONLY**

This is a Product Vision document. It is not Architecture, not Runtime, not Roadmap, and not Implementation. It records long-term product concepts only. It defines no technology, no model, no API, no database, no code, and no runtime behavior. It names no LLM, no agent, no planner, no tool-calling, no workflow, and no reflection mechanism.

It answers exactly one question: **why does Hebun Academy exist?**

Nothing here opens work, authorizes implementation, or transfers authority. Human authority remains supreme and Director authority remains final over any future capability.

---

## Vision

Hebun Academy is envisioned as the **official education platform of the Hebun AI ecosystem**.

It is not Heby Academy. Heby is not the platform — Heby is envisioned as the Academy's personal AI Mentor. The Academy is the place where mastery is built; Heby is the guide who walks each learner there.

The vision is a single learning environment that carries a person from first contact to genuine expertise — in the Hebun platform itself, and in the professional technologies the enterprise depends on.

---

## Philosophy

The philosophy is **mastery, not course sales**.

The measure of success is not lessons completed or certificates issued — it is a learner who can operate at a real expert level. Content, exams, and labs exist to serve that outcome, never to replace it. A learner who passed but cannot perform is a failure of the Academy, however good the material looked.

## Heby Mentor

Heby is envisioned as far more than a chatbot that answers questions.

Within the Academy, Heby is the learner's **personal AI mentor**. Its envisioned responsibilities:

- measure the learner's current level
- build a personal learning map
- find gaps in knowledge
- prepare real-world scenarios
- run laboratory exercises
- administer exams
- track progress
- re-teach forgotten topics through spaced repetition
- analyze strengths and weaknesses
- guide the learner until an expert level is reached

Heby never merely produces an answer. Its envisioned purpose is to carry the learner all the way to expertise.
