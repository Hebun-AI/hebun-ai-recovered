It answers exactly one question: **why would Quantum Intelligence exist?**

---

## Vision

Quantum Intelligence is envisioned as a strategic Intelligence Domain to be added to Hebun AI in the future.

Its envisioned purpose: to **understand, observe, and explain to the Director** the operations of organizations that use quantum computing.

It is not a quantum computer. It produces no quantum algorithms. It performs no scientific research. It is an intelligence layer that makes quantum operations legible to enterprise leadership — nothing more.

---

## Philosophy

The philosophy is **observation and explanation, not computation**.

Quantum Intelligence stands beside quantum workloads, not inside them. It does not run experiments; it makes sense of them. It does not decide; it shows the Director what quantum operations cost, consume, and risk — in plain terms a non-specialist can act on.

---

## Envisioned Responsibilities

- monitor quantum infrastructure
- understand quantum workloads
- analyze quantum costs
- track quantum resource usage
- organize quantum experiments
- explain quantum operations
- surface quantum risks
- report quantum capacity
- outline a quantum roadmap
- offer recommendations to the Director

Every responsibility above is observational and advisory. None executes a quantum job, allocates a resource, or takes action on the enterprise's behalf.

---

## Example Platforms

The domain is envisioned to make sense of operations running on platforms such as:

- Amazon Braket
- Azure Quantum
- IBM Quantum
- Google Quantum AI

These are named as examples of the environments Quantum Intelligence would observe — not integrations designed or authorized by this document.

## Advisory Boundary

Quantum Intelligence is envisioned to produce strategic understanding only.

It never decides on the enterprise's behalf. It never starts an automatic action. It always operates under Director approval. Understanding flows to the Director; judgment and action remain human.
