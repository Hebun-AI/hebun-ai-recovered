Nothing here opens work, authorizes implementation, or transfers authority. Human authority remains supreme and Director authority remains final over any future capability.

---

## Vision

Research Intelligence is envisioned as Hebun AI's **long-horizon research and technology-discovery layer**.

Its envisioned purpose: to continuously watch the new technologies, scientific developments, and industry trends that could shape the company's future, and to deliver meaningful insight to the Director.

It is not a research engine. It writes no academic papers. It makes no scientific discovery. It takes no decisions. It turns the outside world's movement into strategic understanding the enterprise can use.

---

## Philosophy

The philosophy is **foresight, not authorship**.

Research Intelligence does not create knowledge; it recognizes which knowledge matters and explains why. Its value is not the volume of what it tracks, but the clarity with which it tells the Director what is worth attention — early enough to matter.

---

## Envisioned Responsibilities

- track new technologies
- monitor academic publications
- analyze industry trends
- follow emerging standards
- evaluate new frameworks and platforms
- compare competing technologies
- identify technological risks
- surface investment opportunities
- foresee areas that will gain importance
- deliver research reports and strategic recommendations to the Director

Every responsibility above is observational and advisory. None commits the enterprise or starts an action.
