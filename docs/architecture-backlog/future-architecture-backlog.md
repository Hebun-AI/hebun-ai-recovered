# Future Architecture Backlog

## Governance Status

The Future Architecture Backlog is a Director-managed register of high-value architectural initiatives approved for investigation but not authorized for architecture delivery, implementation, Runtime realization, Agent creation, workflow design, roadmap placement, or execution.

This document is not part of the canonical roadmap. Its initiatives have no phase numbers, delivery commitments, implementation priority, or implied promotion. The Hebun AI repository remains the canonical source of truth, and completed architecture remains unchanged.

An initiative becomes eligible for roadmap consideration only through an explicit Director decision after its dependencies, evidence, boundaries, strategic value, compatibility, and readiness have been reviewed. Investigation approval is not architecture approval, implementation approval, or execution authorization.

## Initiative — Innovation Intelligence

**Readiness Status:** Future Investigation

### Vision

Enable Hebun to continuously identify credible digital product opportunities from global market signals and present evidence-grounded opportunity proposals to the Director.

The intended outcome is a governed portfolio of validated opportunity candidates—not autonomous product selection, startup creation, investment approval, MVP execution, or market action.

### Why It Exists

Enterprises usually discover opportunities through fragmented research, individual intuition, episodic workshops, and disconnected trend reports. Signals appear across markets faster than organizations can consistently interpret them, while novelty is often mistaken for value.

Innovation Intelligence exists to make opportunity discovery systematic, evidence-aware, comparable, and Director-governed.

### Problem Statement

Hebun currently has canonical architecture intelligence for understanding its own enterprise architecture, but it has no approved capability for continuously evaluating external market change as a source of new digital product opportunities.

Without a bounded future capability:

- weak signals remain disconnected;
- trends are confused with durable demand;
- opportunities lack traceable validation;
- product proposals cannot be compared consistently;
- startup and MVP ideas become informal commitments;
- the Director receives enthusiasm rather than decision-quality evidence.

### Strategic Value

- increases the discoverability of defensible product opportunities;
- reduces dependence on intuition-only innovation;
- connects trends to validated enterprise problems and market evidence;
- creates comparable opportunity and innovation assessments;
- improves the quality of Director portfolio review;
- preserves uncertainty, provenance, alternatives, and rejection evidence;
- keeps product proposals separate from approval and execution.

### Core Capability Areas

- market signal collection;
- trend analysis;
- opportunity discovery;
- opportunity validation;
- product proposal generation;
- startup backlog;
- MVP proposal;
- innovation scoring.

These are investigation subjects, not approved components, stages, services, Agents, workflows, algorithms, data pipelines, or Runtime behavior.

### Enterprise Use Cases

- identify emerging demand patterns that may support a new B2B digital product;
- compare a market trend with regulatory, technology, customer, and competitive evidence;
- distinguish a temporary signal spike from a persistent opportunity;
- assemble a traceable product opportunity proposal with alternatives and uncertainty;
- maintain an uncommitted startup-opportunity backlog for Director review;
- frame an MVP proposal without authorizing its design, funding, development, or launch;
- score opportunities for comparability while preserving the rule that scoring is not truth or approval.

### Expected Outcome

Present validated digital product opportunities to the Director for review, with evidence, provenance, assumptions, uncertainty, risks, alternatives, validation status, and explicit non-approval status.

### Relationship to Existing Intelligence Architecture

Architecture Intelligence analyzes Hebun's canonical architecture while preserving architectural authority. Innovation Intelligence would investigate external market signals to discover possible future products.

The proposed initiative therefore differs by subject and outcome:

- Architecture Intelligence explains and validates canonical architecture.
- Innovation Intelligence would form non-canonical opportunity candidates from external and enterprise market evidence.
- A candidate opportunity would not become canonical truth, strategy, roadmap scope, recommendation, approval, or execution authorization.

The existing [Strategic Research Intelligence](01-strategic-research-intelligence.md) backlog item may become an upstream research capability if promoted. It gathers and synthesizes research; Innovation Intelligence would evaluate that governed evidence specifically for product-opportunity discovery. Neither capability is authorized by this document.

### Dependencies

- canonical evidence, authority, provenance, uncertainty, and confidence contracts;
- governed external-source qualification and source lifecycle;
- Architecture Ingestion and Knowledge Processing boundaries where applicable;
- Query, Reasoning, Governance, and Director Decision boundaries;
- organizational strategy and Capability context;
- privacy, legal, regulatory, information-classification, and Tenant constraints;
- explicit separation from [Strategic Research Intelligence](01-strategic-research-intelligence.md);
- explicit Director criteria for investigation and future roadmap eligibility.

### Risks

- trend popularity may be mistaken for market demand;
- correlation may be presented as causation;
- biased or manipulated external signals may distort opportunity discovery;
- innovation scoring may be treated as truth or investment priority;
- product proposals may be mistaken for recommendations or approval;
- startup and MVP backlogs may silently become delivery commitments;
- confidential market or customer information may cross Tenant boundaries;
- continuous investigation may be mistaken for autonomous Runtime operation;
- overlap with Strategic Research Intelligence may duplicate responsibility.

### Why It Is Excluded from the Current Roadmap

The current canonical roadmap is completing foundational Intelligence, Multi-Agent, Runtime, and Enterprise AI Operating System responsibilities in a protected order. Innovation Intelligence is an optional future enterprise capability, not a prerequisite for the currently approved sequence.

Its sources, validation standards, ownership, economic criteria, privacy boundaries, market-evidence authority, and relationship to existing research capabilities are not yet sufficiently governed for canonical placement.

### Future Placement

No phase or roadmap position is assigned.

The initiative may become eligible for roadmap consideration only after:

- the applicable canonical platform layers are complete and approved;
- external market evidence can be admitted with trustworthy provenance;
- its boundary from Strategic Research Intelligence is explicit;
- product, startup, MVP, and scoring semantics are demonstrably non-authoritative;
- privacy, legal, Tenant, and market-manipulation risks have governed controls;
- a bounded architectural scope and compatibility assessment exist;
- the Director explicitly opens a roadmap consideration gate.

### Director Notes

- Investigation may compare opportunity-discovery methods and enterprise value assumptions.
- Investigation must not create a product roadmap, startup commitment, MVP execution plan, Agent, workflow, or Runtime service.
- The Director retains strategy, portfolio selection, investment, roadmap placement, approval, and execution authorization.
- Rejection, deferral, and “insufficient evidence” are valid investigation outcomes.

## Initiative — Problem Intelligence

**Readiness Status:** Future Investigation

### Vision

Enable Hebun to discover persistent, unresolved business problems across industries and present evidence-grounded problem–solution opportunity pairs for Director consideration.

The intended outcome is a governed set of validated business-problem candidates with proposed AI-native product hypotheses—not autonomous consulting, solution selection, customer targeting, product approval, or execution.

### Why It Exists

Many product initiatives begin with available technology rather than a validated enterprise problem. Pain points are often anecdotal, duplicated across sources, poorly segmented, or disconnected from willingness to adopt and pay.

Problem Intelligence exists to keep discovery problem-first, evidence-based, cross-source validated, industry-aware, and bounded by Director authority.

### Problem Statement

Hebun has no approved capability for continuously distinguishing meaningful cross-industry business problems from isolated complaints, temporary friction, vendor narratives, or unsupported market assumptions.

Without a bounded future capability:

- recurring pain points remain hidden across fragmented sources;
- the same symptom may be counted as several problems;
- industry context and organizational constraints are lost;
- solution hypotheses are generated before problem validation;
- customer demand is assumed rather than evidenced;
- proposed AI products lack traceability to a material problem.

### Strategic Value

- improves problem–solution fit before product investment;
- reveals recurring enterprise needs across industries;
- supports comparable pain-point and opportunity assessment;
- makes customer-demand assumptions explicit and testable;
- reduces technology-first solution bias;
- connects validated problems to bounded AI-native product hypotheses;
- improves Director review with traceable evidence and uncertainty.

### Core Capability Areas

- problem discovery;
- cross-source validation;
- industry clustering;
- pain-point analysis;
- solution hypothesis;
- AI product proposal;
- enterprise opportunity scoring;
- customer demand analysis.

These are investigation subjects, not approved components, stages, services, Agents, workflows, algorithms, data pipelines, or Runtime behavior.

### Enterprise Use Cases

- identify a recurring operational problem across several industries without merging distinct causes;
- validate a reported pain point against independent customer, market, regulatory, and organizational sources;
- cluster related problems while preserving industry and jurisdiction differences;
- distinguish a root problem from its symptoms and proposed vendor solutions;
- form a bounded AI-native solution hypothesis with explicit assumptions and failure conditions;
- assess whether customer behavior supports demand rather than relying only on stated interest;
- present a problem, affected stakeholders, evidence, solution hypothesis, alternatives, and opportunity score to the Director.

### Expected Outcome

Present validated business problems together with proposed AI-native product hypotheses, evidence, provenance, affected enterprise contexts, customer-demand signals, assumptions, uncertainty, risks, alternatives, and explicit non-approval status.

### Relationship to Existing Intelligence Architecture

Existing Architecture Intelligence understands and validates canonical Hebun architecture. Problem Intelligence would investigate external and enterprise problem evidence to form non-canonical opportunity hypotheses.

It differs from current Intelligence architecture because it:

- studies unresolved business problems rather than canonical architecture;
- treats source material as evidence, not architectural truth;
- produces hypotheses and opportunity candidates, not decisions;
- does not alter Reasoning Output, Governance Outcome, Decision Support, or Director authority;
- cannot authorize a product, customer engagement, roadmap change, or execution.

The existing [Strategic Research Intelligence](01-strategic-research-intelligence.md) backlog item may supply governed research if promoted. Problem Intelligence would apply problem identity, cross-source validation, clustering, pain-point, demand, and solution-hypothesis semantics to that evidence.

### Dependencies

- canonical evidence, provenance, identity, uncertainty, and conflict contracts;
- governed source qualification and cross-source validation;
- stable problem identity and duplicate-prevention principles;
- industry, jurisdiction, organization, stakeholder, and Tenant context;
- Query, Reasoning, Governance, and Director Decision boundaries;
- privacy, legal, regulatory, classification, and research-ethics constraints;
- separation from Strategic Research Intelligence and Innovation Intelligence;
- explicit Director criteria for problem validation and future roadmap eligibility.

### Risks

- symptoms may be mistaken for root problems;
- repeated claims may be mistaken for independent validation;
- industry clustering may erase material contextual differences;
- customer statements may be mistaken for demonstrated demand;
- solution hypotheses may bias or rewrite problem definition;
- opportunity scoring may obscure uncertainty and minority evidence;
- external or customer data may violate privacy, classification, or Tenant boundaries;
- AI product proposals may be mistaken for recommendations, commitments, or authorization;
- continuous discovery may be mistaken for autonomous Runtime activity.

### Why It Is Excluded from the Current Roadmap

Problem Intelligence is not required to complete the protected canonical Phase 1–30 sequence. It is a possible future enterprise opportunity capability whose source authority, problem identity, validation criteria, industry semantics, customer-demand evidence, privacy limits, and solution-hypothesis boundaries require investigation before canonical placement.

Adding it now would risk introducing external-market concerns before the applicable platform, governance, Multi-Agent, Runtime, and enterprise control foundations are approved.

### Future Placement

No phase or roadmap position is assigned.

The initiative may become eligible for roadmap consideration only after:

- applicable canonical platform foundations are complete and approved;
- problem identity and cross-source validation can be governed;
- customer-demand evidence standards are explicit;
- industry, jurisdiction, privacy, classification, and Tenant boundaries are defined;
- the relationship to Strategic Research Intelligence and Innovation Intelligence is non-duplicative;
- solution hypotheses and scores remain separate from recommendation, approval, and execution;
- a bounded architectural scope and compatibility assessment exist;
- the Director explicitly opens a roadmap consideration gate.

### Director Notes

- Investigation must remain problem-first and preserve counter-evidence.
- A plausible AI solution must never validate its own problem statement.
- Investigation must not create domain Agents, customer workflows, sales activity, product commitments, implementation, or Runtime services.
- The Director retains product strategy, customer targeting, roadmap placement, approval, investment, and execution authorization.
- “No validated problem” and “insufficient demand evidence” are valid outcomes.

## Initiative — Device Runtime & Computer Use

**Readiness Status:** Future Investigation

### Vision

Enable Hebun to interact with and, where explicitly authorized, act upon the user's or company's own devices — desktops, laptops, browsers, applications, files, and terminals — through a governed execution substrate, so that a human intent expressed to Heby can eventually resolve into a permitted, bounded, audited action on a specific authorized device.

The intended outcome is a **governed device-execution capability** — not autonomous machine control, not silent background observation, and not Heby acquiring direct operating-system authority.

### Why It Exists

Computer Use has a published constitutional home in [Program VII — Computer Use](../architecture/programs/program-07-computer-use/constitution.md) (Phases 40–43, `PLANNED — NOT OPEN`), which governs the *constitutional meaning* of computer interaction, governed execution, automation, and integration. Program VII deliberately defines no device substrate: it names no Device Agent, no machine-local execution boundary, no multi-device resolution, and no operating-system permission model.

This initiative exists to investigate that missing **device substrate** — the layer between the governed-execution meaning Program VII owns and the actual authorized operating-system capability on a real machine — without duplicating, reopening, or redefining Program VII.

### Problem Statement

Hebun has no approved capability describing how a governed computer-use decision reaches a *specific authorized device* and returns *execution evidence*, while preserving the operating system's own permission model and the separation of Heby from execution authority.

Without a bounded future capability:

- there is no defined machine-local boundary that discovers, enforces, and sandboxes device capabilities;
- an intent expressed to Heby cannot be resolved to a target device, capability, permission, and execution session;
- observation permission risks being conflated with action permission;
- sensitive operating-system capabilities (camera, microphone, terminal, credentials) risk being implied by ordinary computer-use permission;
- continuous observation risks being treated as a default property rather than a separately governed, time-bounded grant;
- consequential device actions lack a defined attribution, evidence, and revocation model.

### Strategic Value

- gives governed Computer Use a concrete, least-privilege path to a real authorized device;
- keeps the operating-system permission model authoritative rather than bypassed;
- preserves the separations Program VII already protects (Capability ≠ Permission, Interaction ≠ Authority, Execution ≠ Runtime authority, Automation ≠ Autonomy);
- keeps Heby as the intent and explanation surface, never the execution authority;
- makes device actions attributable, revocable, and auditable by design;
- treats sensitive capabilities as explicit, separately granted, and OS-bounded.

### Core Capability Areas

- Device Agent (machine-local execution boundary);
- Device Runtime (governed cloud-side execution substrate);
- device identity, trust, and attestation;
- device capability discovery and registry;
- browser, application, file-system, terminal, and screen/window adapters;
- local tool invocation;
- permission gate and human approval gate;
- execution policy and risk classification (reused, not redefined);
- execution session isolation;
- execution evidence and audit trail;
- action reversibility where possible;
- kill switch / emergency stop;
- sensitive-capability controls (camera, microphone, continuous capture, credentials, privileged access).

These are investigation subjects, not approved components, stages, services, Agents, workflows, adapters, protocols, daemons, or Runtime behavior.

### Observe vs Act

Observation and action are distinct authorities. Observation permission must never imply action permission.

- **Observe** (read-only, explicitly scoped): inspect an authorized screen/window, browser state, application state, or authorized file; capture an explicitly permitted screenshot.
- **Act** (consequential): open an application, navigate a browser, interact with an application, modify an authorized file, execute an authorized terminal command, trigger an authorized local tool.

Each direction is granted separately; a read grant confers no write or execute authority.

### Sensitive Capabilities

The following are classified separately and must never be implied by ordinary Computer Use permission. Each requires an explicit capability grant and rides on the operating system's own permission boundary:

- camera;
- microphone;
- continuous screen capture;
- continuous audio capture;
- broad file-system access;
- credentials and secrets;
- clipboard;
- accessibility APIs;
- terminal / shell;
- administrator / root actions.

Example: a human asks Heby to "open the camera." Heby interprets the intent; the Device Runtime checks device, capability, policy, and authority; the local Device Agent requests or uses an *explicitly granted* operating-system camera capability; the result returns with execution evidence. Heby does not bypass the operating-system permission model.

### No Passive Surveillance by Default

Hebun must not be architected to continuously watch everything a user does. The architecture must not define default keylogging, permanent screen recording, permanent microphone or camera monitoring, unrestricted file-system surveillance, credential capture, or hidden background observation.

It must prefer explicit scope, least privilege, time-bounded grants, user-visible execution, auditability, and revocation. Any future enterprise-monitoring requirement must be a separately governed capability — never an implicit property of Heby or of ordinary Computer Use.

### Device Agent

A future machine-local component — the **Hebun Device Agent** — would hold the execution boundary on the device itself. Investigation subjects include: device identity; capability discovery; local permission enforcement; an execution sandbox/boundary; browser, application, file-system, terminal, and screen/window adapters; execution evidence; health/heartbeat; revocation; and a local emergency stop.

The cloud-side runtime must never assume a capability exists merely because a device is connected. Capabilities must be explicitly discovered and granted.

### Multi-Device Model

Hebun should eventually support multiple authorized devices per organization and user:

```
Organization → Users / Roles → Authorized Devices → Device Capabilities → Permission Grants → Execution Sessions
```

An intent such as "Open Safari on my MacBook" must eventually resolve identity → organization → target device → device capability → permission → execution session. This initiative records the requirement only; it authorizes no resolution logic.

### Authority & Permission Model

Action classes follow the existing governance direction — **risk → policy → permission → authority → execution → evidence → audit** — and reuse the published Enterprise Security (Program V) and Governance risk/approval vocabulary rather than introducing a parallel taxonomy. Conceptually: low-risk (open an approved application, navigate an approved page, read explicitly shared context); medium-risk (edit an authorized document, invoke an approved workflow, run a constrained local action); high-risk (terminal execution, destructive file operations, external communication, security-sensitive configuration); sensitive (camera, microphone, credentials, privileged OS access, continuous observation). Permission originates only from Security and applicable Governance, never from technical possibility.

### Audit & Evidence

Every consequential device action should eventually be attributable. A conceptual execution record captures: who requested it; which agent/system prepared it; target organization; target device; requested capability; policy decision; approval/authority where applicable; execution timestamp; execution result; evidence/result reference; failure or rejection reason; and a correlation/session identifier. This initiative documents the requirement; it invents no schema and defers to any existing schema authority when promoted.

### Failure Model

Future execution must fail closed when: the device is unavailable; the capability is unavailable; permission is missing; policy blocks the action; approval is required; identity is unresolved; the target is ambiguous; the execution session is invalid; device trust is insufficient; the operating system rejects permission; or required evidence cannot be produced. Heby explains the failure; it never bypasses it.

### Relationship to Existing Computer Use Architecture

This initiative does **not** duplicate or reopen Program VII. Program VII remains the canonical constitutional owner of Computer Use meaning. If promoted through an explicit Director gate, the device substrate described here would be delivered **through** the existing Program VII phases — Computer Interaction (Phase 40) and Governed Computer Execution (Phase 41) in particular — together with Program V Security and applicable Governance, **not** as a new Program, new phase numbers, or a competing architecture.

It also relates to the published `tool-execution` and execution-runtime architecture, which it consumes without redefining. The [Tool Registry](12-tool-registry.md), [Policy Engine](13-policy-engine.md), and [Permission Engine](14-permission-engine.md) backlog items may become upstream capabilities if promoted.

### Architectural Ownership

Ownership maps to the existing **seven-workspace** Information Architecture; this initiative creates **no eighth workspace** and lands inside existing workspaces as future surfaces:

- **Operations** (primary) — Device Runtime is a live execution substrate; execution sessions, device connections, and running device actions belong here.
- **Workforce** — Agents may *request* bounded device capabilities; they receive a capability, never a machine.
- **Governance** — device trust, permissions, policy, human approval, sensitive-capability controls, and audit.
- **Platform** — device registration, runtime configuration, adapters, and integration administration.
- **Heby** — natural-language intent, action preparation, explanation, and execution-result presentation. Heby may request or prepare an action; it must not silently acquire device authority.
- **Command** — high-risk escalations and the Director authority boundary where required.

### Strategic-Domain Mapping

The capability connects all three of Hebun's strategic domains:

1. **Enterprise Multi-Agent Orchestration** — agents use governed device capabilities, bounded per capability.
2. **Reasoning & Computer Use** — the Device Runtime becomes the execution substrate beneath reasoning-driven computer use.
3. **ChatGPT-native / Heby Applications** — Heby is the natural-language interaction and explanation surface over device actions, without holding execution authority.

### Dependencies

- published [Program VII — Computer Use](../architecture/programs/program-07-computer-use/constitution.md) constitution and its Phases 40–43;
- Program V Enterprise Security permission and authority architecture;
- Governance policy, approval, risk-classification, and audit boundaries;
- canonical evidence, provenance, identity, uncertainty, and Tenant-isolation contracts;
- published `tool-execution` and execution-runtime architecture;
- operating-system permission models as an external, authoritative boundary;
- explicit Director criteria for investigation and future roadmap eligibility.

### Risks

- capability may be mistaken for permission, and connection for authority;
- observation permission may be silently widened into action permission;
- sensitive capabilities may be implied by ordinary Computer Use permission;
- continuous observation may drift toward a default rather than a bounded grant;
- Heby may be mistaken for an execution runtime rather than an intent surface;
- multi-device resolution may act on an ambiguous or wrong target;
- device actions may lack attribution or reversible evidence;
- cross-Tenant device access may violate isolation;
- an operating-system permission bypass may be attempted for convenience.

### Why It Is Excluded from the Current Roadmap

The canonical roadmap is completing foundational Intelligence, Multi-Agent, Runtime, and Enterprise AI Operating System responsibilities in a protected order, and Program VII itself is `PLANNED — NOT OPEN`. A device substrate cannot be placed before Program VII opens and before the applicable Security, Governance, and Runtime foundations are approved. Its device-identity, trust, sensitive-capability, and operating-system-boundary semantics require governed investigation before canonical placement.

### Future Placement

No phase or roadmap position is assigned.

The initiative may become eligible for roadmap consideration only after:

- Program VII — Computer Use is opened through an explicit Director gate;
- the applicable Security, Governance, and Runtime foundations are complete and approved;
- observe-vs-act, sensitive-capability, and no-default-surveillance boundaries are demonstrably enforced by architecture;
- device identity, trust, and multi-device resolution can be governed;
- a bounded architectural scope and Program VII compatibility assessment exist;
- the Director explicitly opens a roadmap consideration gate.

### Director Notes

- Investigation may compare device-agent boundaries, permission models, and evidence approaches.
- Investigation must not create operating-system code, a device daemon, adapters, a device bridge, execution behavior, an Agent tool, or Runtime services.
- Heby must remain non-executing; execution authority belongs to the governed execution/runtime layer under Security and Governance.
- The Director retains authority over device authorization, sensitive-capability grants, roadmap placement, approval, and execution authorization.
- Refusal, deferral, and "insufficient authority" are valid investigation and execution outcomes.

## Initiative — Creator Business Engine

**Readiness Status:** Future Investigation

### Vision

Enable Hebun to connect an organization's own content channel, understand it from provider-confirmed evidence, and reason from real audience and performance data — so that content strategy, production support, publishing preparation, and monetization analysis eventually rest on measured channel truth rather than on assumption.

The intended outcome is a **governed channel-evidence and closed-loop measurement substrate** — not autonomous publishing, not automated content creation at scale, not a monetization authority, and not Hebun operating a creator business on the owner's behalf.

### Why It Exists

Creator-facing product intent already has a published architectural home. [Personal Enterprise Mode](../architecture/capabilities/personal-enterprise-mode/README.md) names *YouTube channel creation planning*, *content strategy*, *publishing strategy*, *digital product creation*, *business monitoring*, *revenue optimization*, and *portfolio management* as core capabilities, and its [Director Gates](../architecture/capabilities/personal-enterprise-mode/03-director-gates.md) already place publishing and spending on the gated side. Its [Revenue Engine](../architecture/capabilities/personal-enterprise-mode/04-revenue-engine.md) already defines continuous opportunity search as a product behavior.

What Personal Enterprise Mode deliberately does not define is the layer beneath it: **where the channel truth comes from.** It is a product capability composed on graph, memory, reasoning, and orchestration; it names no provider, no channel connection, no analytics admission, no performance evidence contract, and no attribution boundary. Its plans are prepared from the organization's own remembered context, not from a connected channel's measured reality.

This initiative exists to investigate that missing **channel-evidence substrate** — the layer between a real content platform and the product capability that reasons about it — without duplicating, reopening, or redefining Personal Enterprise Mode.

### Problem Statement

Hebun has no approved capability describing how a real content channel becomes governed, attributable, tenant-isolated evidence, nor how a published piece of content is connected back to the audience response and commercial outcome it produced.

Without a bounded future capability:

- content strategy is advice without measurement, and cannot improve from results;
- channel access, content access, analytics access, publishing authority, and monetization authority collapse into a single undifferentiated "connect the account";
- provider-supplied engagement numbers may be treated as demand, and correlation as causation;
- an affiliate commission may be mistaken for evidence that a product is suitable for an audience;
- generated or assisted media enters the world without recorded provenance;
- a prepared publication and an executed publication become indistinguishable;
- there is no closed loop, so the same content strategy is proposed indefinitely regardless of outcome.

### Strategic Value

- grounds an already-designed product capability in provider-confirmed evidence instead of assumption;
- makes the five distinct authorities visible and separately grantable;
- allows content strategy to be judged by measured outcome rather than by plausibility;
- connects attention to intent to commercial result while preserving the uncertainty in each link;
- gives affiliate and offer reasoning an evidence base other than commission rate;
- keeps preparation abundant and commitment gated, consistent with the existing Director Gates;
- establishes a reusable pattern for any future content or social platform, so the concept is not permanently coupled to one provider.

### Core Capability Areas

- channel connection and organization binding;
- channel intelligence — identity, configuration, and standing characteristics;
- audience intelligence — composition and engagement signals as evidence, never as persons;
- topic and market research over channel-adjacent signals;
- content strategy grounded in measured performance;
- content planning and production assistance;
- publishing preparation;
- performance analytics admission;
- monetization analysis;
- affiliate offer reasoning;
- revenue attribution with explicit uncertainty;
- closed-loop strategy optimization.

These are investigation subjects, not approved components, stages, services, Agents, workflows, algorithms, data pipelines, provider integrations, or Runtime behavior.

### The Five Authorities

The single most important boundary in this initiative is that the following are **five different authorities**, separately requested, separately granted, separately revocable, and never implied by one another:

```
CHANNEL ACCESS        the organization's channel is identified and bound to a Tenant
CONTENT ACCESS        the channel's content inventory and its metadata may be read
ANALYTICS ACCESS      performance and audience evidence may be read
PUBLISHING AUTHORITY  something may be released to the outside world
MONETIZATION AUTHORITY a commercial relationship or offer may be attached to attention
```

Connecting a channel must never grant the right to read its analytics. Reading analytics must never grant the right to publish. Publishing authority must never grant the right to monetize. Each step outward is a separate grant, and the existing platform distinction between a *capability* and an *authorization* applies unchanged: a read capability never mints a permit.

### Enterprise Use Cases

- bind an organization's own content channel and report what is genuinely readable and what is not;
- distinguish a channel whose analytics are connected from one whose analytics are merely requested;
- assemble a content opportunity proposal from measured audience evidence with alternatives and uncertainty;
- prepare a title, description, or thumbnail set to the point of decision without releasing anything;
- present a monetization analysis that names commission economics *and* audience relevance, brand safety, refund risk, platform rules, and disclosure obligations as separate considerations;
- attribute a commercial outcome to content while preserving competing explanations;
- retire a content strategy that measurement shows is not working.

### Expected Outcome

Present provider-confirmed channel, audience, and performance evidence — together with content and monetization proposals built on it — to the Director for review, with provenance, assumptions, uncertainty, risks, alternatives, and explicit non-approval status.

### Relationship to Existing Architecture

This initiative does **not** duplicate or reopen Personal Enterprise Mode. That capability remains the canonical owner of creator-facing *product meaning* — what the mode is for the owner, and where its Director Gates fall. If promoted through an explicit Director gate, the channel-evidence substrate described here would be delivered **beneath** it, as provider truth and measurement that Personal Enterprise Mode consumes, **not** as a competing product capability, a second Revenue Engine, or a new set of gates.

It relates to existing intelligence authorities without absorbing them:

- **[Marketing Intelligence](../architecture/enterprise-domain-intelligence/07-marketing-intelligence.md)** already owns audience evidence, channel and content performance, campaign outcomes, and attribution uncertainty — with the invariants `Audience segment ≠ Person`, `Attribution ≠ Causation`, `Engagement ≠ Demand`, and `Marketing intelligence ≠ Campaign authority`. This initiative supplies it evidence; it does not become a second interpreter of that evidence.
- **[Finance Intelligence](../architecture/enterprise-domain-intelligence/04-finance-intelligence.md)** already owns revenue, cost, and unit economics as *non-authoritative* intelligence over canonical financial systems. Monetization analysis is a contribution to it, never a replacement for it.
- **[Innovation Intelligence](#initiative--innovation-intelligence)** in this register would evaluate market signals for product opportunity. Channel evidence may become one of its inputs; the two remain separate by subject.
- The published **Provider Onboarding Standard** governs every provider this initiative would ever touch. No content platform is exempt from it.

### Architectural Ownership

Ownership maps to the existing **seven-workspace** Information Architecture; this initiative creates **no eighth workspace** and lands inside existing workspaces as future surfaces:

- **Intelligence** (primary) — channel, audience, content-performance, and monetization intelligence is advisory output about where the organization stands and where it could go.
- **Platform** — the channel provider connection, its granted permissions, and its health.
- **Operations** — a prepared publication that has been authorized becomes running work, and is monitored there.
- **Governance** — publishing permits, monetization permits, disclosure obligations, brand-safety policy, and the audit record.
- **Command** — the Director authority boundary: the approval act for publishing and for monetization.
- **Heby** — natural-language interaction, explanation, and presentation of channel evidence. Heby may prepare a publication; it must not acquire publishing authority.
- **Knowledge** — only where provider-derived material is *separately admitted* through an admission authority. Provider-derived is not Knowledge by default.

### Dependencies

- the released provider foundation: provider catalog, integration authority, capability availability, credential authority, and the Provider Onboarding Standard;
- an approved content-platform provider connection and its granted-permission truth;
- canonical evidence, provenance, uncertainty, confidence, and Tenant-isolation contracts;
- Governance policy, approval, risk-classification, and audit boundaries;
- Marketing, Finance, Sales, and Customer Success domain-intelligence boundaries;
- a Knowledge admission authority, if provider-derived material is ever to become Knowledge;
- generated-media provenance and disclosure contracts;
- [Personal Enterprise Mode](../architecture/capabilities/personal-enterprise-mode/README.md) as the consuming product capability;
- explicit Director criteria for investigation and future roadmap eligibility.

### Risks

- channel connection may be mistaken for publishing authority, and analytics access for either;
- provider engagement metrics may be treated as demand or as customer intent;
- attribution error may be presented as commercial fact;

## Initiative — Growth & Commerce Engine

**Readiness Status:** Future Investigation

### Vision

Enable Hebun to reason about revenue opportunities across paid and owned commerce channels — discovering an offer, proposing a bounded experiment, measuring what it actually cost and earned, and improving from the result — so that growth decisions rest on contribution economics rather than on channel-reported success metrics.

The intended outcome is a **governed experiment-economics capability** — not autonomous advertising spend, not a media-buying service, not a financial authority, and not Hebun running a commerce operation.

### Why It Exists

Paid acquisition is the first place in Hebun's product direction where a proposal becomes *money leaving the organization*. Every existing authority is careful about this and none of them owns it: [Marketing Intelligence](../architecture/enterprise-domain-intelligence/07-marketing-intelligence.md) explicitly cannot "launch, stop, target, personalize, publish, spend, alter brand policy, select a campaign, or authorize customer communication," and lists media buying and budget use among its non-responsibilities. [Finance Intelligence](../architecture/enterprise-domain-intelligence/04-finance-intelligence.md) explicitly cannot "approve budgets, allocate capital, price products, move funds, create accounting entries, authorize spend, or determine financial policy."

Those refusals are correct and must stay. What is missing is the bounded, governed capability that connects a *prepared* commercial experiment to an *authorized* one, and then honestly reports what it cost.

This initiative also exists because a channel-reported success metric and a profitable outcome are not the same thing, and the difference is where most commerce operations lose money.

### Problem Statement

Hebun has no approved capability describing how a commercial experiment is proposed, bounded, authorized, measured against its true cost, and either continued or stopped.

Without a bounded future capability:

- connecting an advertising provider may be read as permission to spend;
- a campaign that reports strong ROAS may be destroying contribution margin, and nothing would notice;
- affiliate economics may be modelled as `traffic × commission`, which omits every cost that decides the outcome;
- an experiment may have no expiry, no ceiling, and no stop-loss;
- scaling may occur without a second authority act;
- attribution error may be laundered into financial reporting;
- derived commercial intelligence may be confused with authoritative accounting truth;
- an optimization loop may become a runaway.

### Strategic Value

- makes spending authority explicit, bounded, and separately granted;
- replaces channel-reported success with contribution economics as the decision basis;
- gives affiliate commerce an honest economic model rather than a flattering one;
- allows low-capital validation of demand before an organization commits to building a product;
- preserves attribution uncertainty instead of resolving it in the proposal's favour;
- keeps derived commercial intelligence separate from authoritative financial truth;
- establishes a reusable permit shape for any future consequential-spend capability.

### Core Capability Areas

- market intelligence and opportunity discovery;
- product and offer intelligence;
- offer selection;
- affiliate versus owned-commerce model reasoning;
- creative strategy and creative production assistance;
- landing destination and store reasoning;
- paid acquisition preparation;
- conversion evidence admission;
- attribution with explicit uncertainty;
- unit economics and contribution analysis;
- campaign optimization;
- governed scaling.

These are investigation subjects, not approved components, stages, services, Agents, workflows, algorithms, data pipelines, provider integrations, or Runtime behavior.

### The Economic Model

Affiliate and paid commerce must **not** be modelled as `traffic × commission = profit`. Investigation should treat the outcome as a contribution calculation in which every subtracted term is a real cost the organization pays:

```
revenue
  − advertising spend
  − marketplace / affiliate fees
  − payment processing fees
  − refunds and chargebacks
  − fulfilment, where the organization fulfils
  − creative production cost
  − operational cost
  = contribution
```

Ratios such as ROAS, CAC, CPA, conversion rate, average order value, commission rate, refund rate, contribution margin, and LTV are **comparison instruments, never verdicts**. A campaign can hold an excellent ROAS and a negative contribution at the same time; the model must be able to say so.

This initiative defines **no financial authority and no financial persistence.** Every figure it would produce is derived commercial intelligence contributed to [Finance Intelligence](../architecture/enterprise-domain-intelligence/04-finance-intelligence.md), which is itself explicitly non-authoritative over canonical financial systems. Accounting truth remains where it already lives.

### Spend Authority

Connecting an advertising provider grants Hebun **no** ability to spend. The released platform distinction holds without amendment:

```
PREPARED   ≠   AUTHORIZED   ≠   EXECUTED   ≠   SUCCESSFUL
```

A future spend would flow through the existing Governance and execution authority as a **bounded permit**, never as a standing capability. Limits a permit might carry — as investigation subjects, not as approved design — include: an approved product or offer, an approved campaign, an approved geography, a maximum daily spend, a maximum total experiment budget, a maximum CPA, a minimum ROAS or contribution threshold, a stop-loss rule, a campaign expiry, and a human approval requirement.

The intended shape:

```
Hebun proposes a campaign.
Governance authorizes a bounded experiment.
Execution spends only inside that permit.
Measurement determines success or failure.
Scaling requires another permit, or an explicitly authorized policy.
```

Exhausting a permit ends the experiment. It does not renew itself, and success does not renew it either.

### Enterprise Use Cases

- assemble an offer proposal with its full contribution model and its uncertainty;
- compare an affiliate model against an owned-commerce model for the same demand;
- prepare a bounded acquisition experiment to the point of decision without spending anything;
- report that a campaign with strong reported ROAS is losing contribution margin, and say why;
- stop an experiment that has crossed its stop-loss without waiting for a review cycle;
- present evidence that an affiliate-tested category has earned consideration as an owned product line — as a business decision for a human, never as an action;
- refuse to recommend a product whose commission is attractive and whose audience fit, brand safety, or refund risk is not.

### Expected Outcome

Present bounded, economically honest commercial experiment proposals and their measured outcomes to the Director for review, with provenance, cost assumptions, attribution uncertainty, competing explanations, alternatives, and explicit non-approval status.

### Relationship to Existing Architecture

This initiative does **not** create a marketing authority, a financial authority, or a second revenue interpreter.

- **Marketing Intelligence** keeps campaign evidence, channel and content performance, and attribution uncertainty, with its invariants intact. This initiative supplies it evidence and consumes its qualification; it never overrides `Attribution ≠ Causation`.
- **Finance Intelligence** keeps revenue, cost, and unit economics as non-authoritative intelligence. Contribution figures are contributed there.
- **[Sales Intelligence](../architecture/enterprise-domain-intelligence/06-sales-intelligence.md)** keeps demand and pipeline context, with `Pipeline score ≠ Revenue fact` and `Forecast ≠ Commitment` unchanged.
- **[Executive Intelligence](../architecture/enterprise-domain-intelligence/03-executive-intelligence.md)** already owns enterprise-wide synthesis across domains and already "cannot manufacture consensus." Cross-channel revenue synthesis therefore has an owner; it does not need a new one.
- **[Cross-Domain Dependencies](../architecture/enterprise-domain-intelligence/12-cross-domain-dependencies.md)** already defines how these domains relate without becoming one another. Revenue intelligence is a *composition* under that model, not a new domain.
- **[Cost Intelligence](17-cost-intelligence.md)** in the Architecture Backlog covers the platform's **own** AI spend — model usage, tool cost, department and customer attribution. Advertising spend is a different subject with a different authority; the two must not be merged.
- The published **Provider Onboarding Standard** governs every advertising, commerce, and analytics provider this initiative would ever touch.

### Revenue Intelligence — Ownership Decision

The desired long-term reasoning is:

```
organic content + paid acquisition + affiliate commerce
+ owned commerce + conversion data + cost data
= revenue intelligence
```

**No new authority is created for this.** Each term already has an owner: content and campaign evidence with Marketing, conversion and demand with Sales and Customer Success, cost and unit economics with Finance, and the cross-domain synthesis itself with Executive Intelligence. Revenue Intelligence is therefore a **cross-domain composition**, in the sense `12-cross-domain-dependencies.md` already defines — a declared semantic need for attributable information, not a message, a store, or an execution edge.

Its questions — which channel creates profitable customers, which content creates purchase intent, which creatives work, which campaigns destroy margin despite strong ROAS, where the next unit of budget should go, whether an affiliate-tested category should graduate into an owned business — are all synthesis questions across existing domains. Answering them by inventing a new authority would create a second interpreter of revenue truth, and the two would eventually disagree.

Derived commercial intelligence remains distinct from authoritative accounting truth in both directions: this composition may not become the ledger, and the ledger does not adopt its estimates.

### The Cross-Engine Flywheel

The two initiatives in this register reinforce one another, and the relationship is worth recording because it is the reason both are worth investigating together rather than separately:

```
market signal → content opportunity → content creation → organic audience
→ engagement evidence → affiliate or product test → conversion evidence
→ paid acquisition experiment → contribution economics → validated offer
→ governed scaling → new market intelligence
```

The strategically important pattern is that **affiliate commerce can act as low-capital product validation.** Before an organization builds anything, it can learn whether its audience actually buys a category of third-party product, at what cost, with what refund rate.

If sufficient evidence accumulates, an organization might later choose to negotiate direct partnerships, create a dedicated commerce operation, launch an owned store, develop a private-label product, or build its own brand. **Those remain human business decisions.** Evidence that a category performs is an input to such a decision and never an authorization for one; nothing in this register grants Hebun the ability to take any of those steps.

### Architectural Ownership

Ownership maps to the existing **seven-workspace** Information Architecture; this initiative creates **no eighth workspace** and lands inside existing workspaces as future surfaces:

## Initiative — Autonomous Enterprise Evolution

**Readiness Status:** Future Investigation

### Vision

Investigate how Hebun's already-canonical objective — a governed **Enterprise AI Operating System** — would hold together once humans, AI agents, digital twins, software systems and, much later, physical AI systems all operate inside one organization at the same time.

The subject of this initiative is **the composition**, not the parts. Almost every part named below already has an authoritative record elsewhere in this repository, and this initiative deliberately owns none of them. What has no owner today is the question of how they compose without any one of them quietly becoming an authority.

The organizing principle is a restatement of existing constitutional language, not a new rule:

> **Maximum intelligence, bounded authority.**

Model intelligence is not authority. Agent intelligence is not authority. A digital twin is not authority. A provider capability is not authority. Heby is not authority. The [Enterprise Constitution](../architecture/00-enterprise-constitution.md) §1.1 already says the governed form of this — *"coordinates bounded participants without creating uncontrolled autonomy"* — and this initiative adds emphasis, not doctrine.

### Naming — the repository's word, not a new one

**The canonical term is `Enterprise AI Operating System`.** It is fixed by the [Enterprise Constitution](../architecture/00-enterprise-constitution.md) §1.1, appears as the top layer of the five-layer architectural evolution, and is repeated in [50 — Enterprise Master Roadmap](../architecture/architecture-intelligence/50-enterprise-architecture-roadmap.md). Any phrasing that replaces "AI" with "Autonomous" in that term is a **rename of a constitutional identity** and is refused here. This initiative is named for the *investigation* — how the operating system evolves toward higher autonomy — and it renames nothing.

The word *autonomous* in this title describes a **direction of travel with governed ceilings**, never a grant. `50`'s own Vision already fixes the limit: *"The target is not uncontrolled autonomy."*

### Why It Exists

Hebun's parts are being built and released one authority at a time, and that order is deliberate. The risk is not that a part is missing; it is that the parts are individually correct and collectively wrong — that composition introduces an authority nobody designed.

Three failure shapes motivate the investigation, and none is currently owned by any single record:

- **Authority laundering through composition.** Agent A asks Agent B to do something; B does it; the audit shows a governed act with no human anywhere in the chain. Each step passed its own check.
- **Derived state overtaking authoritative state.** A company twin, a simulation and a prediction are all *derived*. Once a surface renders them beside organizational truth, the distinction is carried only by a label.
- **A coordinator becoming a super-authority.** Heby sits above every workspace. A coordinator that can ask every capability for anything is one design decision away from being the most powerful actor in the system.

### Problem Statement

The repository can currently answer *"may this actor do this act in this tenant?"* It cannot yet answer:

- what an actor **class** is, when humans, agents, twins, external providers and physical devices must each carry distinct identity, authority, provenance, lifecycle and accountability;
- how a bounded delegation between two agents stays bounded when it is three hops long;
- how an organization declares **how much autonomy** a domain has, in a form a runtime could enforce rather than a document could describe;
- how derived, simulated and predicted organizational state stay structurally distinguishable from authoritative organizational truth at every surface that renders them;
- how a temporary, purpose-scoped group of agents is created, bounded, measured and ended without becoming a permanent shadow organization.

### Core Capability Areas — only what the table does not already own

Five areas remain without an owner. They are the actual scope of this investigation:

1. **Actor-class model.** A single account of identity, authority, capability, provenance, lifecycle, security and accountability that distinguishes humans, AI agents, digital twins, software systems, external providers and physical devices — without flattening them into one participant type.

4. **Enterprise autonomy levels.** A declared, per-domain autonomy ceiling. See below.
5. **Derived-state separation at scale.** How company-twin state, simulation output and prediction stay structurally distinguishable from authoritative organizational truth — extending the released per-source `authoritative` boolean rather than inventing a parallel scheme.

### Enterprise autonomy levels — a concept to investigate, not a contract

A conceptual ladder for declaring how much autonomy a domain has:

```
Level 0  Observe            gather information only
Level 1  Recommend          analyse and advise
Level 2  Prepare            produce plans and artifacts for a decision
Level 3  Governed Execution bounded execution under an explicit Governance policy
Level 4  High Autonomy      broad autonomy inside one domain, with monitoring and rollback
Level 5  Human Reserved     specific consequential decisions reachable only by human authority
```

**This ladder is not a production contract and must not be treated as one.** No level maps to a released mechanism today, `Level 5` in particular is a *restriction* rather than a capability, and the numbering must never be read as a maturity score to climb. Whether the ladder survives contact with the released Governance model — permits, mandates, the deployment-global kill switch — is itself part of the investigation, and the honest outcome may be that it does not.

### Actor classes and the invariants they must preserve

Physical execution deserves the sharpest statement, because it is the one class where an error is not reversible:

```
CONNECTED   != AUTHORIZED
AUTHORIZED  != EXECUTED
EXECUTED    != SUCCESSFUL
```

A connected robot provider is not permission to move a robot. Physical execution would require **higher** authorization than software execution, not equal authorization delivered through the same seam.

## Backlog Governance

The six initiatives above remain at **Future Investigation**.

They may be investigated for evidence, strategic value, overlap, risk, dependency, and boundary clarity. They must not be represented as canonical roadmap commitments, approved architecture, products, Agents, workflows, Runtime services, implementation plans, or execution authorization.

## Admitting a New Business Engine

Two of the initiatives above — Creator Business Engine and Growth & Commerce Engine — describe business models rather than platform capabilities. Hebun should be able to absorb further business models over time without becoming a collection of unrelated features, so admission to this register carries a standard.

A new business engine earns a place here only when each of the following can be stated:

1. **The problem** — a real user or business problem, stated without reference to the technology that would solve it.
2. **The authoritative owner** — which existing authority owns the truth it would reason over. If the honest answer is "a new one," that is a reason to stop and justify the new authority separately, not a reason to create one quietly.
3. **The required provider truth** — what a real external provider would have to confirm before any claim is honest.
4. **The capabilities** — bounded, separately grantable, and named for exactly what they deliver.
5. **The economic model** — how value is actually created and what it costs, including the costs that make the model unattractive.
6. **The Governance boundary** — which of its acts are consequential, and what a permit for one would have to bound.
7. **The legitimate production consumer** — the surface that would genuinely call it. A capability nothing can reach is not a capability.
8. **Provider onboarding** — the full released standard, with no stage skipped and none forced green.
9. **Real-provider acceptance** — the seam executed against the real provider, and the response matching the declared boundary.
10. **Closed-loop measurement** — how the engine learns whether it worked, and how it is retired if it did not.

An engine that cannot answer all ten is an idea, and belongs at [Idea](00-capability-lifecycle.md) rather than here. **A feature must earn its place.** The purpose of the standard is to let Hebun grow through new business models while remaining one coherent enterprise AI operating system rather than a feature collection with a shared login.
